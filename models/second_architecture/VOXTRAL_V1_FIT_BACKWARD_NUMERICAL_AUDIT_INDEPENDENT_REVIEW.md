## Evidentiary limits and observed gap

The sidecar's donor and three no-grad terminal vectors are checked for shape, FP32 dtype and finiteness, but they have no individually cross-checked result hashes or recomputed downstream metric. The `clean_source_hashes` and `donor_source_hashes` fields are checked only as 64-character strings, because their full source tensors are not stored in this follow-up sidecar. I confirmed the scope experimentally: in a synthetic completed FP32 artifact, changing donor/no-grad terminal values, replacing those source-hash fields with arbitrary 64-character strings, and updating the top-level sidecar hash still returned `audited_completed_numerical_consistency_only`. This does **not** alter the recomputed paired edits or outcome deltas, but these report-only controls must not be described as independently validated source/terminal content. A stronger future receipt would store the captured tensors or independent per-vector hashes, and bind those hashes before result generation.

The auditor checks that output metadata *reports* the pinned installed-source hashes; it cannot attest what code or model ran from a mutable result directory. The external source-freeze and launcher gate address prospective source selection, while the auditor addresses internal consistency of a completed artifact. Its claim remains one old-FIT case only: no mechanism, held-out transfer, tool behavior, or security conclusion follows from an audit pass.
