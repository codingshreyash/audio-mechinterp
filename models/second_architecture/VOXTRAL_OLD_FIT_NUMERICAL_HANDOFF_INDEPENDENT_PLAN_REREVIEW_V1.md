# Independent rereview of the old-FIT numerical handoff plan

18 November 2026. I rereviewed the amended protocol at commit `87721f4` (SHA-256 `61a67ac87a4e4e17cb0b1f7ad972f25fda4e0d90e0b70798e7144b3fd6f2484b`) against my HOLD review `cd73405`. **Protocol verdict: GO for bounded implementation and independent source review; no model inference is authorized by this document.** The three prospective blockers are closed at the design level.

The LM0 control now has an exact 24-cell population per arithmetic arm: two sites × three cycles × four signed alpha values, with alpha sign determining the one seeded direction. Each cell has an immediately preceding zero forward; one self-patch follows, yielding 49 control forwards per arm. The target norm comes from that exact site's, cycle's, and alpha's native audio edit. The CPU bracket, 24 bisections, closest-visited tie rule, 5% validity criterion, and retained invalid cells remove the earlier freedom to select a convenient amplitude or omit a failed control. The source-edit statement now says only that exact-realized-edit contraction removes ideal-versus-realized substitution; it does not claim to isolate BF16 rounding.

Stage B is counted separately. Cycle 0 supplies two sites × six signed steps × clean/edited = 24 FP32 source-replay forwards, plus six replay parity forwards. Its fixed clean-replay target list needs 36 encoder-site and 32 projector-site scalar VJPs, **68 total**. BF16 and FP32 direct-LM replays each need 24 forwards; FP32 also has four full-model LM-input override validation forwards. The four independent processes have 900/1200/600/1800-second caps, a 4,500-second cumulative cap across failed attempts, 30-GiB observed MPS limits, and 1.5/1.5/1.0/1.0-GiB output ceilings. The preflight must count planned bytes before model load and preserve a failed receipt if any ceiling is hit. These are coherent planned counts, not measured feasibility. The prior FP32 arm peaked at 28.79 billion MPS driver bytes, so Stage A may still stop on memory; neither probes nor conditions may then be dropped.

Two implementation limits should be explicit in its source review and report. First, Stage B replays LM0, LM14 and LM29 but omits the Stage-A LM7 and LM22 probes. It can compare downstream arithmetic at the fixed replay checkpoints; it cannot independently confirm the exact first-mismatch interval if Stage A places it at an omitted checkpoint. Second, the large tensor sidecars are local, content-addressed files rather than committed Git blobs. The committed manifests must bind exact SHA-256, byte counts, attempt IDs and auditor statuses, and any report must say that a checkout without the sidecars cannot reproduce its tensor audit.

The case remains one already opened old-FIT recording, with no new speech population, v2 corpus or locked-test inference. Fixed stage probes, full terminal tensors, zero/self/no-hook parity, exact BF16-source FP32 replay, direct LM-input replay and the non-audio control can give a coarse model-state diagnosis if the future runner passes all gates. Source replay still changes the FP32 clean state and text embeddings, and the LM0 control matches norm rather than input position or direction. Neither establishes a unique BF16 kernel, an audio-specific failure, a reusable Jacobian lens or a security finding. Before any run, require a runner, CPU auditor, independent source reviews, external source freeze, and fail-closed launch receipts for both stages.
