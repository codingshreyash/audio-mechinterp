# Prior art for the VoiceChat raw-function and heard-speech assay

**18 November 2026. Focused primary-source search; no model run.** Scope: 2025–2026 papers and official source on spoken tool selection, dual speech/action outputs, and audio-to-decision interventions. This is a bounded novelty check, not proof that no other study exists.

## Assessment

VoiceChat's **dual-output architecture and spoken tool calling are already published**. NVIDIA reports a streaming speech encoder, shared decoder LM, parallel agent-text and function streams, separate function head, RNN-T transcription branch, and streaming TTS. Its tool benchmark reports 82.5% tool-selection F1, 42.2% argument accuracy, and 33.0% Pass@1. The training setup even delays text targets by two frames while leaving the function channel at its original time, so coincident text and function output should not be assumed. [1] The [pinned NeMo implementation](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/097dfe9e2f55baf653b83035868bdc89849f1b47/nemo/collections/speechlm2/models/duplex_stt_model.py) confirms separate `lm_head` and `function_head` outputs. The proposed study cannot claim discovery of two channels or of ordinary speech tool errors.

| Closest primary work | What it actually measures | Boundary relative to this assay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VoiceChat report and released source** [1] | Architecture, full-duplex behavior, tool selection/arguments/end-to-end benchmark success. Function-channel output is trained alongside agent text; TTS consumes agent text. | Does not report a frame-resolved causal intervention on the *complete raw function JSON* and independently heard speech under the same unconfirmed request. The official tool-call filler can be spoken during a call, so a generic “let me check” utterance alone is not evidence of a model contradiction. |
| **Full-Duplex-Bench v3; Audio2Tool; From Text to Voice** [2–4] | Behavioral spoken tool use under human disfluency, complex task/noise, or paired text/audio. These are strong precedents for lexical, speaker, and acoustic controls. | They score selected calls, arguments or task outcomes across model families; they do not localize a VoiceChat head/state or jointly audit its raw function stream, unspliced heard audio, parser, and replay pass. |
| **DuplexSLA; Heard Enough to Act** [5–6] | Synchronized audio and textual action streams; earliest speech prefix sufficient to select a tool. | DuplexSLA's rate-limited action channel is a different architecture from VoiceChat's parallel specialized text/function heads. “Causal” in the latter paper means online chunk-level decisions from observed speech, not an activation-patching intervention. |
| **Audio refusal direction; prosodic underuse** [7–8] | Activation interventions in audio-language models. Refusal work mainly evaluates a first-token margin and explicitly notes that shifting it need not change the written answer; prosody work tests answer distributions and some final decisions. | Establishes methodology and why full outputs matter; neither tests native function-call JSON plus synthesized speech in a dual-head spoken assistant. |
| **Local or Escalate?** [9] | Frozen hidden-state readouts on MiniCPM-o and, in a transfer experiment, VoiceChat layer states to route *speech QA* to an external expert. | This is the closest VoiceChat internal-state prior art. It demonstrates that its LM states can support a useful readout, but does not patch states or inspect the native function channel/heard speech for an authorization decision. |

## One falsifiable open question

**On independently verified, intelligible English requests to *list* meetings or to *ask before cancelling*, does a narrowly timed intervention at the first VoiceChat function-boundary decision change a complete, parseable native `cancel_calendar_events` call to `list_calendar_events` or no call, while the same-pass independently heard answer remains consistent with listing/asking, and self-donor, off-frame, and norm-matched random controls do not?** Record both raw heads, all function tokens, parser outcome, unspliced audio, and the official two-pass replay separately. A mere logit shift, malformed JSON, canned on-hold line, or pass-1 function compared with pass-2 speech does **not** answer the question. If the unmodified model never proposes an unconfirmed cancel on the preregistered fixture, the specific mismatch premise fails; if the targeted patch does not change a complete parsed action beyond controls, the proposed localization fails. A successful internal edit would establish a model-local causal dependency, **not** an audio-reachable exploit or real calendar deletion. [1, 7, 10]
