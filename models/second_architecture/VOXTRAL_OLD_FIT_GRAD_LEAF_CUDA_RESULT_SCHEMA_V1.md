# CUDA old-FIT gradient-leaf result and sidecar schema V1

18 November 2026. This is a source-only serialization contract for the prospective CUDA V1 protocol and V2 direct/full amendment. It adds no experiment or decision criterion. One case is written to one exclusive attempt directory. The two attempt directories are independent; the campaign manifest records both, including invalid attempts. No checkpoint may be loaded until the runner, auditor, gate, environment, and freeze receive their required reviews.

## Paths and status

The runner writes `meta.json` before model construction, append-only `progress.jsonl`, `model_layout.json` after loading, one content-addressed `blobs/<sha256>.pt` tensor sidecar, and `result.json` only after all planned forwards and serialization succeed. On any worker exception it writes `error.json` with `status`, `error_type`, `error`, `elapsed_seconds`, and `progress_sha256`; a failed or partial sidecar is never a result. The launcher retains exit code, capped stdout and stderr, watchdog/cap decisions, and the no-retry claim separately. All JSON uses finite numbers and sorted keys. Raw tensor comparison means contiguous CPU storage bytes, not `torch.equal` or a numeric tolerance.

`meta.json` has `schema="voxtral_old_fit_grad_leaf_cuda_meta_v1"`, `status="started"`, `case_id`, `case_index` (0 beaver, 1 waterfall), `attempt_id`, `freeze_sha256`, `protocol_v1_sha256`, `protocol_v2_sha256`, `schema_sha256`, `runner_sha256`, `environment_receipt_sha256`, `checkpoint_tree_sha256`, `source_rule`, `batch_sha256` (three tensor hashes), `source_sha256` (two FP32 source hashes; waterfall filled after passive capture in a separate `source_receipt.json`), `full_schedule`, `direct_schedule`, `planned_counts`, `caps`, and UTC/monotonic start times. `source_receipt.json` records the two passive waterfall source hashes at each site, equality flags, and frozen first-pass hashes. Beaver records the archived BF16 and cast FP32 hashes and archive sidecar hash. The worker must not rewrite `meta.json` after model construction.

The `blobs/<sha256>.pt` sidecar is saved with `torch.save` and loads with `weights_only=True`; it contains only primitive values, lists/dicts, and CPU tensors. Top-level keys are exactly `schema`, `case_id`, `case_index`, `batch`, `sources_bf16`, `sources_fp32`, `passive` and `calls`. `schema="voxtral_old_fit_grad_leaf_cuda_tensors_v1"`. `sources_bf16` has the two archived BF16 beaver sources or is empty for waterfall. `sources_fp32` has both frozen FP32 sources. `passive` is empty for beaver; for waterfall it is a two-element list in execution order. Each passive record has `ordinal` (0 or 1), `sources` (encoder31 and projector CPU FP32 tensors), `source_sha256`, and `hook_counts`; these records do not enter the 32 factorial `calls`.

`calls` is a 32-element list in exact execution order: 16 full then 16 direct. Every call includes `kind` (`full`/`direct`), `ordinal` (0–31), `site`, `mode`, `repeat` (0/1), `factor_observed={grad_enabled,leaf_requires_grad}`, `downstream_grad_flags={lm_input:{grad_enabled,requires_grad},lm0_pre:{grad_enabled,requires_grad}}`, `source_sha256` for full or `input_sha256`, `origin_mode`, `origin_full_ordinal` for direct, `lm_input_full` CPU FP32 `[1,396,3072]`, `lm0_input_full` same, `lm_terminal` with exactly `lm0`, `lm7`, `lm14`, `lm22`, `lm29` CPU FP32 vectors `[3072]`, and `first_answer_logits` CPU FP32 `[131072]`. Full calls also contain `source_post_full` (site shape), `projector_full` `[375,3072]`, `audio_placeholder_positions` (375 increasing integers), and `hook_counts` (source override/observer, projector, LM input, LM0 pre and terminal stages). Direct calls contain `hook_counts` (LM0 pre and terminal stages). Tensor flags and hook counts are captured during the call, never inferred by the auditor. `origin_full_ordinal` points to the first full N0 call for that site. There are no GPU tensors or autograd graphs in the sidecar.

`result.json` has `schema="voxtral_old_fit_grad_leaf_cuda_result_v1"`, `status="completed"`, `case_id`, `case_index`, `attempt_id`, `freeze_sha256`, `protocol_v1_sha256`, `protocol_v2_sha256`, `schema_sha256`, `runner_sha256`, `meta_sha256`, `source_receipt_sha256`, `model_layout_sha256`, `progress_sha256`, `details_sha256`, `details_path`, `details_bytes`, `actual_counts`, `elapsed_seconds`, `peak_cuda_allocated_bytes`, `peak_cuda_reserved_bytes`, `peak_process_rss_bytes`, `audio_token_id`, and `execution_metadata` (Torch, CUDA, cuDNN, GPU name/capability, driver, Transformers/Accelerate, deterministic and TF32 controls, requested/model attention setting). The runner does not write scientific pattern labels; the independent CPU auditor does. The auditor matches direct and full by case/site/mode/repeat and enforces the V2 24 byte comparisons per cell.

Before freezing, a CPU-only serialization pilot must build the maximum-shape 32-call sidecar without weights, save it, and show that each case remains under the V1 worker output budget. The live runner enforces its own 42 GiB CUDA allocated/reserved, 110 GiB RSS, 600-second worker and output caps at monitored boundaries; the separate launcher provides an external wall-time and process-tree resource kill. Neither worker polling nor the pilot replaces the external cap.
