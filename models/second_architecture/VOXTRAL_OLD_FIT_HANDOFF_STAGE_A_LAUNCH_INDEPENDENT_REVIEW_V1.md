**GO for an external committed freeze, not for an interpreted model result.** I reviewed the launch gate and protocol changed at `30f24fe`, its final test adjustment at `9cf247b`, and the earlier HOLD at `5d24c28`. This review also compared the launcher with the GO-reviewed Stage-A runner at `5b9c95c`/`6430f8d` and auditor at `0bd048c`/`d6e9e97`.

The earlier output-cap blocker is resolved for successful attempts. The parent drains both child pipes with an 8-MiB per-stream ceiling, polls the *entire* attempt directory while the runner or auditor operates, leaves reserved space for logs and final receipts, and rejects success if the whole directory reaches or exceeds 1.5 GiB. A fast writer could transiently overshoot a polling threshold on a failed attempt; the final size check prevents calling that a successful bounded attempt. The auditor output uses the same bounded path. I found no unbounded `audit_error.txt` write.

FP32 admission now rehashes the committed BF16 start, finish, result and audit receipts, the metadata/layout/progress files, and both content-addressed tensor sidecars; it checks each sidecar's digest and byte count. Git first-add history requires a sole post-review addition of the external freeze and a sole post-freeze addition of the BF16 manifest. The gate enforces one BF16 reservation and attempt before one FP32 reservation and attempt, with no retry, fixed 900/1,200-second arm caps, measured process wall time and a 4,500-second cumulative model-work ceiling. It verifies pinned source bytes before loading and after runner and auditor execution.

I reran all **13 CPU synthetic tests**; they passed. On the actual checkout, `--check-freeze` and `--run --arm bf16` both exited nonzero with `External freeze receipt absent`; the latter created no attempt directory. The final test-only change removes an assertion that would become false after the external freeze is legitimately committed; it does not relax a live gate.

This review authorizes a *separate actor* to commit the exact external freeze and then check it before the first model run. It does not certify the future model execution, tensor derivatives, or an audio-security finding. The BF16 attempt needs its own independent result audit and separately committed manifest before FP32 admission.
