The auditor requires a matching reviewed freeze receipt and exactly **194** JSONL rows: one preflight, all **192** patches in the frozen direction/site/arm order, and one completion, with no failure row. It rejects duplicate JSON keys, truncated records, missing/extra tensor files, symlinks, changed hashes, wrong float32 shapes or nonfinite arrays. For every arm it independently rebuilds the patch from saved baseline states: the random arms use a single concatenated left-then-right PCG64 draw and scatter, separate from the runner's patch function. It checks exact patch equality, natural W/C and realized delta norms, all CTC argmax IDs and complete decoded transcript, frozen strict eligibility and intended-word classification, donor/recipient/other transcript class, logit distances, hook count and resource receipts.

The three replay controls are recomputed from saved logits: `W` must equal the prior word-patch logits, IDs and complete transcript; `self_C` must equal clean recipient logits/IDs; `full_donor` must equal clean donor logits/IDs. The auditor also verifies the completion's full control-failure list and returns `saved_data_verified_controls_hold` if any control fails rather than treating C outcomes as interpretable. It independently reports counts for strict target-only, model-donor, model-recipient and other complete transcripts across eligible and descriptive groups. The resulting report verifies saved evidence and its provenance; it cannot by itself prove that live forwards happened.

The auditor intentionally shares the runner's hash-bound baseline/prior-record loaders, normalized-word helper and pinned processor decoder. Its patch reconstruction and output/control arithmetic are separate. A later independent result reviewer should rehash all new sidecars and decode the saved CTC paths directly from the pinned vocabulary, without calling either evaluator, before making a finding.
