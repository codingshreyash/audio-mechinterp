# Independent preliminary review: Stage-A launch scaffold

**HOLD for the source scaffold at `b2154c2`; no model launch or freeze authorized by this review.** This is not the final pinned launcher review required by the external freeze. I compared the launcher and its nine synthetic tests to the prospective handoff protocol at `87721f4` and the launch-gate protocol committed in `b2154c2`.

The source does enforce the intended order in ordinary operation: external committed freeze and source ancestry before model load; exclusive BF16 reservation; a separately committed BF16 manifest with matching freeze, start, finish, result and audit hashes before FP32 admission; no existing BF16/FP32 attempt directories; 900/1,200-second in-process caps, 120-second child termination grace, separate 120-second auditor timeout, and measured wall accounting against the 4,500-second cumulative cap. It checks the freeze again after the child and after the auditor. `LIVE_SOURCE_PINS=None` deliberately stops both live modes. All nine CPU synthetic tests passed. Real `--check-freeze` and `--run --arm bf16` both exited nonzero with “Live source pins absent”; the latter created no output directory.

**Blocking gap — whole-arm output cap.** `launch_start.json` promises a 1.5-GiB `arm_output_cap_bytes`, but the launcher opens `runner_stdout.txt` and `runner_stderr.txt` without a byte ceiling. On audit failure it also writes unrestricted `audit_error.txt`. The runner and auditor cap only the nested `runner/` directory. Thus a child can leave more than 1.5 GiB under the arm attempt while the nested audit remains valid; stdout/stderr growth is also not stopped during the run. Enforce a whole-attempt cap while the child runs, or use bounded output capture with an explicit failure receipt, and have the final audit verify all launch artifacts against that cap. Add a CPU synthetic test for oversized child output.

**Additional hardening before final review.** `verify_bf16_manifest` checks the committed manifest and four small receipt files, but it neither verifies that the manifest commit follows the freeze commit nor rechecks the BF16 tensor sidecars listed by the result. Require post-freeze ancestry for the manifest and validate sidecar presence/hash/size at FP32 admission, or state precisely that these are deferred to the Stage-B gate and that FP32 admission does not attest ongoing BF16 artifact availability. The external-freeze “first appears after launcher review” check currently proves only absence from the launcher-review tree; a prior add/delete/readd is not ruled out.

The corrected launcher, final review bytes and pins require a new independent rereview. No GPU or model path was exercised here.
