This is a concrete counterexample to the simple assumption that a local speech edit stays local until attention. The result is **stage localization in one pinned English CTC ASR model on synthetic FIT utterances**. It does not establish that the model made an unsafe decision, that a patch changes its answer, or that the effect transfers to a speech-to-speech model. A causal intervention and independent held-out voices or wording are required before those stronger claims.
