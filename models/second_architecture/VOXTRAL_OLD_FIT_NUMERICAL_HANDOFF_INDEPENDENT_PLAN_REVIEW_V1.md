# Independent review of the old-FIT numerical handoff plan

18 November 2026. I reviewed committed protocol `1c74d93478a2aa517599971425b46a9b65df554f`, SHA-256 `f91a4269d134404e3e0581373aa02af49ef13e9583e98c0648a024962ffd3c59`, against the independently audited old-FIT BF16 and FP32 numerical results and the pinned local Voxtral implementation. **Verdict: HOLD before runner implementation or model inference.** The proposed state trace and replay are useful, but three prospective details need to be fixed in the protocol itself so later source code cannot silently choose them after seeing data.

1. **The LM0 non-audio control population is ambiguous.** The prose specifies two sites, four signed alpha values, and a random unit vector plus its negative, but the resource ledger counts 24 edited and 24 paired-zero forwards per arm. That could mean three repeats of eight cells, two signs of 12 cells, or another population. Freeze the exact cell key `(site, alpha, cycle, direction)`, order, repeat count, matching target for the 5% norm rule, bisection tolerance/iteration rule, and whether a failed norm match still gets its paired zero forward. Reconcile the count with that population before using the control to discuss audio specificity. The 1c74d93 correction properly removed an unsupported LM0 Jacobian-accuracy claim.
2. **Stage-B source replay has an uncounted backward workload and no fixed phase clock.** It requires a clean replay-state VJP for the same probes after replacing a full encoder or projector tensor. The plan counts 24 source-replay forwards, but not the VJPs needed at the two replay baselines. Freeze the number and target set for each baseline, whether projector self-derivatives are computed or treated analytically, the order of the replay calls, a Stage-B wall cap per process and cumulative cap, and the output-size bound. Do this before Stage A starts, as the plan itself requires. Keep a missing target or cap failure in the planned denominator.
3. **Tighten the proposed source-rounding inference.** Contracting the exact realized row edit removes the *ideal-versus-realized edit* mismatch from the prediction calculation. A remaining discrepancy, even with ≤10% mean-fidelity and row-nonuniformity ratios, does not by itself isolate downstream arithmetic: the realized edit may still cross a nonlinear or quantized boundary, and the FP32 arm has a different clean state. State the narrower diagnostic implication and reserve causal attribution for the exact-source and LM-input replays, with their stated baseline caveats.

The rest is appropriately bounded. The case, WAVs, source rows, six signed alphas and three cycles are already opened old FIT data; v2 and locked sets stay sealed. The model-path sites are distinguishable, including projector and post-replacement LM input; the pinned source calls the language model with those `inputs_embeds`. Saving full terminal vectors alongside 16 seeded projections can reveal whether the original four raw coordinates were atypical. A no-hook/passive/self/zero equality check is a sound plumbing gate. Exact BF16-source replay in FP32 can test whether the realized edit pattern alone explains the difference **conditional on a changed downstream baseline**. Direct LM-input replay can localize a difference to the language path if within-mode replay reproduces full-model states and logits. A positive LM0 control can show that a similar pattern occurs without an audio-source edit; a negative one cannot establish audio specificity, as the protocol already states.

Feasibility is plausible but unproven. The previous FP32 arm peaked at 28.79 billion MPS driver bytes with four VJPs, leaving little space under the 30-GiB observed-allocation limit. The new 44-VJP graph and passive full-tensor captures need a pre-run byte and memory budget, not an assumption of fit. The protocol is explicit that a resource-limited attempt is retained and that no probe, site or alpha can be dropped after an outcome. After the three fixes, an implementation still needs independent source review, a CPU auditor, a separately committed freeze, and bounded attempt receipts. This plan review is not authorization to run a model.
