I confirmed this with a fake-only adversarial check: after replacing both A fields with arbitrary valid hashes and updating the fake external A/R pins, `verify_pre_pilot()` still returned success. Thus the current contract cannot claim that A's named checkpoint/source hashes equal the actual no-weight fingerprint.
