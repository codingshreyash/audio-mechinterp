The strict primary population is only Piper `plate`↔`cup` in stock mode S and shared-stat mode C: **4/16 directed mode cells per site**. These four are two directions of one lexical pair in one synthetic voice, repeated under two correlated normalization modes. The other 12 per site remain descriptive because at least one clean endpoint was misrecognized. The W patch nonetheless matched the model's own donor transcript in all 32 site-direction outcomes; this includes donor spellings **TEEL**, **TIL**, and **CUB**, so 32/32 must not be described as intended-word success. Each P or Q equal-width donor patch preserved the complete recipient transcript in all 64 site-direction outcomes. Self and full-donor exact-logit controls passed in all 32 outcomes each.

The random controls have an important qualification. None of the 64 equal-donor-W-norm random arms produced the target-only donor transcript, but **11/64 changed the complete recipient transcript** to another spelling: six at feature projection and five at post-block 11. At feature projection, **2/8 primary eligible random arms** changed Piper stock-mode `CUP` to `HUP` (R1 and R2 of the same directed case); **0/8** primary random arms changed the transcript at post-block 11. Nine other changes were in descriptive directions, including `TIL`→`TILL`/`TILE` and `CUB`→`COB`/`CUP`/`COBE`. Thus the random control supports donor specificity for this exact target, but it does **not** establish general transcript stability under arbitrary word-window edits.

The causal conclusion is deliberately narrow: transplanting the paired recording's word-window hidden rows at either tested site was sufficient to make this CTC model emit its own complete donor transcript on the already-open synthetic FIT pairs. Equal-width immediately preceding and following donor patches did not change those transcripts. The GroupNorm baseline and [independent formula reconstruction](WAV2VEC2_FIT_GROUPNORM_RECONSTRUCTION_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md) show that off-word activation differences can already exist before attention; this patch run does not test whether all of those distant states carry useful word information or whether GroupNorm spread contributes to decoding. P/Q magnitudes also need not match W, so their zero transcript effect is not a pure location contrast. There is no attacker-controlled waveform, assistant action, human-voice or multilingual result, independent fresh-word validation, or security-property violation here.

**Next assay recommendation (not an observed result):** prospectively test the *entire active-audio complement* of W at both sites under S and C, retaining full-transcript, self, full-donor, W, equal-norm random-complement and resource controls. That directly asks whether the widespread off-word states are sufficient to transfer the model's donor transcript. The current P/Q controls support W sufficiency versus those two local windows only; the random transcript instability makes a broad claim that all non-W edits are inert untenable. After that mechanism check, use fresh word pairs and independent voices for generalization.
