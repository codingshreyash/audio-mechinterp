## Blocking gap

`verify_continuation_freeze` checks that the freeze matches the current HEAD blob and that its first-add commit follows launcher review, but it never compares the current freeze bytes to the **first-add blob**. An executable synthetic Git-repository regression demonstrated the gap: after the freeze was committed, I changed only `reviewed_utc` and committed it again; `verify_continuation_freeze` accepted the modified freeze (`cf20d7ea...` first-add SHA-256 versus `b31c6f63...` current SHA-256). Thus the external admission receipt is mutable after commitment, contrary to the protocol's immutable-receipt requirement. The gate must pin the first-add blob bytes or hash and reject later revisions, with a negative regression test, before a new source review can grant GO.

`verify_posthoc_receipt` likewise does not compare the posthoc receipt or its independent review with their first-add blobs. After a continuation freeze exists, the gate's review-commit snapshot and freeze hashes reject subsequent edits to those files. Before freeze, later committed edits can be considered by a subsequent review. To make the protocol's immutable-receipt claim literal, first-add equality should also cover the posthoc receipt; the independent review should remain bound to the bytes it actually reviewed. This is a source control issue, not evidence that the preserved BF16 numerical output is wrong.

The gate's `POSTHOC_REVIEW` points to `VOXTRAL_STAGE_A_BF16_POSTHOC_AUDIT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md`, while the separate receipt reviewer is writing `VOXTRAL_STAGE_A_BF16_POSTHOC_AUDIT_RECEIPT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md`. As written, the gate cannot consume that independent review. Align the bound path with the reviewed receipt artifact and rerun the fail-closed tests before source GO.
