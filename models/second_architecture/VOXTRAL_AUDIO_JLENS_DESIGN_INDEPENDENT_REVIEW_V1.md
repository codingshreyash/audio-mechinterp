# Independent review: prospective Voxtral audio Jacobian lens

**Verdict, 18 November 2026: sound research question, not ready for an executable freeze.** I reviewed proposal SHA-256 `4677afb0583ce6403cfd31402b6ff4282de00091b2091fa79c0d119fd5c1ffa9`, the pinned Voxtral configuration and source, the committed Qwen v1 negative reports, the completed Voxtral FIT causal review, and the primary methods below. I did **not** open the ongoing Voxtral development run or generate model outputs. The v2 corpus's technical WAV binding is complete; its independent ASR/listening gate remains pending.

## Checks that pass

- The proposed estimands are meaningfully separate. A FIT-fitted map applied to unpatched source activations is a **source-only readout**; a derivative predicting a specified small edit is **local transport**; an observed full generated suffix after an actual intervention is **causal behavior**. A pass on one cannot substitute for a pass on another. This is consistent with the released [Anthropic Jacobian Lens](https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens), whose central readout maps source residuals to the final-layer basis and vocabulary, and with [J++](https://github.com/safety-research/jpp_lens), whose expert clustering is FIT-time and whose deployed map is fixed.
- The target at Voxtral LM block 29 is the final transformer block, since the pinned local `config.json` has 30 text layers (indices 0–29), width 3,072. Applying the final norm and LM head to its post-block prompt-final state can represent the actual next-token distribution if hook placement and positional indexing are verified. The source sites and the pair-excluded 16-basis/20-map FIT split are coherent; the rank-32 basis does not use a held-out map pair.
- The novelty boundary is appropriately narrow. Existing [audio Jacobian Lens code](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens), [Voxtral/Qwen/DeSTA causal tracing](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13768), [prosodic hidden-state interventions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19211), and [encoder/projector/LM/readout probes](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05871v1) already cover nearby ideas. The proposed contribution would be a **word-disjoint reusable time-indexed audio-to-final-LM map with measured edit prediction and complete-answer controls**, if it passes. It would not be the first audio lens, causal trace, or proof of a security vulnerability.

## Pre-freeze blockers and exact repairs

1. **Validate that the first-token score represents the complete answer.** The proposal requires nonempty, disjoint recipient/foil token sets and ≥60/80 valid natural FIT answers, but it does not require that a generated valid answer's first token belongs to its semantic class or that a classified first token decodes to that complete class. Thus a high preferred-candidate readout score could track a proxy unrelated to complete answers. Before any FIT model output, freeze the exact initial-capital alias/token rule, `logsumexp` class-mass contrast over the full vocabulary, and two entry gates: **≥90% prefix coverage** among complete recipient/foil answers and **≥90% prefix purity** among complete answers whose first token falls in either class. A zero denominator fails. Apply the same percentages independently to development and locked test. Keep incorrect and malformed outputs in all transport denominators. The [draft Qwen v2 protocol](../../eval/audio_jlens/PROSPECTIVE_AUDIO_LENS_V2_DESIGN.md) already specifies this grammar.
2. **Make the derivative input match the BF16 edit actually applied.** The formula contracts the six-bin uniform Jacobian with ideal `αδ_b`, while the patched forward uses `BF16(H_r+αδ_b)−H_r`, which can vary by row. A single summed row-gradient Jacobian is exact to first order only for a uniform row perturbation. For every planned case/bin/α, save the realized rowwise edit, its mean `ē_b`, and both relative discrepancies `||ē_b−αδ_b||₂/max(||αδ_b||₂,10⁻⁶)` and `||E_b−1ē_b||_F/max(||E_b||_F,10⁻⁶)`. Prespecify **≤10%** for each discrepancy; otherwise the cell is invalid and the site's all-planned gate fails. Contract the shared map with `ē_b`, and report an FIT local rowwise-gradient oracle separately to quantify residual nonuniformity. Do not describe the ideal-δ contraction as predicting the exact realized patch.
3. **Pin the pilot's proceed/stop calculation before looking at FIT outputs.** The proposal says a “gradient/local-linear mismatch” stops the pilot but gives no number. Preselect the first FIT map pair/voice/direction, both source sites, the first four rank-32 basis coordinates, and all three declared `α` values. Repeat zero edits and set the effect floor to `max(1e−4, 10×maximum absolute zero-repeat first-token contrast drift)` as in the draft Qwen v2 method. Require nonzero, connected, finite VJPs and, for each site/α, a four-coordinate projected response above the analogous frozen state-drift floor with **relative L2 first-order error ≤0.25** between the local VJP prediction (using realized mean edits) and measured patched-minus-clean state. If a site/α fails, stop this transport protocol rather than changing α, basis coordinates, or tolerance. Treat these numbers as prospective design choices; the implementer may propose a different fixed tolerance **before** the pilot, but not after results.
4. **Specify the required comparator before it gates advancement.** The proposal names equal-rank ridge and a J++-inspired expert map, but the gate only compares fixed Jacobian with zero and ridge, and the expert's cluster count, weight objective, penalty grid, tie rule and training-only folds remain unspecified. Before FIT, choose whether J++-inspired is descriptive or a required advancement comparator. If required, include its MAE ratio/uncertainty in both FIT and development gates; if descriptive, state that a pass does not establish superiority over J++. In either case, bind the exact code, hyperparameters and parameter counts before any FIT model output. A plain-gradient expert average must never be called an exact J++ reproduction: released J++ uses an LRP backward graph and a different readout objective.

## Smaller corrections and interpretation limits

- The basis step has **64 paired difference vectors but 128 endpoint states**: 16 pairs × two voices × two phrasings × two words. The resource pilot and projection should count all 128 captures plus preprocessing, suffix generations, patched forwards, and the 2,560 best-case/5,120 two-graph VJPs. The existing 30 GiB and cumulative six-hour limits are appropriate gates, not measured feasibility.
- Define “projected-terminal L2 MAE” as the mean over planned cells of each cell's projected-coordinate Euclidean norm, and disclose full-state norm and basis energy separately. Otherwise `MAE` could be read as coordinatewise absolute error. Pair-bootstrap intervals on out-of-fold errors quantify variation across these sampled pairs conditional on the fixed basis and training rule; they do not capture model-family or speaker-family uncertainty.
- Nominal 20/80-ms row positions are intervention supports, not acoustic provenance: a bidirectional encoder may place word information in pre/post rows. The proposed α=1 complete-answer controls are useful, but a **time-selectivity** claim needs its own frozen valid-complete-answer and matched-small-edit score rule before those outputs are opened. Preserve the failed Voxtral FIT restoration time gate unchanged.
- A result on two synthetic TTS families and lexical choices would be a task-specific audio-to-LM mechanism. It would not explain noise, accents, generated speech, native action channels, or an attacker-controlled input. The security north star still requires a permissible waveform change and a violated property in the unmodified composed system.

**Disposition:** revise the prospective design and obtain another independent pre-run review; keep the v2 Qwen/Voxtral model outputs sealed until corpus intelligibility QA and the executable runner/auditor freeze. No finding is inferred from this design review.
