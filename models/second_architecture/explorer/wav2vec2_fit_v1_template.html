The highlighted window is the direct receptive-field overlap of the edited PCM slot. It is a fixed nominal support, not a learned CTC alignment. GroupNorm is applied over time in this checkpoint; seeing a difference outside the window does not show that those positions control the final word.

W is the nominal word window; P and Q are equal-width windows before and after it. Random R1/R2 use unique seeded Gaussian edits with the donor W-delta norm. Equal width does not imply equal edit magnitude. “Frames with changed logits” counts a frame when any of its 32 logit values differs, even if its selected CTC token and transcript stay the same. The primary intended-word test needs both clean transcripts to contain the correct word and differ only there; other directions remain visible as descriptive evidence.

Provenance and limits

Source data: baseline records, causal records, saved-data audit, and independent result review. This browser embeds only data whose local source hashes passed the builder.

All data are from one English ASR checkpoint, two synthetic voices, and already-open FIT word pairs. Internal activation patches are not playable audio edits. There is no assistant action or security finding here.
