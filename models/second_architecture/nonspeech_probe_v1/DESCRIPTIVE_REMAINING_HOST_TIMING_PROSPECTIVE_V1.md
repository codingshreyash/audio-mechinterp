# Remaining descriptive models: prospective one-shot host timing plan

19 November 2026. **HOLD for model inference.** This source adds separate one-shot admission paths for the two frozen descriptive arms not covered by the multilingual Whisper V2 host timing parent: `whisper_english` and `wav2vec2` (the exact worker model names). The source uses the same 20 audited non-speech WAVs, the unchanged V1 descriptive worker and saved-record auditor, and the corrected worker-exit host-log fence from `descriptive_parent_v2.py`. It does not inspect multilingual outputs or alter either multilingual attempt.

Each model has a distinct absolute attempt path: `runs/descriptive_whisper_english_20261119_v1` and `runs/descriptive_wav2vec2_20261119_v1`. Each has its own source-only preflight receipt and separately committed independent GO JSON. A GO for one model cannot authorize the other. Both parents also require the existing reviewed V1 worker receipt/GO and recompute its pinned assets, source, and 20-case manifest. The worker produces 20 case rows plus two exact-silence repeat rows per model; its saved-record auditor requires those rows and 18 silence-difference rows. A failed or interrupted attempt remains partial and has no automatic retry.

The parent stamps `worker_exit_observed_utc` immediately after the bounded process has exited and before the after-host probe. It requires the after-host log to cover through that timestamp and reach collection under the pinned terminal-snapshot rule, a collection lag of at most 120 seconds, and no Sleep, DarkWake, Wake, or Thermal event from parent start through collection. AC power, open lid, external charger, recent-transition, 1,800-second wall, sampled 4-GiB RSS, 128-MiB evidence, and process-group kill controls remain. The post-host log is saved before validation. The English-only Whisper and Wav2Vec2 checkpoints cannot test Russian or Hindi script; they provide architecture and nonempty-output controls.

No candidate weights are deserialized by this source handoff, its preflight, or its fake tests. The two source receipts and two GO files are separate; neither model may launch until its own later independent review binds the exact source and receipt bytes.
