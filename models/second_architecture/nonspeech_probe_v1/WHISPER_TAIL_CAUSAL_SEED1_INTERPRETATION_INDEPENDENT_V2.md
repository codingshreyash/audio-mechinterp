The three **predeclared** V1 rescue cells all recur with seed 1: zero-tail donor rows restore the noise-recipient's complete EOS `You` path from its unpatched `I` path at the post-convolution boundary, post-convolution tail interior, and post-block-0 boundary. The source-row donor control has zero measured edit norm at both post-convolution regions, so the equal-norm displaced control carries more weight as a magnitude-matched comparison there.

The replication is **directional**. No tested local zero-recipient → noise-donor transfer induced the noise answer in V1 or V2 (zero of six cells in each). V2's reverse post-convolution boundary cell is not control-qualified because its equal-norm displaced control reached the 128-token cap without EOS; the three V1-unqualified late cells are also unqualified in V2 because displaced controls alter the answer, including two censored loops. These artificial internal edits do not demonstrate an attacker-accessible availability issue. No local final-encoder-norm patch rescued `You`, although complete full-donor swaps worked. The later nulls do not identify a unique circuit: self-attention can distribute information across rows before the final site.

The three seed-1 complete-answer rescues do **not** reproduce the full donor first-step raw-logit vector: maximum absolute differences from the zero-tail donor are about `0.55`, `1.15`, and `0.94`, respectively. The result is a controlled change in the decoded path, not complete donor-state/score restoration.

Seed 1 is a second noise realization applied to the **same source sound**, using the same generator, model, and decoding configuration. V4 already exposed the `You`/`I` response contrast for both seeds. This is a predeclared replication of the *intervention pattern*, not held-out response discovery or independent sound/model generalization. It concerns engineered non-speech hallucinations and has no demonstrated security impact.
