## Interpretation limit

Seed 1 is held out from the V1 *patch intervention*, but its waveform answer difference was already visible in V4. Even a 3/3 result would replicate one local patch pattern across two engineered non-speech noise realizations of the same source recording. It would not establish a general audio mechanism, human-speech behavior, or a security vulnerability. Source-row donor controls can have near-zero actual delta; their measured norms must be reported alongside the equal-norm displaced controls. A changed score or a censored output is not a complete-answer rescue.
