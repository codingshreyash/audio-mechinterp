# Independent design review: Whisper Tiny time translation

19 November 2026. **PASS for the prospective source-only design at `ab71f52`; HOLD target-model inference.** I reviewed the frozen V1 protocol and plan against the V1–V3 Whisper tail interventions, the exact translated WAVs, and the local no-weight processor output. This review does not cover a candidate model runner, saved auditor, resource supervisor, host freeze, or executable GO. I loaded no model weights.

## What the proposed comparison can test

The earlier qualified rescues patched the **union** of two five-row windows around nominal encoder rows 400 and 500; they did not establish that either window alone was sufficient. V1 preserves that union as its primary continuity test and separately measures original isolated-window viability. No isolated result may be promoted after seeing its answer. The amended schedule has 28 possible calls in the original attempt and 62 per offset attempt, 152 maximum across three separate capped attempts. The six new equal-norm relocation arms per offset apply the correct-window donor-minus-recipient delta at the exact competing window. This is a better location control than an unrelated source-row edit alone.

At 16 kHz, 8,000 or 16,000 inserted zero samples shift the waveform by 0.5 or 1 second. With 10-ms mel hops and the encoder's stride-two convolution, the planned shift is 50 or 100 mel frames and 25 or 50 encoder rows. **Prepend** moves the source/tail and tail/end transitions to those later rows; **append** keeps the source/tail transition at its original row while making total active duration equal. The strict crossover rule requires the qualifying direct patch to move with the waveform in prepend and remain fixed in append; opposite direct patches and the relocated equal-norm edits must fail to transfer the donor's complete answer. It also requires the original union result, both same-offset layouts' clean contrasts, language-prefix stability, full-donor positives, identity/displaced controls, and measured mel shift. A failed cell remains in the denominator.

I independently reconstructed all **8/8 PCM WAVs** from the two pinned original files. The [no-weight preprocessing checker](whisper_time_translation_independent_preprocessing_check_v1.py) and [saved receipt](WHISPER_TIME_TRANSLATION_INDEPENDENT_PREPROCESSING_RECEIPT_V1.json), commit `57cd133`, hash the pinned local processor assets and all WAVs. For both zero/noise variants at both offsets, the saved source has bitwise-equal interior mel values after the prescribed prepend shift and at fixed positions for append; maximum absolute difference is **0.0** in each checked window. Prepend and append attention masks match exactly, with 1,050 or 1,100 active mel frames. These interior checks do not establish boundary locality. The candidate's six no-model source/plan tests also pass.

The staged order is now consistent: two original baselines precede original arms, and four same-offset baselines precede that offset's arms. All ten baselines are frozen across the three attempts; within each attempt its baselines precede its arms, and final crossover interpretation awaits both layouts. A no-contrast, changed-prefix or censored pair skips its own patches and remains a reported result; the other layout's descriptive arms cannot become a crossover claim. No waveform, offset, site, or row is chosen from translated outputs.

## Interpretation limits and execution gate

If the union crossover qualifies, it supports **timing-linked causal behavior for this constructed pair**. It cannot identify which of the two boundaries matters. The isolated source/tail claim has its own original-window viability gate and is limited to the post-convolution site. After block 0, self-attention can mix information across rows, so a successful row patch is coordinate-specific rather than proof that acoustic information stayed local. Leading silence and explicit trailing silence also change context; equal-duration and relocation controls reduce this ambiguity without eliminating it. A null opposite-window donor patch with zero or tiny donor delta is weak on its own; the equal-norm relocation null is necessary for the strict claim. Formatting-only token differences should be reported separately from lexical changes. This experiment is one engineered non-speech pair and cannot establish human-perceptual robustness, attacker reachability, or a security violation.

Related work already applies activation patching to ASR [Glazer et al., AAAI 2026](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/41073) and probes temporal focus with activation steering in audio-language models [Lin and Lee, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11400). This V1 design is a narrow time-translation falsification of this project's earlier Whisper observation, with no priority claim over those methods.

**Target inference stays on HOLD** until a different reviewer checks the complete runner, per-attempt host/thermal/RSS/evidence enforcement, exact processor/weight/source freeze, saved auditor, and one-shot admission/GO for each attempt. The planned 62-call translated attempt exceeds the V3 56-call reference but remains under the prospective 1-GiB evidence cap only if the supervisor actually enforces it. A cap failure is a failed attempt, not an omitted control or a reason to choose fewer arms after observing outputs.
