# Independent rereview: Whisper padding-channel V3 design

19 November 2026. **GO as a prospective design; HOLD for executable GO and candidate inference.** I rereviewed the [V3 amendment](WHISPER_PADDING_CHANNEL_PROSPECTIVE_V3.md) at commit `f1a3db2` against the [V2 independent HOLD](WHISPER_PADDING_CHANNEL_INDEPENDENT_DESIGN_REREVIEW_V2.md), the planner and its tests. I ran only the six no-weight planner tests and printed the schedule; no candidate checkpoint was loaded or run.

The prior ordering blocker is fixed. The first four rows are the two silence `no_hook`/`read_only_donor_capture` pairs. For each recipient/site, the frozen order is `RR`, `read_only`, `RD`, `DR`, `DD`, `self`, four random controls, `padding_detect_only`, and `padding_transcribe_only`. `RR` is a true no-hook recipient baseline; `read_only` captures both sites and both encoder phases before any recipient edit. The planner's `validate_capture_dependencies` rejects a moved `RD` before its read-only capture, and the new mutation test exercises that rejection. The later site block must compare its duplicate native captures with the first site's by shape, dtype, and exact content hash at runtime.

The schedule retains all 18 non-silence cases, both duration-matched silence donors, two sites, and all 12 arms per case/site: **436 calls**. The canonical schedule SHA-256 is `d939618689e989a2dfe249cc6d4f0d3b32a532c184991680b77f11b4af6a0ace`. All **6/6 no-weight tests pass**, including the source-pin and resource-budget checks. The conservative core evidence estimate is **1,375,203,488 bytes**; with the frozen 256-MiB allowance and 128-MiB reserve it is **1,777,856,672 bytes**, below the 2-GiB evidence cap. The 1,800-second wall and 4-GiB sampled process-tree RSS ceilings remain specified. These are planning bounds, not observations of a worker run.

V1/V2's claim limits remain essential. The pinned Transformers encoder does not use its supplied audio mask, decoder cross-attention is unmasked, and the log-mel floor uses the waveform's global maximum; nominally padded state is not an information-free control. The primary endpoint is a complete prefix/text/EOS change with all censored and failed arms retained in the ledger. White-box swaps can establish dependence on this pinned model interface only. A waveform-level attacker-control test and a relevant audio-language or speech-to-speech consequence are separate studies.

**This GO does not authorize inference.** Before weights load, freeze and independently review the worker, fake-model hook tests, admission receipt, parent host/resource gate, and saved-result auditor. Those checks must prove phase/site hook counts and axes, immutable donor/recipient captures, exact splice support, no-hook/read-only/self and full-donor parity, complete output/EOS evidence, hard live caps, and AC/open-lid/no-thermal host readiness. A passing planner cannot substitute for an executable GO or a valid run.
