The corrected score rule is necessary: the installed generation processor writes `-inf` to suppressed-token scores. V4 permits `-inf` **only** in binary float32 processed-score vectors, requires at least one finite entry and no `NaN` or `+inf`, and defines parity as identical `-inf` masks plus finite-entry max-absolute difference `≤1e-5`. It keeps raw pre-processor logits and saved encoder/decoder states fully finite. The first-step final decoder state is the full float32 `[1,4,384]` array, with exact shape/dtype and all-finite values; comparing one width-384 row would omit three prefix positions. V4 explicitly supersedes V2's blanket nonfinite-array rejection and width-384 decoder-vector rule. These rules are specified but have not yet been demonstrated by worker/auditor tests.

The worker still must enforce actual bytes and reserve before each forward and write; this arithmetic does not prove runtime compliance.
