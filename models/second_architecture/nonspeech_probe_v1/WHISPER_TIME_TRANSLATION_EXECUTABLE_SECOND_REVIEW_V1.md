## Scope and limits

**Trusted local-owner assumption:** the inherited pipe and on-disk receipts guard accidental or unauthorized ordinary launches. They do not prove provenance against a deliberate process running as the same local user, which can fabricate a launch file and pipe. The saved auditor provides internal consistency, not cryptographic evidence against that actor. Later token-step logits are represented by recorded hashes and selected scores, not independently saved full vectors; the first step is checked against saved full vectors. Source-only tests do not establish whether the model will complete the schedule, whether translated contrasts survive, or whether a timing-linked result is a security finding. A qualified result would remain limited to this engineered non-speech pair and would not uniquely identify a circuit.
