# Prospective test of Whisper's nominal padding channel

19 November 2026. **Source review and no-weight plan only. No candidate inference or new output claim.** This design was written after the engineered-sound descriptive result and the 328-call causal study. The latter's independent result audit is still pending. Do not use its provisional outcomes to select cases or adjust this schedule.

## The question

For a fixed short waveform, does the *nominally padded* part of Whisper's input representation change its **complete generated language prefix, text, or EOS**, independently of the nominally active part? If so, can an ordinary waveform change drive that channel and its output without a white-box transplant? These are two separate questions. A different mel value, encoder activation, or first-token score alone answers neither.

Use the 20 frozen 16-kHz PCM WAVs in `runs/render_20261119_0740Z/manifest.json` (SHA-256 `a37e6e6027611555cd65ceb1a8d9288b79c72460ae1155469eb1f094d7ddee90`): 18 non-silence recipients and exact 2-s/8-s silence donors. All 18 recipients stay in the denominator, including dither and cases whose prior output matched silence. Never select only the four beep cases after seeing the descriptive result. The no-weight [schedule](padding_channel_plan_v1.py) has SHA-256 `b6ae293a9a28ae13338a6ef17fae42ad8c62feae7c63afd73a5c991bf7eeccc1` under canonical sorted-key JSON.

## Source-backed reason for this test

In pinned Transformers 5.20.2, [Whisper feature extraction](../.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/transformers/models/whisper/feature_extraction_whisper.py) applies `np.maximum(log_spec, log_spec.max() - 8.0)` before scaling; the torch implementation applies the analogous global maximum. Thus zero-padded waveform samples need not produce a fixed, waveform-independent log-mel tail. Installed source SHA-256: `dcce2e7820be059e657a9e12a60e8a55cfd37b1a82ee202ca15fc7d0934374cb`. The pinned [encoder source](../.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/transformers/models/whisper/modeling_whisper.py) says its `attention_mask` argument is unused, passes `None` to encoder blocks, and passes `encoder_attention_mask=None` into decoder cross-attention. SHA-256: `bf4f022d37ed523d09a472dae267b86007aaae0cd39d2ce37927de44bfc5b9e6`. A nominal padding row can therefore remain available to the model. This establishes **exposure**, not that those rows affect an answer. The prior Stage-B “displaced” rows begin in this channel and cannot be treated as a clean no-information control.

Processor geometry is `[1,80,3000]`. The processor mask marks 200/800 active mel frames for 2/8 s, leaving 2800/2200 nominal padded frames. Conv strides 1 and 2 yield `[1,1500,384]` before encoder block 0, with 100/400 nominal active rows and 1400/1100 nominal padded rows. At the boundary, convolution mixes nearby mel frames: with two kernel-3 convolutions, encoder row *j* can depend on mel indices `2j-3` through `2j+3`; rows beginning at active limit + 2 are strictly beyond the active mel region by this local receptive-field calculation. The primary factorial uses the complete nominal intervals, including boundary rows. A separately declared sensitivity analysis excludes the two boundary encoder rows and four mel frames around the boundary; it cannot stand in for the primary complete-pad result or pass full-donor parity.

## Two factorial intervention sites

For every recipient *R*, use only its same-duration silence donor *D*. At each site define four 2×2 cells. The first letter supplies the **active** interval; the second supplies the **nominal padding** interval. `RR` is native recipient, `RD` recipient active plus donor pad, `DR` donor active plus recipient pad, and `DD` full donor. The splice covers all channels and complete time intervals, not selected features. Use a cloned tensor and immutable native captures. The recipient and donor processor masks are exactly equal within each duration stratum; fail if not. Carry the same recipient mask through every cell.

1. **Log-mel input:** splice the frozen processor tensors at frame 200 or 800. Intercept the encoder's first convolution input separately for the detection and transcription pass, so both passes receive the declared cell. A plain `RR` call installs no edit. `DD` must be numerically equivalent to feeding the silence donor through the same call, or this site's local comparison is invalid.
2. **Early encoder state:** capture the tensor after the two convolutions and positional embedding but immediately before block 0. Splice at row 100 or 400 in both phase-specific native tensors. This tests the site after local convolution has mixed the boundary and after the global mel floor has affected the input. It does **not** identify an isolated waveform time or a neuron. `DD` must reproduce the silence donor's downstream computation.

Keep detection and transcription state distinct even if their captured bytes happen to match. For every case/site, add `read_only` (capture hook, unchanged tensor), `self` (explicit clone/replacement by the native tensor), two SHA-seeded Gaussian controls on active support, and two on padded support. Normalize each random edit to the L2 norm of the donor-minus-recipient displacement **on the same site, same phase, and same support**; zero donor displacement means a zero control, marked uninformative. Record seed, support, target and realized L2, cosine to the donor displacement, and tensor hashes. The random arms use native recipient outside their stated support. Add `padding_detect_only` and `padding_transcribe_only`: recipient active/donor pad in just the named encoder phase, with native recipient input in the other. These separate a changed automatic language decision from transcription conditioning.

The frozen schedule contains two silence no-hook baselines plus **12 arms × 18 cases × two sites = 434 complete calls**. Every arm is run once, in order, with no output-based retry or promotion. This is a post-descriptive within-checkpoint mechanism study, not held-out discovery. If resources force a smaller execution, write a new prospective staged protocol first and report the omitted arms; do not silently shrink the denominator.

## Required checks and outcomes

Before any candidate forward, source tests must check exact arm census, duration strata, seed uniqueness, and intervention geometry. A separate fake-model hook test must prove one detection and one transcription edit per call, correct tensor axis, no mutation of saved native/donor arrays, and that `RR`, `read_only`, and `self` are no-op paths. Freeze and independently review that executable before admission. At runtime, record both source tensors, actual splice hashes, expected and realized changed regions, dtype/shape, masks, and every exception. Rehash saved tensor sidecars in an independent read-only audit.

Require exact complete prefix-plus-generated-token parity, including EOS and step count, among `RR`, `read_only`, `self`, and the no-hook recipient result across the two sites. Require exact complete sequence parity between `DD` and its same-duration silence donor. For `DD`, save full raw decoder logits for every generated step and require maximum absolute difference at most `1e-5` from the donor's corresponding logits, as well as exact processed-score and first decoder-state comparisons where the prior audit format supports them. For `RR`/`read_only`/`self`, require first-step full raw-logit max absolute difference at most `1e-5`; save complete IDs and first raw vectors for all arms. Log any parity failure by case/site; no local causal interpretation at a failed site. Record missing or length-censored calls explicitly, not as negative results. Keep a prospective evidence-size and host-power gate before running; do not start long candidate inference on the current battery/thermal-unstable host.

The primary outcome is a changed **EOS-complete** language-prefix-plus-text sequence in `RD` versus `RR`, with the donor/recipient difference and controls reported for every case. Report `DR` versus `DD` separately to see whether padding still matters under donor active input. Because outputs are categorical, provide a case-level four-cell table and counts by 2-s/8-s stratum, not an unsupported scalar “padding effect.” Prelabel as *output-informative* only cases whose native `RR` and matched silence donor have different complete outputs; retain all 18 in the overall ledger. In cases with the same native and donor output, a donor match is not an output flip. A score or language-probability change without a complete sequence change is a **representation or score effect**, not a behavioral finding. If only `DD` changes output, active-plus-padding synergy is possible; do not attribute the result to padding alone. Compare random controls before calling a donor-specific result.

The deterministic decoder policy and prefix are held fixed across arms, so this establishes dependence on an edited interface, not that the decoder prior is unimportant. Inspect raw and processed logits because token suppression can change the selected text; the existing [prior-art note](NONSPEECH_MECHANISM_PRIOR_ART_20261119.md) documents this confound. If `padding_detect_only` changes the language token, quantify whether the full output effect survives `padding_transcribe_only`. Forced-language or token-suppression ablations require a separate, frozen study and cannot be retrofitted into this matrix.

## Separate attacker-reachable waveform test

White-box splices are diagnostic; they cannot be sent to a deployed ASR API. Only after the factorial result is audited, freeze a **new** waveform-level plan on the same complete 18-case population. Compare each unmodified PCM with (a) a losslessly appended 0.5-s and 2-s zero tail and (b) the same-length tail carrying seeded low-energy band-limited noise at preregistered PCM-representable levels. The equal-length zero-tail control separates duration and mask-position changes from tail sound; the base comparison shows their combined practical effect. Keep sample rate, encoding, gain, file handling, and generation settings fixed. Measure processor-active and padded mel deltas independently, including maximum, L2, changed-frame count, and the global-floor value. A tail that changes active frames or merely shifts positions does **not** isolate the padding path; use the white-box factorial only as a mediation diagnostic, not proof of waveform-specific causation.

Before claiming a subtle or covert perturbation, verify waveform hashes, peak/RMS and loudness, objective ASR transcript invariance on a separate speech control, and blinded human listening on the actual stimuli. A model transcript alone is not a proxy for human hearing. Do not call a zero-tail duration effect imperceptible by default; duration is a perceptible property. If the low-energy noise quantizes to zero, record it as identical PCM, not a negative model result. New audio families and independent recordings are needed for generalization. A security claim needs an attacker-suppliable waveform, a specified trust boundary and consequence, and replication on a relevant audio-language or speech-to-speech system; Whisper transcription alone is a mechanistic lead.

## Prior-art and novelty limit

Non-speech Whisper hallucinations, audio activation patching and decoder-side interventions are established; see [the source-reviewed literature summary](NONSPEECH_MECHANISM_PRIOR_ART_20261119.md), including [Whisper-AT](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.03183), [Beyond Transcription](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15882), and [AudioSAE](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05027). A positive result here would narrowly explain whether this **pinned implementation's nominal padding pathway** changes complete outputs under controlled swaps. It would not establish a new general audio circuit, robust exploit, or first use of mechanistic interpretability in audio.
