All 18 recipients are retained. Four beep recordings were the only ones whose original complete output differed from their same-duration silence donor, so only those four can test a distinctive donor-output transfer. The other 14 had no baseline output contrast; their donor matches cannot be counted as induced changes. In the four beep cases, the donor transcript was ` you`; the original outputs were ` Fuck` once and ` You` three times. Thus three of the four complete-sequence transfers include capitalization, not a substantive new transcription. This narrow outcome cannot bear a security claim.

The full-support, equal-L2 random controls changed outputs too. Stage B random edits span all 1,500 rows and are not magnitude-matched controls for its shorter active/displaced edits.

The phase split is supported more precisely by saved internal evidence: `donor_detect` made each of the 18 complete 99-language logit maps exactly equal its same-duration silence donor's map, while each map differed from the recipient's read-only map (maximum logit shift 0.459–4.862). An internal detection effect therefore exists even though no final decision changed; the evidence does not support saying detection was unaffected.

## Critical padding limitation

The Stage B `active_both` replacement covers the **entire nominal audio interval**, rows 0–99 for two-second clips and 0–399 for eight-second clips. It does not identify a narrow beep position or circuit. The `displaced_both` interval is nominal padding, but it is **not information-free** in this implementation. Re-running only the pinned processor on the saved WAVs showed that all four beep-vs-silence pairs differed in **every nominal padded mel frame**: 2,800/2,800 for two seconds and 2,200/2,200 for eight seconds. Twelve of all 18 non-silence clips differed in every padded mel frame; the other six differed in one or two. The saved pre-block-0 state differed in every nominal padded encoder row for each informative beep pair: 1,400/1,400 or 1,100/1,100. A mel difference does not by itself prove the decoder used those rows.

The pinned installed `feature_extraction_whisper.py` (SHA-256 `dcce2e7820be059e657a9e12a60e8a55cfd37b1a82ee202ca15fc7d0934374cb`, lines 128–130) clips each log mel spectrogram relative to its **global maximum** before scaling. This is a source-backed way an early sound can change nominal padding values. The pinned `modeling_whisper.py` (SHA-256 `bf4f022d37ed523d09a472dae267b86007aaae0cd39d2ce37927de44bfc5b9e6`, encoder `forward` around lines 604–616) explicitly says its `attention_mask` is unused for input features; the decoder layer is called with `encoder_attention_mask=None` around lines 806–812. These source facts mean nominal padded encoder rows are processed and available to cross-attention in this path. They do **not** show that padded rows caused the observed output changes. A pre-registered 2×2 active/padding complement intervention and attention analysis would be needed to isolate that contribution.

## Claim boundary and next discriminating experiment

The supported result is that, on this frozen small ASR model and engineered corpus, replacing final encoder state during transcription makes four differing beep outputs equal their silence donor's outputs; detection-only replacement changes language scores but not the chosen language or transcript. Replacing the full nominal active pre-block-0 interval also transfers those outputs. Because the cases and donor output were known before the intervention, most transfers are capitalization changes, random perturbations can change output, one displaced informative arm is censored, and nominal padding contains measurable differences, this does not establish a unique acoustic circuit, attacker-reachable exploit, tool-action integrity failure, or transferable audio security property.

The next useful experiment is a frozen, held-out design that separates active and nominal-padding contributions while preserving all four cells, then tests a complete answer or action endpoint on a relevant audio-language or speech-to-speech model. Predeclare a nontrivial behavioral endpoint and independent acoustic families; keep complete sequences, controls, and censored outcomes in the denominator. No further claim should be promoted from this saved run alone.
