# Pre-inference amendment: random support and raw-logit evidence

19 November 2026. **Source-only amendment. No causal candidate forward has run.** This amends the [Whisper non-speech causal protocol](WHISPER_NONSPEECH_CAUSAL_INTERVENTION_PROSPECTIVE_V1.md) after independent design review identified two underspecified controls and an evidence-cap shortfall. The original commits `eeb9bb4` and `a7f00d8` remain in history. This amendment and the revised planner must receive independent source review before an executable is admitted.

1. For **both** Stage A final-encoder and Stage B pre-block-0 `random_1_both` and `random_2_both` arms, use all `[0,1500)` encoder rows and all 384 channels. Detection and transcription receive independent SHA-derived seeds. Normalize each Gaussian edit to the **full tensor** L2 norm of that case/site/phase silence-donor minus recipient state. This is a full-state donor control. It is not an equal-support control for Stage B's active and displaced row patches. If the full donor displacement is zero, use a zero edit and label the arm uninformative.
2. Save complete **float32 binary per-step raw decoder logit vectors** for the two exact silence no-hook donor baselines and all 36 Stage A/B `donor_both` arms. This makes the donor side of the frozen `1e-5` max-absolute parity test inspectable. Continue to save the first-step raw vector for every other arm and selected scores plus complete IDs/EOS for later steps. Every sidecar needs exact shape, dtype, byte hash, call ID, and step index. The saved auditor must reject a missing donor step, extra step, changed dtype/shape, or hash mismatch.
3. Raise the one-shot evidence cap from 1 GiB to **2 GiB**, without changing the 1,800-second wall cap, sampled 4-GiB RSS cap, or 326-call denominator. The no-weight [planner](causal_plan_v1.py) calculates `1,261,037,400` worst-case core float32 bytes: `(38 × 128 + 326) × 51,865 × 4` for raw logits plus `80 × 1,500 × 384 × 4` for native encoder states. Its test adds a 256-MiB metadata allowance and 128-MiB reserve and requires that sum to fit under 2 GiB. The executable must also check actual evidence bytes before every write and forward, retain a 128-MiB reserve, and preserve a named censored-call ledger if any cap is reached. Compression cannot substitute for a verified byte bound.

The revised canonical schedule SHA-256 is `42a73ee56a5bd4382a649b5106bb1aac1e1a717fd5bb04da1a4af62715ba7e78`, replacing `4f32b78ce4542e29eba4bdae99876b0297f7a62715797d4be45c3cab5076c3f0`. Only row metadata changed: the two donor baselines and 36 `donor_both` arms now explicitly request complete raw logits, and 72 random arms state their full-row support and norm target. The fixed 326-call order, 18 non-silence cases, two silence donors, WAV bytes, model pin, and output parity endpoints remain the same. There is still **no authorization to load weights** under this design alone.
