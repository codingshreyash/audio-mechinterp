What changed when Whisper saw a silence donor’s internal state?

This page exposes all 18 non-speech recipients and every saved intervention arm in attempt_002. Stage A edits the final encoder state; Stage B edits the state before encoder block 0. The donor is always a same-duration silence clip. Select a case to inspect saved outputs, token paths, termination, intervention support, and source/donor comparisons.

No security finding. This is a white-box intervention on an engineered non-speech corpus in openai/whisper-tiny, an ASR model. It does not show an attacker-reachable audio exploit, a tool action, or a mechanism that transfers to speech-to-speech models.

Read these limits before the table

  • Only four beep cases had a baseline output different from matched silence. The other 14 donor matches have no baseline contrast and cannot count as induced output changes.
  • Of four informative donor transfers, three changed only You to you. One changed Fuck to you.
  • A Stage A random edit also exactly matched the donor in one of eight informative random arms; one of those eight was length-censored. Random edits can alter output.
  • Stage B active replacement spans the whole nominal sound interval: rows 0–99 (2 s) or 0–399 (8 s). It does not locate a beep or circuit.
  • Nominal padding is not a clean null: all four beep clips differ from silence in every padded mel frame, and in every padded pre-block-0 encoder row. This run does not establish padding’s causal contribution.
  • Five arms hit the 128-token cap without EOS. They remain visible and in every denominator. A censored output is not a negative result.

How to interpret the phases

Detection-only donor replacement made all 18 saved 99-language logit maps exactly equal their silence donor maps, while the chosen language stayed English and the complete transcript stayed unchanged. Its first transcription raw-logit vector stayed equal to the recipient’s no-hook vector. Transcription-only donor replacement made that first raw vector equal the donor’s. A hidden effect can therefore be real without changing the final answer.

All 36 full-donor arms matched the silence donor’s complete token sequence and saved per-step raw logits. Read-only and self-patch controls preserved their baselines. The independent checker found zero parity failures; the run still has five censored arms. The per-arm parity fields under “Inspect saved fields” are comparison flags: a false recipient/donor match is expected when an intervention changes that output, and is not a checker failure.
