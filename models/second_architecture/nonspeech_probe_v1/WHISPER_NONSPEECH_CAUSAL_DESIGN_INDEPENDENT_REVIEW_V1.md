# Independent review: non-speech Whisper causal design

19 November 2026. **GO as a prospective design; HOLD for executable source and candidate inference.** I reviewed the original design and schedule in `eeb9bb4`, the tensor-storage clarification in `a7f00d8`, and the pre-inference control and budget amendment in `cf3d40e`. No candidate checkpoint was loaded for this review. The design follows the independently audited multilingual descriptive result; it is explicitly post-descriptive, not a held-out discovery or a security finding.

## Checks

- The revised canonical schedule hashes to `42a73ee56a5bd4382a649b5106bb1aac1e1a717fd5bb04da1a4af62715ba7e78`. Its exact 326 calls comprise two duration-matched silence donors, 180 Stage A final-encoder calls (10 arms for every 18 non-silence cases), and 144 Stage B pre-block-0 calls (eight arms for every case). All 14 cases whose earlier answer matched silence remain in the denominator. The 72 random arms have 144 distinct phase-specific seeds. Four no-weight planner tests pass.
- The installed Transformers 5.20.2 `generation_whisper.py` SHA-256 is `e1531401f2cc2e389f13a7a55fa22542132164fefe9d431644c9368f3147583a`; `modeling_whisper.py` is `bf4f022d37ed523d09a472dae267b86007aaae0cd39d2ce37927de44bfc5b9e6`, as specified. With automatic language detection and no supplied `encoder_outputs`, `_retrieve_init_tokens` calls `detect_language`, which performs its own model forward. Short-form transcription subsequently calls `super().generate`. The encoder's final `layer_norm` returns the state consumed by the decoder, and the first encoder block receives the post-convolution, post-position tensor. Thus phase-tagged final-output and early-input hooks are source-feasible. The eventual worker must prove the expected one-detection/one-transcription encoder call topology on every arm before interpreting patches; this review is not that proof.
- The amendment defines full `[0,1500)` support and full donor-minus-recipient norm for both random arms at each site and phase. It correctly labels these as controls for full-state donor edits, not magnitude- or support-matched controls for the early active/displaced row patches. The 2- and 8-second active/displaced windows are nominal processor-to-convolution geometry, not isolated acoustic positions after bidirectional mixing.
- Full per-step raw float32 logit vectors are now required for both silence donor baselines and all 36 `donor_both` arms. The planner computes `1,261,037,400` worst-case core tensor bytes at 128 steps, including first-step vectors for all 326 calls and 80 reusable native encoder states. An additional 256-MiB metadata allowance and 128-MiB reserve fit below the amended 2-GiB cap; the executable must still enforce actual bytes and name censored calls on failure. A regularly sampled 4-GiB RSS ceiling cannot exclude a transient between polls.

## Interpretation and remaining gate

The primary endpoint is an exact complete prefix, generated-token, and EOS comparison with the recipient or same-duration silence donor. A language-token choice, raw-logit shift, or activation difference is secondary. Full-donor token parity and the proposed `1e-5` per-step raw-logit bound are required before a local patch at that site is called sufficient; self/read-only parity must also pass. The separate proposed suppression-policy study changes decoding rules and cannot establish acoustic encoder causality.

An independently reviewed worker, parent, saved-result auditor, fake phase/hook and parity tests, fixed 326-call census, asset admission, and later GO receipt are still required before loading weights. The executable review must verify native/donor tensor reuse and hashes, actual realized edits, generated-step counts and raw-logit sidecars, exact censoring, host timing, and resource caps. No causal or security conclusion follows from this design review.
