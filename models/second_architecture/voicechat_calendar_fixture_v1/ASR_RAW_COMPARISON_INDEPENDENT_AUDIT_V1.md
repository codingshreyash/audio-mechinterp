## Recomputed evidence

I revalidated the frozen 12 final WAVs and source freeze, recomputed both raw-log and completion/meta hashes, joined all 24 rows to the fixed case/voice order by opaque ID, independently normalized every script/transcript and computed word-level edit distance, and compared every row and aggregate with `comparison.json`. All matched. Whisper: **8/12 exact normalized scripts, 17 word errors / 148 reference words**. Wav2Vec2: **3/12, 28 / 148**. Both raw logs contain 12 rows with no reference text; the complete receipts bind raw SHA-256 `18a062d27abb29aac18e9b85939f9559c5e86d97f5afe03b9572755e7ab694bb` and `551f31f482b589ab271878b5275abd557eb9e13da0ec05551ad7eb8f10edd70d`. The comparison retains all **24** observations and both raw sources; there was no outcome-based case deletion.

The four Whisper misses are `A_literal` and `B_literal` in both voices. It rendered the spoken date wording “November nineteenth, twenty twenty-six” as **“November 19, 2026”** in all four outputs, creating four lexical errors under the frozen word metric even when the date appears semantically equivalent. Apple `B_literal` has one further spelling difference, “cancelling” versus “canceling” (5 errors rather than 4). Wav2Vec2 produced an exact literal-date `A_literal` transcript in both voices, but its Apple `B_literal` has “NINETEENTHTWENTY TWENTY SIXTH” and other errors that require listening, not automatic semantic credit. Its other misses include `CANSEL`, split or fused “tomorrow,” and a penguins error. Whisper transcribed both C confirmation prompts exactly under the frozen metric; Wav2Vec2 wrote `CANSEL` in both. These are **ASR observations**, not proof that a human hears or VoiceChat processes the intended words. The planned blinded listener must explicitly judge the date, title, C confirmation phrase, and A/B action restrictions for each exact final WAV before model inference.

## Gate conflict and recommended amendment

The prospective [ASR QA protocol](ASR_QA_PROSPECTIVE_V1.md) says exact full-script match and WER are descriptive, with **no automated score threshold**; ASR agreement permits listening QA but cannot substitute for it. The older [VoiceChat H100 preflight](../voicechat_h100_local_preflight.py) requires every `independent_asr` receipt's `observed_transcript` to equal the fixture transcript after normalization and have `verdict="pass"`. That is a stricter, incompatible gate for these frozen raw outputs: a truthful Whisper receipt would fail on four literal-date clips; a truthful Wav2Vec2 receipt would fail on nine clips. Choosing whichever recognizer happens to be exact for each case would hide the prospective two-recognizer denominator and still leave `B_literal` unmatched. Do not rewrite raw transcripts, invent exact ASR receipts, silently drop clips, or call a numeral-format match a spoken-word identity.

Before any VoiceChat fixture inference, make a **versioned, independently reviewed amendment** to the preflight/fixture receipt schema: bind both complete raw ASR logs and the existing comparison hash to each WAV or to one immutable QA manifest; retain each recognizer's verbatim transcript, exact-match flag, and word errors for all 12 clips; treat ASR as a diagnostic rather than a mandatory exact-text pass; require a separately signed or otherwise attributable human listening receipt with explicit per-case wording and authorization checks. Define exclusions for unclear or wrong audio before seeing VoiceChat output and keep excluded cases in the denominator. If a secondary number/date semantic score is desired, specify a deterministic rule prospectively, apply it to all 24 observations, and keep the original lexical scores. Revalidate the amended gate and frozen artifacts before inference. This changes the *eligibility rule*, not the committed raw results.
