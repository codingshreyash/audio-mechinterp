The plan fixes case B/Piper, the pinned checkpoint/helper and fixture input, a full-cohort human QA and external-anchor gate, the official sample, four fresh fixture processes with one seed, adapter-first response discovery, two wrapped stock runs plus a no-TTS-wrapper stock arm, exact discrete comparisons, a fixed `1e-4` unspliced-PCM control, inert dispatch, and raw artifact retention. The adapter-first order is justified: the official helper requires a prewritten `api_response` before its first pass. If stock pass 1 differs from adapter pass 1, the proposed comparator must fail rather than selecting a new response or WAV.

**Blocking ambiguity:** The no-call branch says to use a “frozen harmless placeholder response” but defines neither the exact `api_response` object nor its serialized bytes and does not list it among the external freeze pins. The pinned `run_fc_offline_inference` reads `api_response["response"]`, serializes it with default `json.dumps`, and records `api_response["tool_name"]` even when it makes no call and injects no tensors. A later arbitrary placeholder would make the stock evidence dependent on a choice made after adapter output. Pin one exact object, its default-JSON formatted bytes/hash, and the no-injection branch rule in the prospective plan and executable freeze before any model output. Tests should reject a different placeholder and any call/response injection in a no-call arm.

Implementation must also check that the unchanged official sample actually exits successfully and retain its raw output, not merely verify that its files exist. Each fresh fixture process needs an independently verifiable source/checkpoint/QA/seed receipt; the low-level comparator cannot prove those conditions. These are source-review checks once the plan is amended, not independent claims of a model or security result.
