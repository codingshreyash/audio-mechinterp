# VoiceChat source-only shape budget after the H200 cap

19 November 2026. This is a dimensional check, not a GPU measurement or a leak diagnosis. The pinned VoiceChat `config.json` is 31,984 bytes with SHA-256 `2e0b67b56fefeb4815e436d66ad84f2f94be192b1d543054c810ed239a28cff5`. The pinned [Nano config](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2/blob/6533e8de2c68e4536bf7c411d7a3ce5734111476/config.json) has `vocab_size=131072`; the [STT source assigns its language head from that LLM](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/097dfe9e2f55baf653b83035868bdc89849f1b47/nemo/collections/speechlm2/models/duplex_stt_model.py#L240-L268). The official H200 sample logged a fixed timeline of 859 steps and ended at step 650 when the supervisor sampled 130,540 MiB against its 130,000 MiB cap.

In the [Nemotron no-cache branch](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/097dfe9e2f55baf653b83035868bdc89849f1b47/nemo/collections/speechlm2/models/duplex_stt_model.py#L3979-L4032), the model receives the full prefix through `t`. Its forward creates [text and function logits for the full returned sequence](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/097dfe9e2f55baf653b83035868bdc89849f1b47/nemo/collections/speechlm2/models/duplex_stt_model.py#L483-L510); the function head is a [copy of the language head](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/097dfe9e2f55baf653b83035868bdc89849f1b47/nemo/collections/speechlm2/models/duplex_stt_model.py#L295-L311). With batch size 1 and a 131,072-token vocabulary, two full-prefix logit tensors at prefix length 651 contain 170,655,744 elements. They require about **0.318 GiB at 16 bits** or **0.636 GiB at 32 bits**. At 859 positions the same pair would require about **0.419 GiB at 16 bits** or **0.839 GiB at 32 bits**. These are tensor payload sizes, not observed allocations; intermediate copies, attention workspaces, allocator reservation, TTS state, and non-PyTorch CUDA memory are excluded.

The simple logit payload is much smaller than 130,540 MiB. That rules out an explanation based *only* on the two final logit tensors, but does not identify the dominant allocation. The [independent source audit](VOICECHAT_H200_MEMORY_TRAJECTORY_SOURCE_AUDIT_20261119.md) recommends a prospective short trace of PyTorch allocated and reserved memory, interval peaks, NVML memory, and carried state shapes before another full run.
