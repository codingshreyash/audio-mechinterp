It loads no model and writes no GO record.

Any missing, failed, reordered, or rehashed-wrong call raises an error.

**Decision remains HOLD even for a complete, timing-feasible ledger.** This worker records post-call MPS samples, not a measured within-call high-water mark; it has no hard per-call watchdog. An independent reviewer must inspect the exact saved evidence and issue any later GO against a future fully measured worker and receipt.
