## Interpretation boundary

The result refutes the absolute statement that these outside-state interventions are inert to the measured CTC sequence score. It does not establish that outside rows uniquely encode the donor word, that the observed margin changes are independent replications, or that a user can induce these activation patches with an audio waveform. FIT and development used previously inspected synthetic stimuli; S/C modes, opposite directions, sites and arms are correlated. L2 normalization is descriptive and does not equalize region width or edit geometry. The ineligible development example cannot support a strict intended-word transfer claim. No action, disclosure, or assistant behavior was measured.
