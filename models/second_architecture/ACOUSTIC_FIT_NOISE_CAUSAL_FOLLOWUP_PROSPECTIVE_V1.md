## One site and fixed arms

Intercept the **first Tensor** of `model.wav2vec2.feature_projection` after its forward; its second tuple member remains unchanged. Capture clean and noisy state and logits once each with no hook, and require their full frame-ID paths and complete CTC transcripts to match the audited V2 run. At this site both states have shape `[1,286,768]`, float32 on CPU. The nominal word rows are `W=[105,179)` from the frozen PCM slot and the old receptive-field rule; `C=[0,105)∪[179,286)` is its active complement. This is a nominal alignment, not a phonetic proof. The recipient and donor directions are **clean→noise** and **noise→clean**. For each recipient, run exactly these eight arms, in order: `self_W`, `W_donor`, `C_donor`, `full_donor`, `R1_W`, `R2_W`, `R1_C`, `R2_C` (16 patched forwards plus two no-hook baselines). The self edit must be bitwise zero. Full-donor replacement must match the donor's full logits and frame-ID path exactly, or the site's replay gate fails. Record but do not silently reselect a site if it fails.

Each `R*_W` or `R*_C` arm replaces only its named rows with a Gaussian direction scaled to the natural donor-minus-recipient L2 norm **in the same rows**. Draw with NumPy **2.3.5** `Generator(PCG64(seed)).standard_normal(shape)` in float64, normalize, cast the edit to float32, and require the realized norm within `1e-4` relative of its target. Fix seeds by direction and arm: clean recipient `R1_W=2026111901`, `R2_W=2026111902`, `R1_C=2026111903`, `R2_C=2026111904`; noisy recipient `R1_W=2026111911`, `R2_W=2026111912`, `R1_C=2026111913`, `R2_C=2026111914`. A zero natural norm makes the arm invalid and stops the assay; do not substitute a magnitude. Save exact baseline state and all logits sidecars, source and realized edit hashes/norms, hook count, full greedy frame IDs, and complete decoded transcripts. Preserve a failed or partial attempt.

## Decision and limits

First require exact baseline, self, and full-donor replay parity and one hook call per patch. Then, separately for each direction, ask whether `W_donor` switches the **complete** transcript to the donor transcript while `C_donor` does not; inspect the full frame-ID path too. A word-specific mediation claim for this selected case also requires neither equal-norm `R*_W` arm to make the same complete-transcript switch. Random complement arms test whether a generic complement edit can have a similar effect. A switch in one direction only, a failing random control, a full-donor parity failure, or unchanged complete transcripts is reported as such, not converted into a favorable score. Do not pool two directions as independent observations. The original clean reading `TIL` is wrong relative to the intended word `teal`; report model-donor transcript transfer separately from intended-word correctness.

Run one CPU FP32 process with four Torch threads, a hard parent wall limit of 1,800 seconds from before child start, 4-GiB process RSS and 128-MiB output caps, AC/open-lid checks and pre/post power-log coverage. A sleep, thermal event, timeout, missing arm, failed replay/normalization/hash gate, or incomplete saved-data audit invalidates the attempt; no retry in place. Commit a no-weight preflight, independent source review, and separate machine-readable worker/parent GO receipts before loading the model. This assay is an internal white-box intervention on a post hoc FIT example. A claim about ordinary audio, held-out transfer, or security requires a separate sealed speech corpus, blinded human wording checks, an unmodified assistant and consequential decision endpoint.
