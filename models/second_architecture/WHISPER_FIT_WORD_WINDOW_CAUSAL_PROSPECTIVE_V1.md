# Whisper Tiny FIT word-window causal assay — prospective v1

18 November 2026. **Design only. No model inference or intervention result under this plan exists yet.** This is a small CPU-only ASR case study, distinct from the earlier Whisper audio-Jacobian readout pilot and from the Voxtral/Omni work. It can establish where a controlled acoustic word difference becomes visible at captured Whisper encoder boundaries, and whether those states are sufficient to change an entire transcription. It cannot establish a general audio-language mechanism or a security vulnerability.

## Fixed measurements and interventions

The model processes the full untrimmed WAV through the stock processor. No typed transcript or reference word is given to the model. For each engine, run both directions, `silver` recipient with `teal` donor and the reverse. Capture donor and recipient 80-bin log-mel features, post-convolution-plus-position encoder input, and outputs of encoder blocks 0, 1, 2, and 3. Report donor-minus-recipient L2 norm at every time position, and separately summarize the word window, equal-width prefix control window, and the remaining positions. The **first captured divergence** is a numerical observation, not a claim that the model has identified the word. Whisper's feature normalization and convolution can spread a local waveform difference, and self-attention can spread it further.

Define `W` per engine from the frozen WAV slot in encoder frame coordinates at 50 positions/second: `[floor(start_seconds*50)-5, ceil(end_seconds*50)+5)`, clipped to `[0,1500)`. Thus Piper `W=[90,161)` and Kokoro `W=[101,184)`. The five-frame guard on each side allows up to 0.1 seconds of acoustic/front-end smearing. Define a **fixed, equal-width outside-slot prefix** `P=[10,10+|W|)`: Piper `[10,81)`, Kokoro `[10,93)`. Both are before the waveform word slot. Use the same `W` and `P` at every captured encoder site; do not move a window after seeing an outcome.

At three sites—post-convolution-plus-position input, post-block 1, and post-block 3—replace only recipient rows in `W` with the donor's rows, then let every remaining stock encoder/decoder computation run and save the **complete greedy transcript**. For post-block 3, replacement is at the final encoder state consumed by decoder cross-attention. For each site and direction also run: (a) donor rows in `P`, (b) recipient self-swap in `W`. At the final encoder site, add (c) a full-sequence donor-state swap as a positive implementation control and (d) two seeded equal-norm Gaussian perturbations restricted to `W` as nonspecific-edit controls. Use fixed seeds `20261118` and `20261119`, independent of observed outcomes. Do not alter weights, generation configuration, decoder states, or waveform bytes. Save all 4 baseline plus 36 site×window/self plus 4 full-donor plus 8 random outputs, including failures and censored responses. A run is invalid if the hook fires zero or multiple unexpected times, shapes/finite checks fail, a self-swap changes output, or full-donor control fails to reproduce the donor baseline exactly.

## Outcomes and limits

Primary outcome is a **complete-transcript target replacement**: the recipient target disappears, the donor target appears exactly once, and all other normalized words match the recipient baseline. Also report looser donor-word appearance, full normalized transcript exact match, word error rate, token-level differences, and target-token logit margins without substituting any score for the complete-transcript outcome. Include all four directions and per-site/per-window tables, not just successful examples. A donor-word change with collateral transcript changes is reported separately. The two equal-norm random controls per case are weak specificity checks; do not calculate a significance claim from four correlated directions.

Run cost is bounded to four baseline and at most 48 intervention decodes on CPU, batch size one, four threads, plus eight feature/encoder captures; hard stop at 30 minutes total or 4 GiB resident memory. Record per-run wall time, process RSS, model and input hashes, script commit, generation settings, exact hook location, state tensors or their hashes, and an append-only failure log. Treat 30 minutes as an upper budget rather than a measured runtime estimate. If the host is thermally unstable or the processor pads cause unmanageable runtime, record that and stop without shortening inputs or changing the frozen design.

This experiment tests an **ASR** encoder-decoder, not a speech-to-speech assistant. It is a single lexical contrast in two synthetic voices, previously used as FIT material. A positive result would demonstrate local causal influence on transcription in this checkpoint only; a negative result would not show the word is absent internally. Future generalization needs distinct words, voices and languages, and a separate audio-language model's response/action pathway. Any later security claim requires an attacker-controlled input and concrete end-to-end violated property.
