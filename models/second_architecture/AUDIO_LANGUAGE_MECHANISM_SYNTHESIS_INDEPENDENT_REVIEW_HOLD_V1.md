## Claims checked

- [Qwen FP32 locked-test review](../../eval/audio_jlens/LOCKED_TEST_FP32_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md): 10/16 planned directions per site met the frozen clean word-form rule; all 30/30 eligible small-step signs per site matched; 10/10 eligible full donor patches per site produced the donor word and EOS. The synthesis preserves the planned denominator and does not call the local derivative a reusable map.
- [Qwen Stage A review](../../eval/audio_jlens/corpus_v1/STAGE_A_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) and [FIT heterogeneity audit](../../eval/audio_jlens/corpus_v1/FIT_JACOBIAN_HETEROGENEITY_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md): the corpus map improved point MAE but failed the frozen development map gate at both sites, with encoder sign accuracy below its gate. The local FIT derivative is correctly labeled an in-sample oracle; no Stage B or locked test for that version is implied.
- [Voxtral FIT audit](VOXTRAL_FIT_V2_CAUSAL_FULL_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) and [development transfer review](VOXTRAL_DEV_TRANSFER_INDEPENDENT_RESULT_REVIEW_V1.md): FIT restoration and opposite-word donor controls were 16/16 per site; the development projector random loss control was 56/96 against an 18/96 ceiling, so the cross-site advancement gate failed. Restoration timing preference did not pass. The synthesis does not promote these constructed patches to an input-reachable exploit.
- [Whisper case study and review](WHISPER_TIME_LOCALIZATION_CASE_STUDY_V1.md), [cross-architecture review](AUDIO_TIME_LOCALIZATION_CROSS_ARCH_CASE_STUDY_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md), and [Wav2Vec2 factorial result review](WAV2VEC2_FIT_COMPLEMENT_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md): the earliest **observed** off-word spread and the causal patch outcomes are kept distinct. Wav2Vec2 donor-word rows matched the model's complete donor transcript 16/16 per site, donor outside rows retained the recipient transcript 16/16 per site, and only 4/16 directions per site were eligible for the stricter intended-word endpoint. The synthesis preserves the smaller natural outside-edit magnitude and the two-pair FIT limit.
- [VoiceChat source map](SPEECH_BRANCH_MODEL_MAP_AND_ASSAY.md) and [dual-head source review](VOICECHAT_DUAL_HEAD_ASSAY_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md): `asr_emb` is a perception-to-language handoff candidate; the text and function heads read one LLM `last_hidden_state` at a generation step. The pinned [STT source](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/097dfe9e2f55baf653b83035868bdc89849f1b47/nemo/collections/speechlm2/models/duplex_stt_model.py) supports shared-state dual-head output and the `should_predict` gate. The synthesis correctly does **not** equate those generation steps with acoustic frames. Word-to-`asr_emb` row alignment and the patch hook are proposed tests, not established capabilities. A one-step shared-state transplant is interpretable only after a frozen shared-prefix selection rule, matched prior token histories, and proof that the function token is actually selected.
- The [dual-head protocol](VOICECHAT_DUAL_HEAD_ASSAY_PROTOCOL.md) and [pinned helper](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/097dfe9e2f55baf653b83035868bdc89849f1b47/nemo/collections/speechlm2/inference/utils/offline_voicechat.py) support the synthesis's pass separation: pass 1 has predicted text/function tokens but `decode_audio=False`; pass 2 may replay an injected call-specific response and has its own predicted streams and decoded audio. The returned object does not expose the locally built trimmed `gen_codes` as `tokens_audio`, so a live identity-checked capture is required. An offline proposed call, JSON parsing, and an inert dispatcher decision remain separate observations.

This HOLD concerns the completeness of the **future run gate**, not the audited counts or the feasibility of inspecting these source-level sites. Once the gate sentence is corrected, rereview the new commit before advancing the synthesis.
