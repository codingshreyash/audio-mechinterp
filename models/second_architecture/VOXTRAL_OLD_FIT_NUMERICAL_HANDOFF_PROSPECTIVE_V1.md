# Prospective old-FIT numerical handoff diagnostic

**Design written after inspecting the BF16 and FP32 old-FIT numerical outcomes on 18 November 2026. No new model run is authorized by this document.** This is a posthoc mechanism diagnosis on the already opened `animal_08|piper|fit_chosen` beaver→monkey FIT case. It neither repairs the failed Voxtral development advancement gate nor opens the fresh v2 corpus or a locked test. A runner, CPU auditor, source review, separate external freeze and bounded launch receipt must precede any new inference.

## Why run this

The [reviewed FP32 attempt](VOXTRAL_V1_FIT_BACKWARD_FP32_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) predicts four LM block-29 coordinates substantially better than the earlier BF16 pilot. The separately frozen BF16 v2 attempt saves exact source edits and clean-source-row parity. In its saved tensor sidecar, nonzero source edits can change most of the 3,072 LM29 terminal coordinates while the four selected coordinates sometimes barely move. The old four-coordinate test cannot tell **where** its linear prediction stops working, whether source rounding is sufficient to explain it, or whether those coordinates are a misleading readout. All numerical claims from the BF16 v2 attempt must still be taken from its independent result review; the observations here motivate a future experiment, not an audited new conclusion.

The pinned [Voxtral implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/18329f1065382e54db698e242cf98bd3f3e5a766/src/transformers/models/voxtral/modeling_voxtral.py) sends audio encoder states through a projector, replaces audio-placeholder embeddings, then calls a 30-block language model. Its locally pinned config has 32 audio blocks and 30 language blocks. The proposed readouts are **model state boundaries**, not isolated acoustic moments: the encoder is bidirectional and each nominal word row can contain broader context.

Implement this as two separately reviewed launch stages: **A, native BF16/FP32 stage traces and the non-audio control; B, exact-source and exact-LM-input replay**. Freeze both stages' estimands, source selection, probe seed and stopping rules before stage A's first model call. Stage B may run only if A's hooks, source equality and parity checks pass; it may not use A's target outcomes to choose a new step, probe or layer. This split limits implementation complexity without turning a failed A into an adaptive B.

## Fixed population, arithmetic and source edits

Use only the same two SHA-bound old-FIT WAVs, fixed one-word audio prompt, recipient/donor direction, encoder31 rows 96–107, projector rows 24–26, four old LM29 coordinates 0–3, model revision `3060fe34b35ba5d44202ce9ff3c097642914f8f3`, and installed Transformers source pins from the prior numerical protocols. The donor direction is the original saved BF16 recipient/donor mean-row difference. Keep encoder and projector edits separate. No labels, directions, sites, probes, alpha values, or checks may be changed after looking at a new model output.

Run BF16 and FP32 as fresh serial processes, with the same load policy as the [BF16 amendment](VOXTRAL_V1_FIT_BACKWARD_NUMERICAL_FOLLOWUP_AMENDMENT_V2.md): the sole BF16 FP32-parameter exception is `model.audio_tower.embed_positions.weight`; FP32 requires all named parameters FP32. Save and audit the complete loaded parameter layout. The six signed nonzero steps are the already frozen `±0.00625, ±0.025, ±0.1`, in the same three-cycle forward/reverse/forward order. Each step has an immediately preceding exact-zero patch. There are 18 pairs and 36 patched forwards per site per native arithmetic arm. `BF16(H+αδ)−H` or `FP32(H+αδ)−H` is the **actual** saved source edit; all predictions contract against these exact rowwise edits, never only the ideal `αδ`. Store full clean and patched source tensors in addition to selected rows, and independently require every prepatch source tensor/selected row to equal the clean source bitwise within its arm. Keep the existing 30 GiB observed MPS driver cap, 15-minute BF16 cap, and 20-minute FP32 cap for each native arm, including preflight and load; no fallback, adaptive alpha or retry.

Before interpreting edits, require no-hook, passive-hook, exact self-patch at each site, and exact zero-patch at each site to agree on the complete prompt LM state **and** greedy suffix within each arm: six parity conditions and six capped generations per arm. A broken hook or changed natural suffix is a plumbing outcome and stops that arm's mechanism analysis. Preserve every failed attempt.

## Passive state trace and fixed probes

On every paired forward, detach and save the selected prepatch source rows, full projector output, the entire language-model `inputs_embeds` after audio-placeholder replacement, and prompt-final residual vectors after LM blocks **0, 7, 14, 22 and 29**. Capture the LM input passively with a forward prehook on the language model; keep prepatch and postpatch projector tensors distinct. Archive the **full** prepatch and postpatch encoder/projector source tensors for the first frozen cycle, which is also the replay cycle. The LM input must have exactly 375 audio-placeholder rows under this fixed prompt; check their IDs and that the postpatch projector rows equal their corresponding LM input rows after the specified dtype conversion. Save hook counts, shapes, dtypes and exact tensor hashes. Compare clean, zero, edited and self paths at every boundary. The full-state L2 change, count of changed coordinates, per-coordinate native ULP spacing, and source-edit fidelity are descriptive measurements that do not require a fitted lens; full encoder-source diagnostics apply to the archived first cycle. Estimate and cap serialized tensor bytes prospectively; if exact archiving exceeds the cap, preserve a failed attempt rather than dropping conditions.

For derivative comparisons, create a fixed seed from this protocol's eventual committed SHA-256 and generate 16 orthonormal Rademacher-derived vectors of width 3,072 using CPU float64 QR with a fixed sign convention. Save and hash the vectors **before** loading a model. At each LM stage, use the first four of these probes; at LM29 use all 16 plus the original four raw coordinates. At the projector word-row output and at the corresponding three LM input audio-placeholder rows, use four separately generated fixed probes of width 9,216. On one clean shared graph per arm, compute VJPs for these scalar projections with respect to both source sites, then contract each with its exact rowwise realized edit. The planned maximum is 4 projector + 4 LM-input + 4×4 earlier LM + 16 LM29 + 4 old LM29 = **44 scalar VJPs per arm**; the two source-site gradients come from the same scalar VJP, not two backward passes. The runner must independently check gradient connectivity, finite values, source hashes and exact hook sites. An intermediate target upstream of a source site is marked *not applicable*, never scored as a failed prediction.

For each stage, site, signed alpha and repeat, report measured projected change, rowwise local prediction, absolute L2 error, relative error, cosine if resolved, zero-repeat spread and the fraction of full target coordinates that changed. Define resolution `N=max(3×max paired-zero projected L2 spread, 10⁻⁶)`. A measured four- or sixteen-probe change with norm `≤N` is **unresolved**; do not divide by a near-zero measured norm and call the resulting number an accuracy result. Otherwise use `||prediction−measurement||₂ / ||measurement||₂`. Report the odd and even response components for every ±alpha pair and compare the alpha ladder. A stage's fixed-probe response is called a *local diagnostic pass* only if its measured norm exceeds `N` and its relative error is ≤0.25 on both signs of α=0.1, in both of three repeats. It is called a *diagnostic mismatch* only if resolved error is >0.5 on both signs and all repeats. Intermediate values or unresolved cells remain indeterminate. These thresholds are engineering diagnostics for this one case, not efficacy or security gates. Publish all cells, including invalid ones.

The **first observed mismatch boundary** is only the first stage in this fixed trace whose probes meet the mismatch rule after an applicable preceding stage passed. If no preceding pass exists, or probe outcomes disagree, report an interval or indeterminate result. Sparse probes cannot prove that no other coordinate or path failed earlier.

## Conditional replay to separate explanations

1. **Source rounding check.** Compare ideal-`αδ`, uniform realized-mean and exact rowwise realized-edit predictions at every stage. If the exact realized-edit prediction remains a resolved mismatch when its source mean-fidelity and row-nonuniformity errors are both ≤0.10, source rounding alone is insufficient for this local derivative test. It does not identify which later operation is responsible.
2. **Exact BF16 source replay through FP32 downstream math.** Archive the BF16 arm's *full* clean and edited encoder31 and projector outputs for one frozen cycle of all six signed steps. In a separate FP32 process, override the **entire** output at the selected source site with each archived BF16 tensor cast to FP32, keeping the FP32 model's other inputs fixed. First require FP32 no-edit replay/self parity against its own replay baseline. For each clean/edited BF16 source-tensor pair, measure the FP32 downstream state changes with the same fixed probes and a clean replay-state VJP. If these matched-source FP32 replays are locally predicted while native BF16 is not, the BF16 source rounding pattern alone cannot explain the difference. The FP32 model's baseline and non-audio embeddings still differ, so call this a conditional downstream arithmetic/baseline result, **not** proof that one BF16 kernel caused it.
3. **Exact LM-input replay.** Independently save the BF16 native clean/edited full `inputs_embeds` pair for the same one cycle. Feed those exact tensors first to the BF16 language model and then, cast to FP32, to the FP32 language model, with identical causal masks/positions and no cache. Within-mode direct replay must reproduce the corresponding full-model LM block states and first-answer logits bitwise (or stop this comparison). The FP32 replay then holds the *entire language-model input*, including text embeddings, fixed between its clean and edited calls. A BF16-versus-FP32 difference that first appears after the LM input can be assigned to the downstream language path under these replay conditions; it still does not distinguish finite precision from nonlinear effects of the different arithmetic modes.
4. **Non-audio pathway control.** At LM block 0's prompt-final residual, use one preseeded random unit vector and its negative. For each site and `±0.025, ±0.1`, choose a scalar by a fixed CPU bisection rule so the **realized native-dtype** residual-edit L2 norm matches that site's observed audio-induced LM0 terminal change within 5%; if impossible, retain an invalid-control cell. Inject this constructed non-audio residual edit in separate no-cache forwards, with an immediately preceding exact-zero forward for each edit and one self-patch parity forward, and read the same later LM probes. If this control exhibits the same BF16 staircase, the numerical pattern is not specific to the audio pathway. If it does not, position and direction differences still prevent a strong audio-specific conclusion. This is an internal-state diagnostic, not an attacker input. No local-Jacobian accuracy claim is made for this control without a separately counted LM0 VJP.

The conditional replay phases are fixed secondary analyses. They cannot replace a failed native arm, select a favorable alpha, or reopen a held-out set. Save every replay input tensor and exact source/LM-input hash so a CPU auditor can recompute pair membership and equality.

## Resource and interpretation limits

The complete planned native work is 72 paired patch forwards plus six parity forwards, one donor capture, one clean backward graph, 44 VJPs and six greedy generations **per arithmetic arm**. The stage-A LM0 control adds 24 edited forwards, 24 paired zero forwards and one self-patch forward per arm. Stage-B replay adds 24 source-replay forwards in FP32 and 24 direct-LM forwards in each mode; the implementation must enumerate exact forward counts, projected serialization size and per-phase wall limits before launch. Use separate exclusive output directories and processes for BF16 and FP32; clear the previous graph before replay. If any phase would exceed 30 GiB observed MPS driver allocation or its fixed arm clock, stop, record a resource-limited receipt and do not reduce probes, steps or sites after seeing outcomes. A CPU-only auditor must recompute tensor hashes, selected-row equality, actual edits, projection vectors, VJP contractions, odd/even components, null-resolution decisions, chronology and all reported counts before an independent result review. Full-source equality outside archived first-cycle tensors remains a model-forward provenance assertion rather than independently replayed evidence and must be labeled as such.

This protocol can identify a **coarse model-state interval** in which a local derivative ceases to predict a constructed edit on one old FIT request. It cannot prove an isolated neuron, a unique acoustic time, cross-word transfer, a reusable Jacobian lens, a generated-speech/tool decision, or a security vulnerability. Any claim about audio specificity requires the non-audio control and new word/voice populations under separately frozen prospective gates.
