## Conditions for a later live GO

This commit supplies no reviewed candidate worker, launcher, or independent saved-result auditor. The helper validates statistic shapes and formulas, but it cannot itself enforce donor provenance, hook site/count, exact checkpoint/source pins, no-hook parity, forward ceilings, or a complete results ledger in a future worker. Those must be implemented, frozen, and independently reviewed before loading the checkpoint. The worker must bind each Stage II `G_clean`/`G_noisy` donor to a raw-convolution capture made under its own pair's common outside-slot input statistics, and never take a donor from stock or Stage I. The auditor should recheck all 20 old row identities, all eight pair cells, both natural diagonals and full logits/CTC paths from saved data.

The Stage II numerical rule marks a changed argmax frame inconclusive when its winning margin is at most twice the relevant diagonal logit reproduction error. That is a useful conservative **screen**, not a measured upper bound on error in an off-diagonal crossed-statistic arm. A future report must preserve exact frame paths and margins and describe any near-tie causal outcome as inconclusive; it must not treat the `1e-2` diagonal logit tolerance alone as proof that an off-diagonal transcript flip is numerically robust.

The experiment can identify conditional statistic sufficiency or dependence for these fixed FIT recordings if the future controls pass. It cannot establish unique mediation, fresh-audio transfer, attacker reachability, a tool-action failure, or a security vulnerability.
