19 November 2026. **HOLD for an executable freeze.** I reviewed the committed [V1 design](VOXTRAL_FP32_TRACK_B_ANSWER_LINK_PROSPECTIVE_V1.md) at `8dd0344` against the [Track B corpus plan](WORD_DISJOINT_TIME_STATE_VALIDATION_PLAN_V2.md), the [four-context FP32 result](VOXTRAL_FP32_NONBEAVER_FOUR_CONTEXT_INDEPENDENT_RESULT_REVIEW_V1.md), the [FIT full-answer audit](VOXTRAL_FIT_V2_CAUSAL_FULL_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md), the [development transfer audit](VOXTRAL_DEV_TRANSFER_INDEPENDENT_RESULT_REVIEW_V1.md), and the cited scoring/classification source. This is a design/source review only; I ran no model and inspected no new Voxtral answers. The fixed `I said {word} and then stopped.` script, four preordered Track B pairs, two synthetic voices, both directions, and nominal projector center `160*(8*r+3)` are coherent with the prior corpus and mask rules. The 32/32 independent human-listening result is a required future admission gate; a source review of the QA auditor is not the result receipt.

## Required V2 changes

1. **Align score and answer surfaces.** V1's full-answer classifier accepts any casefolded ASCII one-word surface with EOS, while `S` scores only one initial-capital token sequence per word. Freeze an explicit canonical-sequence coverage gate for answer-link interpretation, or predeclare and score the full accepted alias set (including EOS). A generated class switch outside the scored surface cannot falsify the local derivative of this particular margin. Save exact generated IDs and canonical/alias membership for every arm.
2. **Specify a predicted switch, rather than an observed switch plus a score movement.** A counted small-alpha link should require `S(0)<0`, the frozen linear prediction `S(0)+alpha*J>0`, measured `S(alpha)>0`, and the *same* arm's complete generation to be donor+EOS, under the surface rule above. Record failures of each clause separately. A score crossing without a free-generation switch limits the two-candidate margin as a behavioral surrogate; it does not invalidate the derivative measurement.
3. **Keep the inferential label local.** V1 computes a recipient-specific gradient on every fresh example. It tests a local oracle margin and its relation to complete behavior across fresh words, not a word-disjoint fitted Jacobian or a reusable lens. No pair-excluded pooled map is needed to answer this narrow question, but such a map needs a separate predeclared experiment before a transfer claim. The old FP32 gate validated random LM29-coordinate first-logit derivatives, not this EOS-inclusive margin; retain the fixed ±0.1 schedule and report no boundary crossing as inconclusive for a categorical answer link.
4. **Make parity and cost executable.** Require equality of the two unpatched projector source tensors used by the separate candidate-gradient graphs, and equality with the clean/zero baseline source; save the comparison and fail the numerical cell if it fails. Verify prompt and processor hashes, expected hook count, nonempty in-range row indices, exact zero-hook source and complete-suffix parity, and whole-projector donor exact-ID parity. Bind the Track B source/manifest and 16 WAV hashes before weights load; a future 32/32 audited listening receipt must also be bound. The work ledger should explicitly include 112 complete generations, 32 clean differentiable candidate-score forwards, and **192** no-grad teacher-forced score forwards (six scored arms × two candidate replies × 16 contexts), plus source captures and at most two old-FIT plumbing generations. Preserve every failed planned context and its reason.
5. **Add the host admission that the prior FP32 continuation needed.** Before each GPU process, verify and record external power, open clamshell, and ten minutes without Sleep, DarkWake, or thermal emergency. Retain pre/post power logs and compare signed UTC-minus-monotonic elapsed gaps against the same five-second rule; a failure invalidates that slot and stops the campaign without reusing its attempt ID. Bind these receipts to the process/time/memory/output/disk caps.

The random vector on the **same word rows** is a useful directional-specificity control, especially because the prior development assay's projector random control changed 56/96 complete answers under its loss criterion. It does not establish *time* specificity: V1 has no equal-norm pre/post/padding intervention arms. Even a passing V2 answer-link pilot would remain an internal state-intervention result, not an attacker-reachable waveform or security finding.
