# Independent review: Wav2Vec2 FIT causal patch plan V1 + V2

18 November 2026. **Decision: GO for the corrected prospective plan; HOLD for real-model patch inference until the new runner, saved-data auditor, no-weight preflight, and a bound freeze receipt have independent source review.** This review covers V1 SHA-256 `23a4303d03cf9c6483985555ac2abc1e6768059bb024527dbc3dfa7d170c5dcd` and V2 SHA-256 `d936f8728ae89715982bcf9c9dc06e7efc6a876a6ace894306af4e84aa21c977`. V1's [HOLD review](WAV2VEC2_FIT_CAUSAL_PATCH_PLAN_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md) remains in force for V1 alone. I loaded no model weights and ran no patch.

The V2 amendment resolves the sole V1 source mismatch. The cached checkpoint config has `do_stable_layer_norm=false`, `add_adapter=false`, 12 blocks, 32 CTC labels, and `mask_feature_prob=0`. The installed Transformers 5.12.1 `modeling_wav2vec2.py` has SHA-256 `c404982e3a063a5f68ee85809356c8a5ed29ad24c8441eea1d56346a57b5da0c`. Its standard encoder adds positional convolution and applies the encoder `layer_norm` **before** block 0, then returns the block-11 output without a final encoder norm. The CTC wrapper takes that state through dropout (inactive in `eval()`) and the linear head. With `add_adapter=false`, there is no adapter between those sites. V2 correctly locates the post-block11 hook after the last encoder block and before the CTC head.

At the feature-projection site, the installed model sends the tuple's first tensor through `_mask_hidden_states` and the encoder. Under the frozen `eval()` and `mask_time_indices=None` path, time masking is inactive; feature masking is also inactive. The second feature-projection tensor is returned as `extract_features` and is not fed to the CTC head. V2's first-member intervention and unchanged-second-member rule match this dataflow. Exact full-donor logits at **both** sites remain empirical gates, not presumed results.

I separately reconstructed the two-endpoint eligibility from all 16 saved clean baseline rows (`records.jsonl` SHA-256 `c9865f4ec306e57baf7dbfc7bd05037ec769034d95db70440972904fcab2507c`). Only Piper `plate`↔`cup` qualifies in stock S and shared-stat C. That yields **4 of 16 directed mode cells per site**, or **8 of 32 site-direction observations** for the primary target-only complete-transcript outcome. Both modes mishear Piper/Kokoro `teal` and Kokoro `cup` at one endpoint; the other 12 directions stay in the descriptive output matrix. The previously reviewed W/P/Q windows fit all four pair/voice frame lengths, and the 16×2×7 matrix is 224 patched forwards with 64 distinct random seeds.

The interpretation limits remain correct: S and C are different preprocessing histories, C is a counterfactual, and first-convolution GroupNorm already spreads the word contrast before the proposed patch sites. A passing word-window intervention would show within-checkpoint ASR state sufficiency on already-open synthetic FIT speech. It would not prove an attacker-reachable waveform, a speech-assistant action, or a security violation.
