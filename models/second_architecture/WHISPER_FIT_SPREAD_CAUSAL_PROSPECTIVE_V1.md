# Whisper Tiny FIT spread-versus-use assay — prospective v1

18 November 2026. **Design only; no model pass under this plan has run.** The completed `silver`/`teal` and `plate`/`cup` FIT assays showed that a donor word-window state changes the complete Whisper Tiny transcript, while donor-minus-recipient differences extend to row 0 after encoder block 0. This follow-up asks whether the numerically changed states *outside* the spoken-word window are themselves sufficient to change the word. The question and both pairs were chosen after inspecting those results, so every result here is **posthoc exploratory FIT evidence**, not held-out confirmation or a security finding.

## Frozen material and regions

Use only `color_05` (`silver`/`teal`) and `object_02` (`plate`/`cup`), phrasing `fit_chosen`, in Piper and Kokoro, from `eval/audio_jlens/corpus_v1/rendered/manifest.json` SHA-256 `adf8cd02d24a43ef576bcb993dc54bd2527440a62798e12fc788def3c063971f`. Validate all eight WAV and PCM hashes, 16-kHz mono format, within-pair equal length, and sample-for-sample equality outside each declared word slot before loading weights. Do not inspect corpus v2 or any locked test. Use cached `openai/whisper-tiny.en@87c7102498dcde7456f24cfd30239ca606ed9063`, checkpoint SHA-256 `db59695928ded6043adaef491a53ef4e12da9611184d77c53baa691a60b958ad`, stock full-WAV processor, CPU float32, four Torch threads, one-beam greedy generation, `max_new_tokens=128`, and the processor attention mask. Pin source, tokenizer, processor, generation assets, versions, and runner commit in a no-weight preflight.

For each pair/voice, define encoder rows at 50 positions/second. `W` is the previously frozen spoken-word slot with five guard frames on each side. `P` is the previously frozen equal-width prefix beginning at row 10. `Q` is a **postword equal-width control** beginning ten rows after `W` ends. `N=ceil(WAV_frames/320)` is the active-audio row count; `C=([0,N) \ W)` is its complement. Leave padded rows `[N,1500)` unchanged in every partial patch. The fixed values are:

| Pair | Voice | `W` | `P` | `Q` | `N` |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `color_05` | Piper | `[90,161)` | `[10,81)` | `[171,242)` | 272 |
| `color_05` | Kokoro | `[101,184)` | `[10,93)` | `[194,277)` | 287 |
| `object_02` | Piper | `[90,139)` | `[10,59)` | `[149,198)` | 250 |
| `object_02` | Kokoro | `[101,175)` | `[10,84)` | `[185,259)` | 279 |

All `P` and `Q` regions are equal in width to `W`, disjoint from it, and within active audio. `Q` can contain a different spoken phrase or silence; it is an **intervention-location control**, not a matched semantic control. `C` covers much more audio than `W`, including `P` and `Q`; its size makes a positive result ambiguous without the matched-norm random arms. No region may move after reading an output.

## Fixed interventions and 128-decode matrix

For each of eight contexts (two pairs × two voices × both word directions), save the unedited complete greedy transcript and capture log-mel input, the input to encoder block 0 after convolution/position/dropout, all four post-block outputs, and final `encoder.layer_norm` output. Save state hashes and per-row donor-minus-recipient L2 and maximum absolute differences. Require bitwise donor/recipient equality in `C` at the post-convolution-plus-position capture and a nonzero difference in `W`; otherwise record an invalid input-locality gate. This validates that the outside-state contrast being tested arose *inside* the encoder rather than from an earlier feature difference outside `W`.

Patch at exactly two sites: the output of `encoder.layers[0]` (**first post-attention block**) and the output of final `encoder.layer_norm` (**state consumed by decoder cross-attention**). At each site and context, run seven arms: donor rows in `W`, donor rows in `P`, donor rows in `Q`, donor rows in `C`, recipient self rows in `W`, and two independent random edits restricted to `C`. Keep every row outside the named region equal to the recipient. At the final site, also run one full-sequence donor-state patch as an exact-token positive control. Thus `8` baselines + `8×2×7=112` site arms + `8` full-donor controls = **128 planned complete decodes**. Record every decoded output before a failed gate raises, including censored outputs and controls.

For each random `C` arm, let `D=donor[:,C,:]−recipient[:,C,:]`; draw standard-normal float32 noise of the same shape with a local CPU generator and scale its **whole-region Frobenius norm** to `||D||_F`. Reject zero/nonfinite target or realized norm. Use base seeds `20261118` and `20261119`. Derive an arm seed as `int.from_bytes(SHA256(UTF8("whisper-fit-spread-v1|{pair}|{engine}|{recipient}|{donor}|{site}|{base_seed}"))[:8], "big") & ((1<<63)-1)`. Require all 32 arm seeds and edit hashes to be distinct. Save base seed, derived seed, both norms and the float32 edit-byte SHA-256. These controls probe nonspecific sensitivity at the same edit magnitude; they are not a significance distribution or a location-matched semantic donor.

## Decision and interpretation

Primary descriptive outcome for each donor region is an **uncensored complete-transcript target-only replacement**: exactly one final EOS, recipient word absent, donor word exactly once, and all other normalized words equal to the recipient baseline. Report the full 8-context matrix at each site, plus donor appearance with collateral changes, recipient-text preservation, word error rate, token differences, and random-control collateral. A rank or generation-processed first-token score cannot rescue a failed complete-transcript outcome. Self patches must reproduce recipient token IDs; full final-state donor patches must reproduce donor token IDs. Baselines must each have exactly one intended target, no paired alternative, and exactly one final EOS. Preserve an invalid or incomplete attempt rather than shortening the matrix after an outcome is seen.

A donor `C` patch that changes the target word would show **causal sufficiency of outside-word encoder rows in this patched context**, not a unique circuit or a naturally reachable attack. A failed `C` patch does **not** prove those rows lack word information: readout may depend on their interaction with `W`, on the patch size, or on a different decoder trajectory. A `P` or `Q` switch has the same location-specific interpretation; a random edit that changes or damages output weakens specificity. Report the count for every arm even if a positive control fails. Keep the time trace numerical and the complete-transcript outcome causal, rather than treating widespread activation differences alone as semantic decoding.

Before inference, commit and independently review the runner, fake-model tests, no-weight preflight, and a separate saved-result auditor. Enforce exact hook counts, shape/dtype/finite checks, input/source hashes, no more than 30 minutes, and no more than 4 GiB peak process RSS; run entirely on CPU. After inference, independently rehash all raw and tensor files and recompute the 128-decode matrix and outcomes. This is one English ASR checkpoint, two previously observed lexical pairs and two synthetic voices; it cannot establish cross-model generalization, speech-assistant authorization, multilingual behavior, or a security vulnerability.
