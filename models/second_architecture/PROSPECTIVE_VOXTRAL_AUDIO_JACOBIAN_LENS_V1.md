# Prospective Voxtral audio-to-language Jacobian lens

**Design for independent review, 18 November 2026. No result and not an executable freeze.** This document was written while the separate, frozen Voxtral development state-patch run was in progress. It uses no outcome from that run. No model weights or new audio answers were loaded for this design. A runner, independent auditor, exact source hashes, and a resource pilot must be reviewed and frozen before the first model answer on the fresh v2 corpus.

## Question, evidence, and prior art

Can a map fitted on other spoken words read the model's natural next-answer preference from an audio state, and predict how a precisely specified audio-state edit changes the language-model state on new words? The map must operate from an unpatched audio activation and frozen FIT parameters. A local derivative computed on the evaluated recipient is an oracle diagnostic, not a reusable lens.

The released [Anthropic Jacobian Lens](https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens) averages source-to-final residual Jacobians for text models, then applies the final readout; its [paper](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html) pairs readouts with interventions. [J++](https://github.com/safety-research/jpp_lens) already uses clustered expert Jacobians, learned signed weights, LRP backward rules, and vocabulary filtering. Its cluster assignments are made during fitting and its deployed lens is one fixed map. The existing [Audio Jacobian Lens](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens) includes Whisper readouts, an LFM language-stream slice, a Chatterbox speech-code pilot, and an explorer. A [Voxtral/Qwen/DeSTA causal-tracing study](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13768) already traces audio-text fusion and a final-token bottleneck. Therefore an audio Jacobian, audio explorer, or Voxtral activation patch alone is not a novelty claim. The testable extension here is a **FIT-fitted, time-indexed audio-to-LM map** with word-disjoint readout and causal-edit prediction, measured at two distinct audio boundaries.

Local evidence sets the bar. The frozen Qwen rank-16 transport failed its one-shot development gate. On FIT, local Qwen derivatives predicted their own small edits far better than a shared map, but the basis captured only about 22% of measured terminal-change energy; a FIT-only Gaussian source router was effectively tied with the global map. These are diagnostics, not transferable Voxtral parameters. The independently audited Voxtral FIT state-patch assay restored the complete recipient answer in 16/16 and caused valid lexical loss in 15/16 at each of two audio sites. Its strict **restoration time-preference gate failed**; finite teacher-forced scores cannot relabel it. That assay uses constructed mel blanks and donor states, and no reusable Jacobian. The separate Voxtral development transfer run and its results are outside this design.

## Three boundaries, two distinct estimands

The **source sites** are fixed: (1) audio encoder block 31 post-block/pre-final-norm, nominal tensor `[1,1500,1280]`; (2) projector forward output, nominal `[375,3072]`. Fit them independently. Their effects are nested on one pathway and must not be summed. The **target** is the prompt-final position at LM block 29 post-block/pre-final-norm, width 3,072; apply the pinned LM final norm and head to read its first-answer-token distribution. Passively capture LM blocks 0, 14, and 29 at that position to describe the handoff through language layers, but do not choose a best target layer after seeing data. Confirm the tensor/tuple structure, hook invocation count, dtype, and exact token position against the pinned implementation before fitting. Audio-placeholder embedding values and projector output should agree at the handoff; that equality is a plumbing check, not an independent causal site. The 20-ms encoder and 80-ms projector row clocks are **nominal**: bidirectional attention can move information across them.

Construct six frozen half-open supports per clip and source site: a full-word support made of four equal-duration word quarters, plus a full-word-width pre and post interval. Keep nominal padding as a separate control. Use the same sample-to-mel-to-row rule as the reviewed Voxtral FIT mask builder, recomputed and hash-bound for every fresh v2 WAV. For recipient source state `H_i^s`, let `R_{i,s,b}` be the selected rows, and let `μ_{i,s,b}` be their mean vector. The paired donor supplies `δ_{i,s,b}=μ_{donor,s,b}−μ_{recipient,s,b}`. The **uniform edit** at scale `α` adds the same `αδ_b` to each recipient row in `R_b` and then casts to the frozen state dtype. A full rowwise donor replacement is a separate intervention and never an exact test of this uniform-edit derivative. Store each realized BF16 row edit `e_j=BF16(H_j+αδ_b)−H_j` in FP32 and its norm, including zeroed edits. For every primary bin/case/α, require its realized mean `m_b=n_b⁻¹Σ_j e_j` to satisfy `||m_b−αδ_b||₂ / max(||αδ_b||₂,10⁻⁶) ≤ 0.10`, and its nonuniformity `sqrt(n_b⁻¹Σ_j||e_j−m_b||₂²) / max(||m_b||₂,10⁻⁶) ≤ 0.10`. Zero/nonfinite intended or realized norms fail. These are pre-fit fidelity gates, not posthoc exclusions; keep a failed cell in its planned denominator.

For a FIT-only rank-32 orthonormal basis `U∈R^{3072×32}` and terminal state `z_i`, define

`J_{i,s,b}=∂(Uᵀz_i)/∂q_b`, where `q_b` is a vector added **uniformly to each row** in `R_{i,s,b}`. Thus one VJP's row gradients are **summed**, not averaged, over the support; the primary prediction for the realized edit is `Δẑ_i=U Σ_b J̄_{s,b} m_{i,s,b,α}`. Report the ideal `αδ_b` prediction separately. Compute the target coordinate in FP32 and accumulate saved row gradients in FP32, while recording that the underlying forward/backward model remains BF16; this is not an FP32-model Jacobian. This estimand must use the same rows, dtype conversion, and effective edit as the actual patched forward. No backward pass, local derivative, output logit, target state, expected word, donor label, category, voice label, or pair ID from an evaluated development/test item enters a fitted map. Donor activations define only the test edit and are disclosed as such.

Estimate `U` on the 16 basis pairs using their matched natural terminal-state differences for both voices and both FIT phrasings (64 difference vectors, one orientation per pair/voice/phrasing). SVD is CPU float64, with a fixed sign convention; require 32 finite nondegenerate singular directions (`σ_j/σ_1>1e−6`) or stop. Do not change rank after a failure. Record the fraction of each measured full terminal change captured by `U`; a low fraction limits any state-transport claim even if a first-token score is predicted.

The **paired-edit estimand** is measured `Uᵀ(z_patched−z_clean)` and the first-token class-contrast change. Score the two frozen initial-capital answer prefixes only if their first-token ID sets are nonempty and disjoint under the pinned Voxtral tokenizer; deduplicate alias IDs and stop if the rule fails a pair. Freeze alias construction before answers and verify **prefix coverage** and **prefix purity** against complete answers: among valid one-word+EOS replies classified as either paired word, at least 90% must start with an ID in the corresponding class; among complete replies whose first ID belongs to either class, at least 90% must be the corresponding paired word. A zero denominator fails. Apply both thresholds separately on the 80 FIT map contexts and all 96 development/test directions, with no case removal. The map's `z_clean+Δẑ` can predict a **first-token** contrast after final norm/head. It cannot predict later reply tokens, EOS, or complete-answer likelihood without an unrolled derivative. Save natural and patched complete generated suffix IDs independently.

The **source-only readout** is a separate, stronger test. With FIT-only anchor `z̄_s` and per-bin source means `μ̄_{s,b}`, form `ẑ_i=z̄_s+U Σ_b J̄_{s,b}(μ_{i,s,b}−μ̄_{s,b})` from the unpatched audio state alone. Compare its first-token class contrast/rank with the model's own natural next-token distribution, the correct spoken word, and valid complete answers as three separate endpoints. Anchors and any calibration are fitted inside each pair-excluded FIT fold, then frozen for development. Agreement with the model's forced two-choice preference when its generated answer is unrelated to either word is **score fidelity**, not a semantic word readout. A successful paired-edit predictor without a successful source-only readout is **local transport**, not an Anthropic-style readout lens.

## Feasibility before fitting

The prior BF16 Voxtral generation assay peaked near 10 GiB MPS driver allocation. It says nothing about backward memory. First run a FIT-only resource pilot after corpus intelligibility review: capture **128 endpoint states to form 64 basis differences** and verify rank, then compute 32 projected-target VJPs for one preselected map context with both source hooks on one graph. Compare one shared backward to separate-site backwards. Measure loaded/forward/backward MPS peaks, seconds per VJP, graph retention, and zero/padded edit drift. The numerical edit grid is **α ∈ {0.025, 0.05, 0.1}** for the primary local test; α=0 and α=1 are controls/stress checks, not members of the small-edit fit. As a prespecified local-linearity pilot, use the **first four basis coordinates** at both sites and all three primary α values. For each of these six site/α comparisons, require a finite measured four-coordinate change above `ε=max(10⁻⁴,10×maximum absolute four-coordinate zero-repeat drift)` and `||Δz_local−Δz_measured||₂ / max(||Δz_measured||₂,ε) ≤ 0.25`, where the local derivative contracts the realized BF16 mean edit `m_b`. Also compute the FIT-pilot rowwise-gradient oracle from the saved row gradients and realized per-row BF16 edits, then report its error beside the uniform-mean approximation; this is an in-sample numerical diagnostic, not a reusable lens. A zero denominator, weak effect, or failed comparison stops the pilot; report every cell. Also fail if any gradient is disconnected/nonfinite, terminal/source shapes or passive suffix IDs differ, the 10% realized-edit fidelity rule fails, or MPS driver allocation reaches 30 GiB. Do not tune α using a development result.

Under one shared graph, 80 map contexts × 32 target cotangents means **2,560 VJPs**; two site-specific graphs mean **5,120**. Six bins come from each VJP's already selected row gradients, not six backward passes. In float32, the two site's six-bin derivative arrays together use about **3.2 MiB per context**, about **256 MiB for 80** before metadata; do not save full rowwise gradients by default. These are arithmetic estimates, not a measured runtime or memory peak. Before a full fit, multiply the measured pilot times by the exact planned captures, forwards, VJPs, patched forwards, and generations, including load/preprocessing/checkpoint overhead. Require a **six-hour cumulative local model-work cap** across all attempts, 30 GiB MPS driver cap, immutable attempt and elapsed-time receipts, and fail-closed resume after an unreceipted interruption. If local backward is too large/slow, stop the local protocol and prospectively port the *same frozen population and method* to an available cloud GPU with a new independently reviewed environment receipt; do not omit a site, bin, or context after viewing outputs.

## FIT comparisons, development gate, and claims

The prespecified **primary candidate** is the pair-balanced average of the 80 FIT local Jacobians, separately at each site. The Qwen FIT Gaussian router's failure gives no basis to elevate a new router to primary. Compare on the same rank-32 basis and fit contexts against zero change, fit-only source/target mean, equal-rank ridge trained on the same edited pairs, a six-bin-shuffled derivative map, and a **required J++-inspired fixed expert comparator**. For that comparator, cluster training-only six-bin source means into `K∈{4,8}` groups with deterministic manifest-seeded k-means, average local Jacobians within each group, and fit one **signed scalar weight per expert** by minimizing squared measured first-token `ΔS` error over the three fixed α values plus `ρ||w||₂²`, with `ρ∈{0.01,0.1,1,10}`. Select `(K,ρ)` by inner whole-pair-held-out FIT folds on score MAE; ties within `10⁻¹²` choose fewer clusters, then larger `ρ`. Refit weights on each outer training fold, producing **one fixed operator** for the excluded pair; the query never routes to a cluster at inference. The equal-rank ridge comparator uses the same training edited pairs, target basis, and penalty grid, with its penalty selected by the same inner whole-pair folds. Freeze k-means iterations, initialization, scaling, ridge objective, and tie behavior in code before FIT inference. Record parameter and forward-work counts. A Gaussian source-conditioned map is exploratory, with no route to replacing the primary after viewing results. This comparator adapts J++'s expert-weight idea but does **not** reproduce its LRP graph, readout filter, or published signed-weight training target; do not label it an exact J++ replication. The local recipient derivative is reported only on FIT as an in-sample ceiling.

For 20 pair-excluded FIT folds, hold out **both voices and both directions of the whole pair** from derivative fitting, source/target centering, ridge/expert fitting, all hyperparameter choices, and numerical calibration. The independent 16-pair basis may stay fixed. On the first FIT phrasing there are 80 contexts × three primary α values = **240 all-planned small-edit measurements per site**. Freeze ridge/expert hyperparameter grids, tie rules, first-token alias sets, and all score arithmetic in executable code before the FIT model run. Report projected-terminal L2, full-terminal L2, captured-energy fraction, predicted-versus-measured first-token score movement, sign above a FIT zero-repeat floor, and source-only readout rank/contrast. Add two descriptive source-only baselines before FIT inference: at the 3,072-wide post-projector site, a direct logit lens formed by adding the training-only centered full-word projector-row mean to the training-only terminal anchor and applying the fixed LM final norm/head; at both sites, a ridge regression from the six concatenated natural source-bin means to the model natural first-token paired contrast, with training-only feature centering/scaling and penalty grid {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10} chosen by inner whole-pair-held-out folds. Evaluate each on the same excluded pair, report model-preference and correct-word agreement separately, and make no superiority-to-probes claim unless the fixed lens beats these baselines on the same cases. Use 10,000 pair-level bootstrap resamples seeded from the binding hash. Keep every wrong, malformed, zero-edit, or missing case in its planned denominator; an invalid primary cell fails the gate rather than shrinking it.

**Proposed advancement rule, subject to independent pre-run review:** at **both sites**, the fixed Jacobian map's **mean Euclidean norm of projected-terminal error** and first-token contrast MAE are each ≤0.85 of **zero-change, equal-rank ridge, and the J++-inspired fixed expert comparator** over all 240 measurements, with pair-bootstrap 95% upper bounds below 1.00 for each ratio. Full-terminal L2 is reported separately and not called MAE. At least 192/240 measured contrast changes must exceed the frozen numerical floor and ≥75% of those must have the correct predicted sign. The source-only readout must identify the model's preferred candidate on ≥60/80 FIT contexts and exceed the FIT-only mean-anchor and ridge readouts by at least 8/80 each. For a semantic word-readout claim, separately require ≥60/80 all-planned natural generations to be the exact intended recipient word+EOS, ≥60/80 to be a valid paired class, and source-only first-token predictions to agree with the correct intended word on ≥60/80 all-planned contexts. The 90% prefix-coverage and 90% prefix-purity tests must each have denominators ≥60 and must pass on their original distinct denominators; unrelated third-word answers remain in the all-planned counts. If this semantic gate fails but score fidelity passes, call the result a forced two-choice preference readout only. If only the paired estimand passes, report the local transport finding but **do not** call it a source-only lens or open the source-only locked test under this rule. These thresholds are design choices, not achieved results.

If FIT passes, freeze the map, centering, aliases, code, and complete population before one development run. Development has **96 directions × three α values = 288 planned small-edit measurements per site** with no new VJP. Apply the same three required comparator error ratios, bootstrap upper bounds, sign rule (at least 231/288 above floor), and source-only readout rule scaled to ≥72/96 preferred-candidate matches and ≥10/96 improvement over each source-only comparator; For semantic advancement, require ≥72/96 exact intended recipient+EOS natural answers, ≥72/96 valid paired-class natural answers, ≥72/96 source-only correct-word predictions on all planned cases, and separate ≥72 denominators for both 90% prefix coverage and purity tests. Use the same requirements on locked test without tuning. A one-site result is a partial result, not a two-site pass. Open the fresh locked test only if both sites pass every prespecified development criterion after an independent audit. Repeat the identical analysis on locked test once with no tuning. A favorable projected score with low full-state capture is a **task-score predictor**, not a full-state lens.

Preserve a separate **complete-answer causal endpoint**: at each site/direction, record the actual generated suffix for clean, passive hook, self patch, uniform whole-word α=1, rowwise clean-donor replacement, and equal-effective-BF16-norm pre, post, padding, and random controls. Score each condition's complete answer and EOS with the strict existing answer classifier. Require exact clean/passive/self token parity. Whole-word donor or uniform-edit answer flips are observations from *actual* model interventions, never predictions inferred from `Δẑ`. Pre/post/padding are comparative controls because attention can move word information off the nominal word rows. **This protocol makes no time-selectivity pass claim**; it reports those control outcomes descriptively. Keep the FIT assay's failed restoration time-preference gate visible; a new time-selective label needs a separately frozen valid-complete-answer paired-control gate, not finite scores alone. A later spoken-versus-typed authorization test needs its own policy, attacker-controlled waveform, and consequential action endpoint before a security claim.

## Outcome decision tree

1. **Incomplete corpus intelligibility, broken hooks, failed rank, gradient/local-linear mismatch, or resource cap:** stop before a corpus fit; publish feasibility and exact failed checks.
2. **FIT pair-excluded gate fails:** publish the negative map/readout result; no development or locked-test efficacy inference.
3. **FIT passes, development fails:** publish all 96 development directions and the negative transfer result; locked test stays sealed.
4. **Development passes:** freeze an independently reviewed locked-test runner and auditor, then run once. Passing supports the specified *Voxtral audio-to-LM task readout and small-edit transport*, bounded by the corpus and rank. It does not explain generated speech, tool calls, accents, noise, or an attacker-reachable security violation.

This document is a design. It does not change the frozen Voxtral development patch protocol, its failed FIT restoration time gate, or the Qwen v1 negative result.
