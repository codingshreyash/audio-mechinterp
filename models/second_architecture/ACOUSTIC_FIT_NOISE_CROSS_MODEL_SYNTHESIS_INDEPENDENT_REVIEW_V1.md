# Independent review: matched acoustic-noise synthesis

19 November 2026. **GO for the bounded synthesis in commit `74d09e2`; no new model, causal or security finding.** I compared [the synthesis](ACOUSTIC_FIT_NOISE_CROSS_MODEL_SYNTHESIS_V1.md) with the independently audited [Whisper](ACOUSTIC_FIT_NOISE_WHISPER_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md) and [Wav2Vec2](ACOUSTIC_FIT_NOISE_WAV2VEC2_V2_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_V1.md) results. I also recounted complete transcripts directly from both saved JSONL files, without model inference: each has 20 case rows. Among each model's 12 noisy arms, Whisper has 9 transcripts identical to its own clean arm and 3 formatting or pause changes; Wav2Vec2 has 10 identical and 2 lexical changes. All four full-noise arms per model match clean. The synthesis identifies the two Wav2Vec2 changes correctly: Kokoro/teal `TIL`→`TEAL` and Kokoro/silver `IS`→`ISE` before `SILVER`. It correctly notes both clean teal recordings were misrecognized by Wav2Vec2.

The first-measured-spread comparison is also supported. For word-slot noise, the raw PCM outside the slot is identical to clean. Whisper's saved mel and pre-block outside rows remain unchanged, with the first observed outside change after block 0. Wav2Vec2's stock whole-utterance normalization changes outside-slot `input_values` before the network. The synthesis does not equate hidden coordinates or L2 scales, infer a unique circuit, claim a general robustness ranking, or treat the post hoc `TIL`→`TEAL` correction as a security exploit. Its proposed causal transplant is clearly prospective.

These are four already inspected synthetic English FIT clips, two ASR architectures, one frozen noise recipe and correlated arms. The synthesis should be read as an architecture/preprocessing case study and a guide to the next causal test. No claim extends to fresh speakers, speech assistants, tool actions, multilingual transfer, or attacker-controlled security impact.
