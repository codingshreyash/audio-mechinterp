19 November 2026. **HOLD for any candidate model load.** [`voxtral_track_b_campaign_supervisor_source_v1.py`](voxtral_track_b_campaign_supervisor_source_v1.py) is a separate 16-context skeleton above the reviewed [one-context slice](VOXTRAL_TRACK_B_STUDY_SLICE_INDEPENDENT_SOURCE_REREVIEW_GO_V2.md). The public `run_campaign()` verifies externally pinned post-pilot GO, pilot measurement and call-log, QA, preflight, study-source and supervisor-source bytes, then **always raises before invoking `load_model`**. The current pilot worker cannot yet issue a claim-bearing `complete_feasible` measurement. No live campaign is authorized by this source.

The private callback schedule is fake-test scaffolding. It requires the exact four-pair × two-engine × two-direction order, a single stable claim outside the output directory, and one natural clean capture per recipient during its generation. It reuses the opposite direction's clean state and answer as the donor, then requires the reviewed one-context call labels and counts: seven generations, up to 72 no-grad singleton scores, and up to 12 differentiable singleton scores per context. Every started and completed or failed model call is fsynced to an append-only JSONL. Completed contexts get exclusive JSON records and lossless CPU FP32 gradient `.pt` sidecars, each reloaded and checked against its tensor hash, shape and norm. Failed and missing contexts also get immutable records; the campaign result retains all 16 rows, exact completed/failed/missing totals, model-work and clock receipts, and descriptive V3/V5 advancement-gate clauses. The result status is at most `schedule_complete_resource_unverified` and its gate status is always `source_only_unverified`.

Fake tests exercise a complete 16-context schedule, paired clean-state reuse, sidecar round-trip, no retry across output directories, a failed call plus 15 missing rows, and a synthetic post-pilot GO that still cannot call the loader. They use no checkpoint or candidate WAV. Run `models/second_architecture/.venv/bin/python -m unittest models.second_architecture.test_voxtral_track_b_campaign_supervisor_source_v1 -v`.
