## Limits

The Wav2Vec2 cases are synthetic English FIT material with already known stock outcomes, and the Kokoro/teal case was selected earlier because word noise corrected a clean `TIL` error. The shared-input path and internal GroupNorm swap are white-box counterfactuals. Stage I feature-projection and post-block-0 time traces were saved as hashes and summaries, so my independent script cannot rebuild those later activations without a full model forward. The margin screen bounds diagonal reproduction error, not crossed-arm numerical error; two raw-source frame contrasts and one statistic-only frame contrast are screen-inconclusive even though none changes a transcript. The parent sampled RSS regularly but cannot exclude a transient peak between samples. Nothing here tests human speech, fresh words, a spoken assistant action, attacker reachability, or a security property.

The next useful causal test is a **new prospective** word-disjoint or human-voice acoustic perturbation in an audio-language model with a complete answer/action endpoint. The present result narrows the FIT mechanism: first-GroupNorm global spread is measurable, while the selected corrected transcript follows the word-region raw signal even when first-GroupNorm statistics are held fixed.
