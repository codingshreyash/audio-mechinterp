# Independent review: Voxtral Track B V5 sequential-alias amendment

19 November 2026. **Decision: HOLD for the V5 resource pilot and all Track B model inference.** I reviewed the committed [V5 amendment](VOXTRAL_FP32_TRACK_B_ANSWER_LINK_PROSPECTIVE_V5_SEQUENTIAL_ALIAS_AMENDMENT.md) at `5664aad` against the independently approved V3+V4 prospective design, its answer-link criteria, call budgets and host gates. I ran no model, opened no Track B listening answers or model outputs, and did not modify V5.

## What checks out

- Singleton teacher forcing with prompt plus `a[:-1]` and `logits_to_keep=L_a`, where `L_a` includes EOS, scores exactly the next `L_a` tokens when the pinned row semantics hold. With alias batch size one, the hook shape becomes `[T,3072]`; the source-state and one-call checks in V5 are appropriate. The class estimand remains `L_c=logsumexp_a q_a`, `S=L_donor-L_recipient`. Its gradient is correctly `G_c=Σ_a exp(q_a-L_c) ∂q_a/∂H`, hence `grad_S=G_donor-G_recipient`. Sequentially releasing each alias graph can compute this without a six-alias backward graph, provided exact saved `q_a`/gradient and parity checks pass.
- The maximum undeduplicated Track B core ledger is **112 generations + 1,152 no-grad singleton score forwards + 192 singleton forward/backward calls = 1,456** projector calls. The fixed old-FIT pilot's 72 no-grad and 12 backward score calls equal the maximum per-context score work; optional separate captures and pilot generations are correctly outside the core figure. The 240-second context and 3,840-second campaign ceilings, 30-GiB MPS cap, 2-GiB output cap, 12-GiB free disk, 20% timing contingency, no-retry rule and host admission remain hard gates. V5 also correctly requires a complete independently audited **32/32 human-listening GO before any model weight load under this version**, including the pilot.

## Blockers to a conservative admission decision

1. **Natural pilot generations do not bound 32-token generations.** V5 uses the slowest observed time of two old-FIT clean/zero *natural* generations to project seven Track B generation calls per context, but either old-FIT output may stop after one or a few tokens. Track B permits `max_new_tokens=32`, and a 20% contingency cannot turn a short observed decode into a conservative 32-token time bound. The old-FIT input/prompt geometry may also be shorter than the longest Track B prompt/audio geometry. Before a pilot can support the fixed 240/3,840-second gates, prospectively define a timing call at validated maximum Track B input/placeholder and answer length, or a prespecified conservative prefill-plus-32-token extrapolation, and include those calls in the pilot ledger and both cumulative and per-context projections. The same maximum geometry or a justified bound must cover no-grad and backward score timing; choosing the longest alias sequence alone covers answer-token length, not audio/prompt length.
2. **The post-pilot freeze boundary is underspecified.** V5 says a frozen auditor projects the workload before the first Track B weight-dependent input, but does not explicitly require an immutable post-pilot resource/admission receipt, its independent GO, and a hash binding to the exact pilot output, source/geometry, timing classes, host log, 20% arithmetic, QA receipt, and reviewed runner/auditor. The pre-pilot no-weight preflight cannot itself bind observed pilot measurements. Fix this prospectively so an invalid, interrupted or over-budget pilot remains a recorded HOLD and cannot be silently reinterpreted or retried before Track B.

The HOLD concerns feasibility and inference admission, not the sequential logsumexp mathematics. The full-answer and signed local-score decision rules, primary 16-context denominator, and limitations of a per-recipient internal state intervention remain unchanged. An amendment made before any weight load can address these two blockers; it needs its own independent review. Neither V5 nor this review authorizes a security or held-out result.
