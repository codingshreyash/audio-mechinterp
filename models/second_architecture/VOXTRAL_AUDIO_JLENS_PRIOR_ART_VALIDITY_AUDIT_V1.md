# Independent novelty and validity audit: prospective Voxtral audio Jacobian lens

**18 November 2026. Pre-executable review only.** I read [the prospective design](PROSPECTIVE_VOXTRAL_AUDIO_JACOBIAN_LENS_V1.md) and the current status ledger. I did not inspect the active Voxtral development run or its outputs, and did not load a model or edit the proposal. This review adds a source-backed novelty check and one semantic-entry issue to the earlier design review.

## What the proposed result could establish

The proposed rectangular, six-bin derivative from post-encoder or post-projector audio states to the prompt-final LM state is a coherent *task-specific* estimand. A map fitted only on other words can be evaluated in two separate ways: (1) an affine source-only readout of a natural first-token preference and (2) a predicted response to a specified small, uniform state edit. An actual regenerated answer after a patch is a third measurement. The design correctly separates these and does not infer a complete answer from a first-token derivative. A positive result on fresh words would be useful even if the map ultimately fails; the Qwen v1 negative makes the transfer test especially important.

This is **not** the first audio Jacobian lens, time-aligned audio readout, audio causal trace, or audio-to-LM probe. [Audio Jacobian Lens](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/blob/main/docs/METHODOLOGY.md) already defines both global and alignment-window Whisper encoder-to-decoder Jacobians, with an affine cross-stream anchor; it also has a [speech-to-speech language-stream prototype](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/blob/main/docs/MLX_LFM.md) and a [Chatterbox speech-code pilot](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/blob/main/docs/CHATTERBOX.md). The [Anthropic method](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html) establishes corpus-averaged residual transport and causal validation for text. [J++](https://github.com/safety-research/jpp_lens) improves a fitted fixed text lens with FIT-time expert clustering, an LRP backward graph, and filtered readout; its inference map is *one fixed linear operator*, not input-routed. [Audio-text fusion tracing](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13768) already studies audio state patches and final-token bottlenecks. [Prosodic underuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19211) already patches Qwen2.5-Omni and other audio models, but its causal endpoints are answer-token log-odds and class decisions by their sign, rather than complete free-generated answers. [Acoustic information loss](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05871v1) already probes encoder, projector, LM, and head stages and tests a choice-letter head update. The narrow possible contribution is **a word-disjoint, fixed six-bin audio-to-final-LM transport tested for both source-only natural readout and measured small-edit response, with independently scored complete-answer interventions at two audio boundaries**. It is not an account of generated speech, tools, arbitrary acoustic conditions, or security behavior.

## Pre-executable blocker: semantic entry can pass with wrong words

The proposed natural-answer gate requires at least 60/80 (development 72/96) *valid one-word+EOS* replies, but it does not require those words to be either the expected recipient or the paired foil. Prefix coverage and purity can therefore pass on a tiny subset while most valid answers are unrelated. The source-only gate then rewards agreement with the model's preferred *paired candidate*, even when the model naturally answers a third word. Such a pass would establish prediction of a forced two-choice score, **not** semantic recovery of the spoken word. This matters because [prosodic underuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19211) and [acoustic information loss](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05871v1) both document gaps between decodable internal information and actual answers; a score can move while the generated behavior does not.

Before the first fresh-corpus Voxtral answer, freeze separate counts and gates for (a) exact expected recipient+EOS generation among **all planned** natural cases, (b) valid paired-class generation, and (c) prefix coverage/purity **with minimum denominators**. A straightforward conservative choice is exact expected generation ≥60/80 FIT and ≥72/96 development/locked test, and to require the same lower bounds on the denominator of each prefix test; the exact numbers are design choices to decide prospectively. Keep model-preference fidelity as a separate endpoint and require source-only semantic agreement with the actual correct class on all planned cases before using “word readout” language. If the model cannot meet this entry gate, the experiment can still report first-token transport against its own distribution, but its semantic lens claim should stop. Do not remove wrong answers or change aliases after seeing them.

## Missing comparators and numerical checks

- At the **post-projector** site, width 3,072 matches the LM width. Add a no-transport direct logit-lens baseline using the same frozen source pooling, anchor/normalization convention, and first-token ranking. It is a deliberately naive baseline, but without it the incremental value of a Jacobian transport over a simple readout is unknown. At both sites, distinguish the proposed ridge fit on *edit differences* from a source-only supervised linear readout trained on **natural source states**; the latter is a useful descriptive accuracy comparator, not a causal Jacobian. If comparison to probes is not intended, explicitly limit any claim of superiority to the listed baselines. The existing [Audio Jacobian Lens methodology](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/blob/main/docs/METHODOLOGY.md) likewise warns that affine anchors can dominate absolute cross-stream readouts.
- The fixed J++-inspired comparator is appropriately labeled an **adaptation**. Its clusters are FIT-only and the deployed operator must remain fixed. Do not call it a J++ reproduction or interpret a win as beating released J++, since the [released code](https://github.com/safety-research/jpp_lens) uses LRP and a different readout/weight objective. The proposal already says this; preserve it in all reports.
- The six-bin Jacobian is the derivative for **uniform row edits**. Contracting it with the realized BF16 *mean* is an approximation when quantization makes row edits unequal. The proposed 10% fidelity and nonuniformity tests, and four-coordinate local-linearity pilot, are necessary gates. The final result should include a rowwise-gradient oracle on FIT for at least the preselected pilot, so a failed averaged prediction can be separated from quantization/nonuniformity error. The rank-32 target basis is fitted from 64 FIT differences; report captured **full-state** energy and never call low-dimensional projected agreement a full-state explanation.

## Verdict

**Hold executable freeze until the semantic-entry loophole is closed in the protocol and independently reviewed.** The direct projector logit-lens and natural-state supervised readout are important comparison gaps; at minimum freeze them as descriptive baselines or narrow the claim before inference. I found no algebraic contradiction in the specified uniform-edit derivative or affine source-only map. Its real validity remains contingent on pinned hooks, exact score code, the ASR/listening gate for the 480 new WAVs, the resource pilot, all-planned denominators, and word-disjoint development and locked-test results. The active separate Voxtral development patch experiment must not be used to tune this lens.

## Re-review of revised design (`9eb963c`)

I reviewed revised proposal SHA-256 `84e7e0b46c01f698cf42ea9c1ba8a8d9b480a6561b8b68edc4e60beeb5454087` without opening model outputs. The previous semantic-entry blocker is **resolved in the text**: FIT now requires at least 60/80 exact intended recipient+EOS natural answers and 60/80 source-only correct-word predictions, with separate ≥60 denominators and ≥90% rates for both prefix tests. Development and locked test scale these to 72/96. Model forced-choice preference is separately named score fidelity. These thresholds are internally consistent; the paired-class count is redundant when exact intended answers already meet the same threshold, but harmless. The projector direct-readout baseline is dimensionally valid because its projector rows and LM target are both width 3,072. The added FIT-pilot rowwise-gradient oracle can distinguish BF16 row nonuniformity from failure of the uniform-mean derivative, provided executable code freezes its exact contraction and uses no evaluation-time oracle as a fitted map.

**One comparator blocker remains.** The new natural-state ridge is specified as a regression from six natural source-bin means to a *scalar natural paired contrast*. That scalar is defined with the recipient word as positive, so its target changes meaning for every pair and reverses orientation across the two directions of a pair. The inputs contain no candidate-token identity. A constant-positive scalar predictor can therefore score well simply when the natural model already favors the spoken recipient; the regression cannot yield vocabulary logits or a word rank for unseen lexical pairs. To make it a fair source-only readout comparator, regress the six natural source-bin means to the fixed 32-dimensional `Uᵀz` target (or full terminal state), fit an intercept/anchor using only training pairs, reconstruct the target state, and apply the **same frozen LM final norm/head and candidate-token scoring** as the Jacobian map. Keep the whole-pair outer and inner folds, source scaling, penalty grid, and all-planned denominators. If scalar contrast prediction remains as an additional diagnostic, label it a pair-orientation confidence baseline and do not score its word readout or claim that the Jacobian beats a natural-state probe from it.

**Disposition:** Hold the executable freeze for this comparator correction and exact runner-level specifications. No new scientific outcome or security claim follows from a revised design alone.
