## Required correction

The plan calls the post-block-11 hook “before the encoder's final layer norm.” That is false for this exact checkpoint. Cached `facebook/wav2vec2-base-960h@22aad52d435eb6dbaf354bdad9b0da84ce7d6156/config.json` has `do_stable_layer_norm=false`. The installed `modeling_wav2vec2.py` SHA-256 `c404982e3a063a5f68ee85809356c8a5ed29ad24c8441eea1d56346a57b5da0c`, `Wav2Vec2Encoder.forward` (lines 657–728), applies positional convolution and `self.layer_norm` **before** looping over the 12 encoder blocks; it returns the block-11 output without another norm. `Wav2Vec2ForCTC.forward` (lines 1704–1726) then applies its final dropout, an identity in `eval()`, and the linear CTC head. This is a site-description error, not evidence against the proposed hook. Correct the site and interpretation in a prospectively committed V2 amendment before runner preflight or inference; preserve V1 and this HOLD.

Under the plan's **stronger two-endpoint complete-transcript rule**, only Piper `plate`↔`cup` qualifies in each mode. With `eval()`, `mask_time_indices=None` and no attention mask, the pinned missing `wav2vec2.masked_spec_embed` is not used.
