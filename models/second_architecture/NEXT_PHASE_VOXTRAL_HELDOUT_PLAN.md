# Voxtral next phase: held-out audio-time mechanism study

**Design only, 18 November 2026.** No new model run, fitted lens, or locked-test result is claimed here. Freeze a versioned protocol, scripts, split manifest, and resource estimate before opening new development or test outputs. This proposal is the Voxtral companion to the [Qwen corpus transport proposal](../../eval/audio_jlens/NEXT_PHASE_CORPUS_LENS_PROPOSAL.md); share a measurement grammar and prospective stimulus split, never learned weights, prompt serialization, or layer indices.

## Question and current boundary

For a fixed spoken question, where does the answer-relevant change first arise, where can it be causally transmitted, and can a mapping fitted on other words predict a held-out word's effect? Separate four transformations: **PCM waveform → raw log-mel → clip-normalized mel → audio encoder/projector → language-model answer**. A difference at a state is observational; a donor patch that changes the complete answer is a causal intervention on that state; a fit-only transport that predicts fresh cases is a stronger generalization test. None alone establishes an exploitable security violation.

The [Piper fit result](DECOMPOSITION_FINDINGS_V1.md) and [Kokoro Sarah fit result](VOXTRAL_KOKORO_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) each found six of six full Red/Blue answers followed the feature word-window donor, with EOS. These are **one lexical pair** and six off-manifold feature conditions per voice. Exact PCM was unchanged outside the edited word, yet the processor changed off-word and padded mel values through a clip-wide maximum/floor. Shared-max reconstruction removed those off-word feature differences. The broad projector-state difference therefore cannot by itself be called a word circuit. The existing Qwen locked test and these Voxtral fit clips are historical/plumbing material, never a new locked test.

This question overlaps published [causal tracing of Voxtral, DeSTA, and Qwen](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13768), which reports early audio-text fusion in Voxtral and a final-token retrieval bottleneck using clean-versus-silence patching. [Prosody intervention work](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19211) also shows why readable information or a shifted internal score is weaker than a changed full answer. The proposed contribution is narrower and testable: matched-word PCM and frontend-factor controls, time-resolved donor interventions, plus a **corpus-fitted**, held-out audio-to-answer prediction across voices. It is not a first claim to trace audio activations. Compare any lens against the [published Audio Jacobian Lens code](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/tree/1784e62e4caaf801bfc8dc1208860703b5f3d074) and [Anthropic's Jacobian Lens method](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html).

## Prospective corpus and freeze order

Use the same **new**, balanced 48 lexical-pair stimulus design proposed for Qwen: four answer categories, 12 pairs/category; whole-pair split 24 fit, 12 development, 12 locked test (6/3/3 per category); two independently generated voices and two prompt templates per split; both directions. Freeze the split seed, all WAV/PCM hashes, target-word sample spans, target-model-independent ASR check, source-specific answer aliases, processor tensors, prompt token IDs, and row maps. Add an independently recorded speaker challenge only as a separately named challenge. Use a blinded listening check for a small sample. Previously opened Red/Blue, cat/dog, Monday/Friday, north/south, seven/eight are excluded from the new locked set.

Freeze the Voxtral protocol **before either architecture opens the new locked test**. Qwen observations, including its already opened historical locked set, cannot be used to choose Voxtral sites, alpha grid, templates, aliases, or thresholds. Reusing the same new stimuli provides a comparison of methods across architectures, not independent replication of a model result. Publish all-planned counts even when clean answers are wrong. Freeze literal valid outputs and semantic aliases (e.g. `Seven`, `seven`, `7`) before any new answer is viewed; record raw suffix IDs, exact text, EOS, strict exact-surface success, and separately normalized semantic success. Clean eligibility means **both natural endpoints** in a pair/voice/template cell produce the expected complete answer with EOS under the frozen surface policy; it is a supplementary analysis subset, never a reason to remove a direction from the all-planned denominator.

## Staged assays

1. **Fit-only capability and frontend gate.** For each candidate pair, check identical PCM outside the word span where splice design permits, clean transcript and valid answer, identical audio prompt IDs, independent feature reconstruction error ≤1e-6, and shared-max off-word error ≤1e-6 for truly matched PCM. Record native raw maxima and the four crossed raw-content × normalization-maximum feature inputs on fit items only. Keep these off-manifold feature effects distinct from natural PCM behavior. For natural non-spliced voices, report outside-word PCM mismatch instead of asserting equality.
2. **Local Jacobian and patch feasibility on fit only.** For class `c`, freeze a set `A_c` of **unique token-ID sequences** for all accepted literal aliases, each with EOS. Define `L_c=logsumexp_{a∈A_c} LL(a)` where `LL(a)` is the teacher-forced sum of each token's conditional log probability, including EOS; deduplicate aliases that encode to the same sequence. The complete-reply margin is `S=L_B−L_A`. For recipient source `H`, compute an FP32 vector-Jacobian product `∇_H S` at encoder-31 and, separately, projector output. For donor difference `ΔH`, predict `ΔS≈⟨∇_H S, αΔH⟩` at `α={0,.01,.025,.05,1}`; compare to an actual no-grad intervention with the **same rounded edit and site**. `α=1` is a nonlinear stress check, not a derivative-validity claim. Run reverse direction, same-source self patch, zero edit, and passive-hook parity; require exact unpatched suffix-ID equality to the ordinary route. Save layer/site, source/target shapes, hook count, activation dtype, gradient finite/norm, measured/predicted margins, generated full answer and EOS. No test-example backward pass is allowed later.
3. **Time-resolved donor interventions.** Define four equal-duration bins inside the validated word interval plus equal-duration immediately pre/post bins, then map through the frozen frontend to conv/encoder/projector rows. A bin-uniform additive edit and a full rowwise donor replacement are **separate interventions**. At projector and encoder-31 sites, compare matched word-bin edits with same-source sham, reverse, equal-duration **and Frobenius-norm-matched** pre/post edits, equal-norm random directions on the same row support, shuffled bins, and padding rows. Freeze the scale rule on fit: scale each nonzero pre/post donor difference to the corresponding word-bin edit norm; a zero-norm control remains zero and is reported as undefined for the norm-matched contrast, never silently excluded. Report raw unscaled effects too. Off-word state edits are comparative controls, not null controls because attention can spread lexical information. Record projector row-distance profiles as descriptive context, not localization proof. A donor answer flip requires a complete generated suffix with EOS, not a logit change.
4. **Reusable fit-only transport, conditional on cost gate.** Use the Qwen proposal's fit-only rank-16 terminal-residual basis and six-bin grammar, estimated *separately* for Voxtral. For site/bin, fit a corpus-average rectangular map from a uniform row edit to the prompt-last residual; report paired difference and absolute affine readout. Compare zero, fit mean alone, equal-rank global pooled map, shuffled-bin map, fit-only ridge, and exact-transcript/independent-ASR text routes through Voxtral. A per-recipient VJP is an oracle diagnostic, never the fitted baseline. Measure development once with no backward pass; open locked test once only if the frozen development gate passes. Never sum projector and encoder transports.

The primary **behavioral** endpoint is complete generated answer plus EOS on every planned direction. The continuous **full-sequence** `L_B−L_A` margin above is secondary. For a fitted prompt-last-residual transport, separately report **first-token** alias mass/rank: sum next-token probabilities over the unique first token IDs of frozen aliases in each class. This is a different, weaker endpoint; aliases sharing a first token are counted once, and first-token mass does **not** predict later tokens, EOS, or the full reply. Report predicted-versus-measured small-alpha full-sequence score effects only where that score is actually evaluated, held-out target-state projection coverage, paired error, absolute-affine first-token advantage over target mean, and pair-level bootstrap intervals. Use the common proposed development gates in the [Qwen plan](../../eval/audio_jlens/NEXT_PHASE_CORPUS_LENS_PROPOSAL.md): ≥75% sign agreement above a fit-derived numerical floor, MAE ≤0.75 of zero and global baselines, affine advantage on ≥70% clean-eligible directions, and ≥75% clean eligibility. Evaluate sign, error, and answer effects on **all planned directions** and again on the clean-eligible subset; report both denominators and never replace the all-planned result with eligibility conditioning. Label time-selective only if the word-bin effect beats **each equal-norm** pre/post and bin-shuffled comparator in ≥75% clean-eligible directions and fewer than 10% of those controls flip to the exact donor answer. These numbers are *proposal thresholds* until committed in a versioned protocol. Cluster intervals by whole lexical pair.

## MPS budget and stop rules

The existing **BF16** six-condition fit runs peaked at approximately **9.93 GiB MPS driver** under the 30 GiB process cap. This is evidence for generation and teacher forcing only; it does **not** certify FP32 backward memory. The 30-second padded feature path yields 375 projector rows even for short clips, so shortening speech alone does not proportionally cut compute. A direct FP32 Voxtral gradient may need roughly twice the BF16 model weight storage, plus activations and autograd; measure rather than extrapolate a safe peak.

Before implementing the full corpus, benchmark **one fit pair in both directions, both sites, all six bins**, including a serial FP32 backward pass, candidate+EOS scoring, one full generation, and memory samples at the loaded, forward, and backward peaks. Stop if the **FP32 backward** MPS driver allocation exceeds 30 GiB, a gradient is nonfinite, unpatched IDs change, or source/target dimensions differ from frozen expectations. Run batch size 1 and no concurrent model process. Fit has `24 pairs × 2 voices × 2 templates × 2 recipient clips = 192 contexts`; development and locked test have `12 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 96 contexts` each. A single backward pass can expose gradients at both sites and all rows/bins if hooks retain both states, so a rank-16 fit has a **best-case** `192 × 16 = 3,072` backward passes, plus fit forwards and SVD. If implementation requires separate site passes, budget **6,144**; if it requires a pass per bin, multiply again by **six**. Do not label the best case as measured until the benchmark proves shared-pass gradients match separate-pass gradients.

The full time grid is large even without backward passes: per 96-context evaluation split, `2 sites × 6 bins × 4 nonzero α = 4,608` patched score evaluations, with another `96 × 2 × 6 = 1,152` full-answer `α=1` generations before reverse/sham/equal-norm/random/shuffle/padding controls, aliases, or baselines. Reserve and measure those additional control and candidate-sequence costs explicitly; do not hide them inside the 3,072-backward estimate. Benchmark fit-only time per backward, per complete-sequence score, per full generation, and per control condition; multiply by the exact predeclared counts and record both a worst-case and a measured shared-pass estimate. Require a precommitted **six-hour total local model-work cap for Voxtral**, including fit, development, and locked-test projections. If the projected protocol exceeds it, report method feasibility/cost and defer corpus fitting or redesign **on fit only before the new locked test is opened**; do not silently drop sites, bins, controls, or test cells after seeing answers. A smaller prespecified fit-only pilot can diagnose implementation, with no held-out efficacy claim. No cloud GPU is required by this plan.

## Security decision after mechanism evidence

The lexical study is a positive control for an audio-to-answer pathway, not a security finding. Only after a held-out mechanism passes should a separate, frozen authorization assay compare typed and spoken *same-transcript* requests under an explicit policy, with inert tools and a stated attacker-controlled acoustic surface. A concrete finding requires a reproducible **wrong complete decision or native action**, the exact trust boundary and attacker capability, and matched text/ASR/harness controls. A score shift, activation probe, or feature splice alone is insufficient.
