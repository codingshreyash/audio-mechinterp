## Blocking counterexample

The V2 plan says a third independent listener reviews **any disagreement about the full verbatim transcript**. `human_qa_audit.evaluate()` instead compares only `full_tokens`, the normalized token lists. I independently supplied two otherwise valid synthetic 32-clip exports whose first verbatim responses differed only in formatting: the primary used `I said … stopped.` and the secondary `i said … stopped!`. Their raw full transcripts differed, but both normalized to the same correct tokens. The auditor returned `disagreement: false`, `third_required: false`, and `human_qa_32_of_32_pass` without a tertiary export. This violates the frozen escalation rule even though both responses meet the separate quality criterion.

The correction is narrow: compare the two raw `verbatim_transcript` strings when deciding whether a third full-transcript review is required, while keeping the frozen normalized parser for the intended-word and full-script correctness test. Add a regression test for two raw-different, normalized-equal full transcripts; it must HOLD without a third export and may pass only after a distinct third reviewer resolves the disagreement under the V2 two-of-three rule. Preserve both raw initial responses. A future reviewer should rerun the six existing tests and this new test before source GO. The current 32-WAV technical render GO is unaffected.
