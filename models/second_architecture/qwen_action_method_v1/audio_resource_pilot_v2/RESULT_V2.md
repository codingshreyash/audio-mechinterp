**Result: PASS for the technical, non-study pilot on one constructed input.** The frozen 16-second zero-padded Apple WAV (SHA-256 `b84ce83e90ad498e93063a27e8cba9a3a860985e5801c55385a95e0483735973`) ran on one H100 with pinned Qwen2.5-Omni-3B checkpoint `f75b40e3da2003cdd6e1829b1f420ca70797c34e`. The saved audit returned `technical_nonstudy_resource_parity_only`. The [independent raw audit](INDEPENDENT_ATTEMPT_002_RAW_AUDIT_V2.md) checked the receipts and recomputed the saved-audit result without model weights.

The stock and passive-hook forwards had bitwise identical full first-step logits (151,936 values; maximum absolute difference 0). Both greedy continuations had the same 12 token IDs and stopped at EOS. The passive hooks fired at shared Thinker residual layers 17 and 34, once over the full prompt and once per subsequent generated token. This establishes that these **read-only** measurements preserved this one response. It does not identify or manipulate an audio feature.

The worker exited normally in 24.25 seconds. The V2 parent classified a closed heartbeat at the clean worker-exit boundary, recorded a terminal receipt, and finished in 24.65 seconds. Peak sampled physical GPU memory was 20,012,072,960 bytes, process-tree RSS 14,481,063,936 bytes, and PyTorch reserved memory 19,287,506,944 bytes; all were below the frozen caps. Physical-memory sampling cannot exclude a shorter peak between polls. Raw evidence occupies 16,752,366 bytes. The [saved audit receipt](../runs/audio_resource_pilot_v2/attempt_002_saved_audit_v1.json) and [raw attempt](../runs/audio_resource_pilot_v2/attempt_002) are present locally, and checksum comparison found no difference from the Dallas copy.
