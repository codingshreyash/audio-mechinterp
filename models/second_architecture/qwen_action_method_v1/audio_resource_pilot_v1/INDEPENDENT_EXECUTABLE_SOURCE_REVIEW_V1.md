19 November 2026. **PASS for the committed source at `7ca2380`; HOLD weight loading until the separate no-weight admission, fixed executable review, and committed GO.** This review is not the gate's `INDEPENDENT_EXECUTABLE_REVIEW_V1.md`. I loaded no model weights, created no admission or GO, and processed no `D_*` or `H_*` calendar clip.

I inspected `plan.py`, `gate.py`, `parent.py`, `worker.py`, `saved_audit.py`, their imported local source, the frozen protocol, and the 16-second WAV. The source identity census covers 29 tracked files, including the exact WAV, Qwen adapter and time map, audio candidate validator, typed CUDA preflight modules and manifest, and all pilot executable files. It rejects missing, symlinked, untracked, or dirty source. The source preflight reports the exact 256,000-sample WAV hash, with no GO and no attempt. The fixed source/GO gate checks commit order, exact source hashes, admission and review hashes, Slurm job/node/single allocated GPU entry, one visible idle physical H100, pinned runtime and snapshot, and a fresh fixed attempt before parent launch. This is a same-user liveness and source-integrity gate, not protection against a malicious local owner.

The worker checks the inherited parent pipe and nonce before importing Torch or Transformers or loading weights. It independently rehashes the snapshot before `from_pretrained`, reconstructs only the pinned non-study audio batch, and requires the frozen processor geometry: IDs/mask `[1,423]`, features `[1,128,30000]`, 1,600 active mel frames, 800 encoder rows, 400 projected rows, and audio placeholders 17–416. The four processor tensors are saved; tensor equality is checked after each forward and generation call. The no-cache stock and passive-hook forwards use the same batch, enforce one hook call at each shared Thinker residual site `.17` and `.34`, retain full first-step logits, and require maximum absolute difference at most `1e-5`. The hooks return `None`. Greedy stock and passive generation use the same 12-token cap and EOS 151645; full raw paths and stop reasons must agree. Empty and unexplained short stops now fail, while EOS, malformed early EOS, and length censoring are explicitly recorded without a correctness claim.

The parent keeps a 900-second deadline with a 20-second terminal reserve, samples process-tree RSS and physical GPU use, kills the worker group on sampled cap/monitor failure, and caps whole-attempt evidence. The worker also checks PyTorch peak reservation below 60 GiB at three phases. The saved auditor requires the fixed parent/worker/GO receipt chain, resource samples and peaks, exact sidecar inventory and hashes, a fresh no-weight processor reconstruction equal to every saved tensor, full-logit parity, hook shapes, and complete generated-path parity. A partial or oversized attempt cannot be promoted to technical PASS by the auditor. Sampled memory bounds do not prove that no transient peak occurred between samples.
