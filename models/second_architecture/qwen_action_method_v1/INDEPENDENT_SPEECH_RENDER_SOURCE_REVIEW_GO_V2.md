## What the GO permits and what it does not prove

The renderer writes the fixed 18 scripts in three pinned synthetic voices, rejects a converted source at or beyond 16 seconds, writes exact 16-kHz mono PCM16 with zero tail to 256,000 samples, and preserves a failed attempt. Its saved auditor reconstructs each converted/final waveform from raw bytes and checks the fixed case inventory. A terminal receipt is written and fsynced after the manifest; the auditor checks terminal hashes, ordering, mtime and 900-second deadline, pre/post AC/open-lid/thermal-free logs, a sampled 8-GiB process-tree RSS limit, and the 200-MiB evidence cap. The RSS guard samples every 0.25 seconds, so it can miss a shorter transient; the source claims only a **sampled** bound. The byte audit uses the same pinned conversion implementation as the renderer, with independent saved-byte reconstruction and this separate review, not a second resampling algorithm.

The bundle builder is downstream of a passing technical audit. Its read-only audit rehashes all 36 copied WAVs, reconstructs both different fixed queue orders, and compares the two HTML forms byte for byte. It verifies that only opaque `q01.wav`–`q18.wav` names are inside reviewer folders; the technical mapping stays outside them. The forms contain no script, case ID or expected label. No human listening result exists, and all 18 sources still require two blinded reviewers plus third review on critical disagreement.

The one-shot GO is conditional on exact committed source and review hashes in `SPEECH_RENDER_GO_V2.json`. A failed live AC/lid/thermal, time, memory, source, waveform or byte gate remains a failed attempt; it must not be silently retried under this receipt.
