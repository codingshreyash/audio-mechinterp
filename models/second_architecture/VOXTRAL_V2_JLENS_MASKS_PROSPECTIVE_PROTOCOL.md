# Prospective CPU mask preflight for fresh Voxtral Jacobian corpus

**Source proposal, not an inference authorization or an executable mask freeze.** This checks only the 480 technically bound v2 WAVs and their metadata. It reads locked-test **input audio** to validate geometry and bytes, but never loads a model or opens any locked-test answer, score, or activation. The [Jacobian design](PROSPECTIVE_VOXTRAL_AUDIO_JACOBIAN_LENS_V1.md) still requires independent ASR/listening QA before Voxtral answers.

`voxtral_v2_jlens_masks.py --validate-only` checks exact SHA-256 pins for the corpus manifest, its independent technical binding, candidate assignment, render freeze, reviewed Jacobian design, and old FIT mask builder. It reconstructs all 240 pair/voice/template records, 60 primary word pairs, 16 basis and 20 map FIT pairs, 12 development and 12 locked-test pairs, and two words per record. The 12 reserves remain inactive. It rehashes all 480 WAV files and their PCM16 payloads, checks mono/16-kHz/PCM16 format, equal paired lengths, and bit-identical PCM outside each word slot with distinct PCM inside.

For each matched record, the script reapplies the **same nominal geometry** as the reviewed FIT mask builder: one full-word-width pre interval, four half-open word quarters, one full-word-width post interval, directly overlapping 10-ms mel frames, encoder centers `320*j`, projector centers `160*(8*r+3)`, and equal-count nominal padding rows. It requires six nonempty, disjoint bins at both sites, no pre/post span outside the clip, and mel frame 2999 to be nominally padded. The intended model tensor shapes `[1,1500,1280]` and `[375,3072]` are recorded as **assumptions**, not verified processor outputs. A later processor-only check and hook-shape check must pass before any model interpretation. An unsupported clip or geometry produces an explicit failed preflight and no substituted mask.

The source currently **does not write a mask manifest**. `--write-manifest` requires a separate `VOXTRAL_V2_JLENS_MASKS_FREEZE_V1.json` binding exact builder and input hashes after independent source review. This receipt does not exist yet. `--validate-only` completed for 240 records and 480 WAVs with no nominal-geometry failure; its three CPU tests pass. That result establishes reproducible time supports, not an acoustic origin, intelligibility, an audio encoder activation shape, a Jacobian, or a security finding.
