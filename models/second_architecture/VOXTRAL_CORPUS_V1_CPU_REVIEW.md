# Voxtral corpus_v1 CPU index review

**18 November 2026; no model inference.** The processor/tokenizer-only builder and fit-gate protocol were frozen before this index run in commit `7e9ff72`. The generated [index](voxtral_corpus_v1_index.json) has SHA-256 `bc739cf1f0d265426cc760ddd6a42223554b1097cab6ba87c2c8b81129d1b971`; it records builder SHA-256 `0fcffb871e1a54a4303c9b407fb111a375c9caeeea8a6d9ee488de650e6a34d5` and source manifest SHA-256 `adf8cd02d24a43ef576bcb993dc54bd2527440a62798e12fc788def3c063971f`.

The CPU run verified **192 matched pair records / 384 unique WAVs**, including file and int16 PCM hashes, matching sample counts, outside-word PCM equality, and a nonidentical word slot. It indexed 96 fit-pool pair records, 48 development, and 48 locked test. There are 16 deterministic pilot fit records (**four pair IDs × two voices × two templates**) and 32 pilot clips. The fixed pilot IDs are `animal_06`, `color_03`, `nature_00`, `object_02`; they were chosen by the lexicographic rule in the protocol, without ASR or candidate-model results. The voices are Piper `en_US-amy-medium` and Kokoro `af_bella`, different from the earlier Voxtral Red/Blue fit voices. The source words are distinct from the opened Red/Blue cases.

Every six-bin interval maps to at least three nominal projector rows. The union of each four word-bin row map exactly matched the earlier independent `nominal_word_mel_frames` and `nominal_word_connector_rows` functions for all 192 records. These are *direct pre-attention receptive-field* maps, not claims that post-encoder row information remains local. The builder loaded Voxtral's pinned processor/tokenizer and no weights. It froze four candidate spelling/leading-space surfaces per word, yielding **1,536 token-ID-plus-EOS sequences** for 96 unique words. For **74/96** words at least one accepted surface spans multiple tokens. Thus a first-token lens cannot by itself score a complete word; the later assay must keep full-sequence alias aggregation and generated full-answer/EOS readouts distinct.

This verifies input provenance and feasibility of indexing. It does not measure processor mel reproduction, clean Voxtral accuracy, hidden-state causality, voice transfer, or a security violation. The future fit-only capability gate may fail, and its failed clips must remain in the all-planned denominator. Development and locked-test candidate answers remain unopened for Voxtral.
