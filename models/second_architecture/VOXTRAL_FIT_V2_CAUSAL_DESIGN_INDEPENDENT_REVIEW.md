# Independent pre-implementation review: Voxtral v2 causal design

**18 November 2026. Scope:** CPU/source review of `VOXTRAL_FIT_V2_CAUSAL_DESIGN.md`, the pinned local Transformers 5.20.2 Voxtral source, the frozen corpus index, and the v2 FIT selection. No Voxtral inference or development/locked-test answer inspection. This reviews a proposal, not an observed causal result.

## What checks out

- The proposed hook boundaries match the pinned source: encoder `audio_tower.layers[31]` returns a tensor before the encoder's final layer norm; the encoder output is reshaped into four-row groups before `multi_modal_projector`, whose output is placed into language-model audio placeholders with `masked_scatter`. The nominal `[1,1500,1280]` and `[375,3072]` shapes are consistent with one padded clip. Encoder row-center `320j` samples and connector row-center `1280r+480` samples follow from the 160-sample mel hop, stride-two convolution, and four-row grouping. These are **nominal center maps**, not exclusive receptive fields.
- The 16-direction assay is FIT-only: four v2-selected word pairs × two voices × both directions. The v2 selection is explicitly fit-informed after the failed v1 homophone gate. Loss/restoration claims are correctly limited to the stated blanking and patch operations. Score-change signs and the `16 × 2 × 2 × 10 = 640` patched-generation arithmetic are coherent.
- A CPU calculation over the eight selected first-template pair/voice records finds nonempty center-assigned bins at both hooks: each word quarter has 11–20 encoder rows and 3–5 connector rows. This supports feasibility but does not replace freezing exact row lists and hashes.

## Initial freeze blockers (resolved in the revised design)

1. **Bind the v2 population explicitly.** The corpus index's `pilot_fit` flag names the *earlier v1* pilot pairs. A runner using that flag would silently run the wrong assay. Bind the SHA-256 of `VOXTRAL_FIT_V2_SELECTION.json`, require exactly its four pair IDs, first template, two engines, 16 directional recipients, and match its 16 selected clip hashes to the corpus index. Reject extras and omissions.
2. **Freeze disjoint rows and blanking bounds.** The index stores receptive-overlap rows, not patch masks. Specify exact integer intervals: word-quarter edges `start + floor((end-start)k/4)`; `pre=[start-(end-start),start)` and `post=[end,end+(end-start))`; center inclusion `lo <= center < hi`. The phrase “equal-duration pre/post intervals” currently leaves their length unclear: each is one *full word-slot* duration, not a quarter. Serialize each site/case/bin row list, verify pair directions share the same map, and reject empty bins. Freeze the exact direct-overlap mel-frame union and verify the recipient's normalized feature tensor is identical outside that union after blanking. Check frame 2999 is in padding for every selected WAV.
3. **Make controls executable before observing results.** Define the pre/post/padding donor-minus-base edit at each arm, padding-row choice, same-word random generator and case/site/arm seed derivation, exact norm calculation after BF16 casting, tolerance, and the zero-norm rule. A same-source zero edit is planned in *both* restoration and loss, so the self-parity gate should say **16/16 for each arm/site** (32 checks per site), not merely 16/16 per site. Require a passive-hook parity check for both clean and blank bases.
4. **Freeze classification and time-preference denominators.** Define precedence for nonterminal special tokens, early/missing EOS, whitespace/case/punctuation, and recipient/opposite/other. State explicitly that whole-word success is counted over 16 planned directions per site and arm, with restoration eligibility separately reported. Time preference should compare restoration and loss **separately** by site; define whether a control's complete-answer success means restoration or loss under that arm, use the same 16 planned denominators, and state how a malformed/control-invalid result affects the ≥12/16 score-comparison gate. Exact score ties already fail.
5. **Budget the actual workload.** The 736 figure counts generations only (640 patches +16 blank bases +48 feature-splice +32 opposite-donor controls). It omits source-activation capture/replays, hook parity, and teacher-forced candidate+EOS scores for the declared continuous endpoint; scoring each condition could add hundreds to more than a thousand prompt/continuation forwards. The 32-clip natural-pilot time is not a valid total-cost extrapolation. Benchmark a representative full case with both sites/arms, controls, capture, and scoring; calculate the projected **total** under the 45-minute/30-GiB gate. If the score is intentionally a subset, freeze that subset and its denominator before inference.

## Re-review of revised protocol and CPU mask artifact

The revised design and `VOXTRAL_FIT_V2_CAUSAL_MASKS.json` resolve all five specification blockers above. I independently recomputed the manifest SHA-256 (`a634fc0ab8454b7a82802c56a5365dc90e79f446e4d280cee6321adfead4108e`), builder SHA-256 (`ab8bdcf0374794c32b904cbdee37cba48c87296cef2970fbc0af896e0d21c2fc`), v2-selection SHA-256 (`f897d360dbcec9c09f1813f9c070294753fbabc25c6213ba6bdac34b1d79b56b`), and corpus-index SHA-256 (`bc739cf1f0d265426cc760ddd6a42223554b1097cab6ba87c2c8b81129d1b971`). All match their literal bindings. I checked all eight manifest records against the v2 selection and index: exactly four selected FIT pair IDs × Piper/Kokoro × `fit_chosen`; 16 selected clip hashes match; none uses the index's old `pilot_fit` flag.

Independently recalculating every half-open interval, mel overlap frame, center-assigned encoder/projector row, and nominal padding row reproduces the stored lists exactly. The four word bins are disjoint and nonempty; word support has 46–77 encoder rows or 12–19 projector rows, and its corresponding four bins have at least 11 or 3 rows respectively. Each word-frame mask excludes fill frame 2999, which lies beyond the selected WAV ends. The builder rejects overwrite, changed source hashes, wrong WAV formats, empty/overlapping bins, and insufficient padding rows.

The control math now defines the target as the **effective BF16** whole-word edit norm, makes one FP32 scale per off-word control, checks its realized BF16 norm within 5%, and freezes deterministic random seed inputs. Invalid norms stay in the planned denominator. Restoration eligibility excludes an already-correct blank answer; lexical loss requires a valid complete changed answer. The strict classifier has explicit special-token and surface precedence. Passive parity is 16/16 per base/site, self parity 16/16 per arm/site, and restoration/loss/time-preference use separate 16-direction all-planned denominators. The workload is correctly expanded to **800 generations + up to 64 capture forwards + 1600 teacher-forced forwards = 2464 forward-like operations**, with a complete first-record benchmark and a 45-minute/30-GiB stop rule. Actual runtime and memory remain to be measured; the protocol no longer treats the natural pilot as that estimate.

**Final static verdict: no remaining design blocker.** Freeze the revised protocol and mask artifact before implementation, then make the runner fail closed on their hashes, tensor shapes, parity, effective norm tolerance, and measured full-workload projection. This is permission to implement and benchmark on FIT only, not a causal finding or development/test authorization. No model inference or development/locked-test answers were used in this re-review.
