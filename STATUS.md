# Research status — 2026-11-18

The objective is an independently verified, audio-aware mechanistic account of information flow through open audio models. A security finding requires a concrete attacker-controlled input, violated property, and end-to-end consequence; a readable activation alone does not meet that standard.

## Next decisive checks

1. Fit a **reusable** audio-time-to-output transport across a separate training corpus, following the published Jacobian Lens estimator where it applies. Freeze data, source/target positions, output-surface normalization, and baselines before development. The current per-input derivative is the local numerical sanity check, not the lens itself.
2. Test that transport on held-out words, speakers, phrasings, and acoustic conditions. Compare against transcript-only, anchor-constant, zero-change, and norm-only baselines. Record clean-answer failures, rank/readout quality, and causal interventions with complete generated answers.
3. Port the same measurement grammar to Voxtral's distinct Whisper-style encoder and projector, while keeping processor-level spread separate from model-internal influence. A cross-architecture comparison needs independently frozen corpora and matched endpoints.
4. After a transferred mechanism is established, test a bounded security decision with attacker-controlled audio and inert downstream actions. State the violated property and required harness conditions; do not treat a changed score or a readable feature as an exploit.

The [evaluation protocol](eval/PROTOCOL.md), [independent Whisper review](eval/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md), and [independent Omni review](eval/OMNI_REVIEW.md) define the checks. This ledger is updated as experiments complete; a failed result stays in the record.
