# Research status — 2026-11-18

The objective is an independently verified, audio-aware mechanistic account of information flow through open audio models. A security finding requires a concrete attacker-controlled input, violated property, and end-to-end consequence; a readable activation alone does not meet that standard.

## Next decisive checks

1. Preserve the negative Qwen Stage A result and keep its locked test sealed. The local-oracle/global gap remains, but the first FIT-only source-conditioned Gaussian router did not narrow it. Implement the [prospective Qwen v2 design](eval/audio_jlens/PROSPECTIVE_AUDIO_LENS_V2_DESIGN.md) with a fresh word-disjoint corpus and higher-rank basis, then compare conditional, same-rank fixed, ridge, zero, and J++-inspired expert controls under pair-held-out FIT gates before opening fresh development data.
2. Freeze a Voxtral transfer protocol before any Voxtral development inference. Carry the exact complete-answer restoration/loss endpoints, wrong-time/random/self/opposite controls, and 16-direction FIT failures into the design. Treat this FIT population as model-internal causal evidence, not a held-out or waveform-level result.
3. Extend validated mechanisms across new speakers, phrasings and acoustic conditions, then across a model with an actual generated-speech branch. Report text, speech and action outputs independently; neither a text-path readout nor a tool harness result explains the other channel by itself.
4. After transfer is established, test a bounded security decision with attacker-controlled audio and inert downstream actions. State the violated property and required harness conditions; do not treat a changed score or a readable feature as an exploit.

The [evaluation protocol](eval/PROTOCOL.md), [independent Whisper review](eval/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md), and [independent Omni review](eval/OMNI_REVIEW.md) define the checks. This ledger is updated as experiments complete; a failed result stays in the record.
