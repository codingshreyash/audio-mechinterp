# Research status — 2026-11-18

The objective is an independently verified, audio-aware mechanistic account of information flow through open audio models. A security finding requires a concrete attacker-controlled input, violated property, and end-to-end consequence; a readable activation alone does not meet that standard.

| Controlled audio-state intervention changes a complete answer | For one red/blue fit-only contrast, both TTS engines yielded correct repeated clean answers. This is a mechanism assay, not a security vulnerability or a general audio-model result. |
| The contrast propagates through the audio-to-language connector | Swapping donor projected states at the nominal word-slot connector rows flipped 4/4 complete answers across both TTS engines and both directions. The Thinker answer and Talker speech pathways are distinct. |
| Thinker patch sufficiency crosses at a late layer in this pair | Word-audio-placeholder donor patches flipped 4/4 answers per post-block layer through layer 30, then 0/4 at layers 31–35. Donor terminal-position patches showed the converse: 0/4 through layer 30, then 4/4 at layers 31–35. All-audio-placeholder patches at layers 28–35 followed the word-position pattern; self and 40 equal-norm random terminal controls at the boundary kept the recipient answer. | This is a fit-only, one-contrast causal influence curve. Layer-31 word patches still moved answer-token margins without flipping the full answer. No configured model layer type changes there. The data do not establish an instantaneous decision formation step or a unique circuit. |
| Block-31 terminal self-attention carries the fit-only word-patch flip | Restoring the clean terminal self-attention output after a layer-30 word-position donor patch prevented 4/4 answer flips, while restoring the terminal MLP output prevented 0/4. Donor attention alone flipped 2/4; donor MLP alone 0/4. Self and 20 equal-norm random attention edits gave 0/28 flips; all patched continuations ended normally. A later 16-head scan found 0/64 single-head donor flips and 0/64 single-head restorations. | The attention restore did not restore the original logit margin, so this is answer-level mediation of one intervention, not complete state mediation. The head scan is posthoc on the same pair and does not identify a unique circuit; attention mediation has not been tested on fresh words. |
| Fresh-word transfer supports late influence but shifts the boundary | On 16 clean-eligible directions from `cat/dog` and `Monday/Friday` across two new voices and two new spoken phrasings, word-position donor patches flipped complete answers at layers 29/30/31 in 16/16 each, 1/16 at 32, and 0/16 at 35. Self, direct wrong-time, and equal-norm random controls gave 0 donor flips across 400 outcomes. Terminal donor patches gave 0/16 at 31 and 16/16 at 35. | The preregistered layer-30 minus layer-31 contrast was **zero**; the fit-only exact layer-31 boundary did not transfer. One model, two lexical pairs, synthetic voices. Independent audit verified the results but noted the plan freeze lacks an immutable pre-run commit. This does not verify the fit-only block-31 attention mediation on new inputs or establish a fixed handoff layer. |

Donor post-block-32 terminal residual alone flipped 10/16; donor attention or MLP output alone 0/16. | The same already-inspected audio was reused, and two Kokoro animal cases still flipped under attention restoration. An independent audit verified the saved results. This is an exploratory path diagnostic, not complete mediation or independent confirmation. |

## Next decisive checks

1. Freeze fresh held-out spoken contrasts before running the model: new word pairs, different phrasings, both TTS engines, and predeclared patch positions and controls. Check clean full-answer behavior first and report failures as failures.
2. Test whether the encoder→connector→Thinker word-position path and the block-31 attention-versus-MLP result transfer to those contrasts. Analyze by independent word pair and voice, not by correlated layer patches.
3. Revisit the full-rank audio-conditioned Jacobian readout with a task frame that has a positive semantic metric; the first 12-item fit-only bridge was negative and an anchor-only control matched most of the apparent gain. Keep the audio encoder and connector as separate time-resolved experiments.
4. Only after held-out mechanism transfer, test a bounded security decision with realistic attacker-controlled audio and inert downstream actions. Separate model behavior from harness effects.

The [evaluation protocol](eval/PROTOCOL.md), [independent Whisper review](eval/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md), and [independent Omni review](eval/OMNI_REVIEW.md) define the checks. This ledger is updated as experiments complete; a failed result stays in the record.
