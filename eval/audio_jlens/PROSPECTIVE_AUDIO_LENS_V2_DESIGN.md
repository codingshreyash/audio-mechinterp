# Prospective Qwen audio transport v2: conditional local derivatives

**Design for review; not a frozen protocol or a result.** Qwen v1 Stage A failed its prespecified development accuracy gate and stops. Its locked test stays sealed. This proposal uses the independently audited **FIT-only** heterogeneity result to choose a new question; it does not relabel v1 or tune against any v1 development item. The FIT diagnostic found local versus shared prefix-score MAE of 0.0176 versus 0.0297 at encoder31 and 0.0252 versus 0.0400 at the connector. Its rank-16 basis captured only about 22% of measured terminal-change energy. The local derivative is an in-sample oracle, so neither number shows that a reusable conditional map works.

**Prior art:** The released [J++ Lens code and method](https://github.com/safety-research/jpp_lens) already clusters activations into expert Jacobians, then learns signed expert weights. Its deployed lens is a *fixed combined map*; it also uses an LRP backward graph and readout filtering. Thus neither clustered Jacobians nor a mixture of maps is a novelty claim here. The distinct empirical question is whether an **audio-context-conditioned map at inference** predicts unseen audio edits better than matched fixed-map baselines. Released J++ weights are for a different text model and cannot be applied directly to Qwen2.5-Omni audio states.

## Questions and estimand

1. Is the shared-map failure mainly **context dependence**, **rank loss**, or both? Can unpatched audio states select a derivative that predicts an unseen pair better than a same-rank global map?
2. Does a model-internal audio representation carry the word-specific effect through encoder31 and connector into the prompt-final text residual, or do observed answer changes depend on other paths? Which acoustic changes are already globalized by preprocessing or attention?
3. Does a predicted small change in the complete first-token class score correspond to a causal change in a **complete generated answer** under the same intervention? Keep these endpoints separate.

For source site `s`, prompt `i`, six frozen time bins `b`, and terminal residual `h_i`, define the **uniform-edit** local derivative `J_{i,s,b}=∂(Uᵀh_i)/∂δ_b`, where the same vector `δ_b` is added to every selected source row in bin `b`. A donor-minus-recipient bin mean supplies the test edit `δ_b`; the intervention and derivative use identical row sets and normalization. The learned predictor is `Δĥ_i=U Σ_b Ĵ_{s,b}(φ_i)δ_b`, followed by the **pinned final norm and LM head** to produce the first-token class contrast change `ΔŜ`. `φ_i` is computed solely from the recipient's **unpatched** audio source activations and frozen row map. It cannot use donor states, the expected word, output logits, target residuals, pair ID, category, voice label, or any local derivative from the evaluated pair. The donor state is used only to define the evaluated edit. A full rowwise donor swap and a natural waveform-pair difference are distinct, secondary interventions; neither is called an exact test of this uniform-edit estimator.

## Fresh corpus and data firewall

Construct **60 new lexical pairs**, balanced 15 per predeclared category (animal, color, nature, object), with no word or close inflection reused from *any* prior corpus split, including the still unopened v1 locked-test manifest. Read the old locked manifest's **word metadata only** for this exclusion; never open its WAVs, model outputs, activations, or scores. No v2 word may occur in two pairs or two splits. Reject homophones and class-prefix collisions by a deterministic lexicon/tokenizer rule before Qwen inference, not by looking at model answers. Freeze the candidate list, exclusion rule, seed, pair assignment, exact sample spans, renderer versions, WAV SHA-256s, processor, tokenizer, prompt templates, candidate-prefix token sets, and scoring code before the first v2 model output. Use two pinned independent TTS families. Consistent with the now frozen [corpus render protocol](corpus_v2/RENDER_PROTOCOL_PROSPECTIVE_V1.md), save independent ASR and listening/intelligibility QA for every WAV before Qwen inference; the technical WAV binding alone does not authorize model evaluation. Matched pair WAVs must have bit-identical PCM outside the word slot.

Feasibility is **480 rendered WAVs**: 60 pairs × two words × two voices × two split-specific templates. The full CPU render and exact PCM audit are complete under a separate frozen source receipt; intelligibility QA remains pending. The 12 screened reserve pairs remain inactive. A failed primary WAV or intelligibility gate stops v2 before Qwen inference; no post-render pair or split substitution is allowed. Existing v1 development results supply no pair choice, scoring policy, threshold, or hyperparameter; the already reported v1 aggregate failure motivates the new question but is not training data.

Split **whole pairs** by frozen seed: 36 FIT (nine/category), 12 development (three/category), 12 locked test (three/category). Within FIT, reserve **16 basis pairs** (four/category) solely to estimate a rank-32 terminal basis from the two voice-specific donor differences per pair, and **20 map pairs** (five/category) for local-Jacobian training and pair-held-out FIT comparisons. The basis pool has 32 candidate difference vectors; define numerical rank by `σ_j/σ_1 > 1e-6` with finite `σ_1>0`, and stop v2 if fewer than 32 survive. Use two templates unique to each split. The first FIT template produces **80 map contexts** (20 pairs × two voices × two recipient directions); the second FIT template is a task/robustness diagnostic, not extra map training. Development and locked test each contain **96 all-planned directions** (12 pairs × two voices × two directions × two split-specific templates). No v1 development or locked-test audio, answer, activation, or score becomes v2 training or evaluation data. The old v1 locked test remains unused; do not rename it as fresh v2 test.

The first-token score is a fit-frozen full-vocabulary log-mass contrast between disjoint recipient and foil prefix-token sets. Candidate sets must be nonempty and disjoint for every pair, and the frozen alias policy must cover the intended one-word forms without depending on generated answers. Report absolute class mass and prefix purity, since a broad prefix can be ambiguous. Independently classify full generated suffix IDs: exactly one final EOS, no earlier special token, and one semantically expected word under frozen aliases. Before development, require on the 80 FIT map contexts (i) at least **60/80** clean semantic one-word+EOS answers, (ii) at least **90% prefix coverage** among complete answers semantically classified as recipient or foil, and (iii) at least **90% prefix purity** among complete answers whose first token falls in either pair class: the complete answer must match that class. A zero denominator or undefined class assignment fails this gate. Apply the same coverage and purity thresholds independently to all **96** development and **96** locked-test directions; if either fails, the primary transport claim fails. Keep all planned directions in transport metrics even when clean output is wrong. Canonical complete word+EOS teacher-forced scores and generated full answers are secondary behavioral endpoints; a prompt-final Jacobian alone does not predict later autoregressive tokens.

## Two fixed candidate maps and fair FIT comparison

Fit **rank 32** at the same encoder31 and connector hooks used by v1. Both sites use the same terminal basis `U` and 80 FIT map contexts. Compute 32 target-basis VJPs per context, recording the six `[rank, hidden_width]` bin derivatives. The three primary small edit strengths are fixed at **`α∈{0.005,0.01,0.025}`**, matching the frozen v1 Stage A metric; `α=0`, `α=0.05` time controls, and `α=1` complete-answer edits have separate roles. The **global rank-32 map** is the pair-balanced arithmetic mean of training local derivatives. The **conditional rank-32 map** is a shrinkage kernel average of the same derivatives: `(1−λ) J_global + λ Σ_i w_i(φ)J_i`, with nonnegative weights summing to one. Build `φ` from the six recipient source-bin means, z-score each coordinate using training-only mean/standard deviation (replace a zero standard deviation by one), then take the first eight training-only PCA components, or all nonzero components if fewer than eight. Use a Gaussian kernel with bandwidth `{0.5,1,2,4} ×` the median nonzero between-pair distance in training `φ`; a zero/nonfinite median fails the conditional candidate. Freeze `λ∈{0,.25,.5,.75,1}` and the deterministic PCA sign convention before FIT inference. Do not expose pair/category/voice IDs as features. Include a fixed `λ=0` global case, zero-change, rank-16 truncation, equal-rank global ridge, equal-rank six-bin ridge, and a shuffled-`φ` conditional negative control. Ridge maps use the same 80 contexts and the same uniform-edit target; choose penalties from `{.01,.1,1,10}` by inner pair-held-out FIT folds. A fresh per-recipient VJP is an **oracle ceiling**, never a reusable baseline or test-time input.

Add a **J++-inspired fixed expert comparator** on the same 80 contexts and 32-dimensional target basis: cluster train-only source activations into four or eight groups, average local derivatives within each cluster, and fit **one signed weight per expert**. Optimize the weights only on inner training pairs to minimize squared error in the same measured small-`α` first-token `ΔS` used by all predictors, with L2 penalty `ρ∈{.01,.1,1,10}`; select group count/penalty in inner pair folds. Refit selected weights on outer-training pairs, yielding **one fixed operator** for each outer fold; the held-out pair never affects clustering, weights, regularization, or basis. This controls for the released method's central idea without pretending that a plain-gradient audio port reproduces its LRP rules, vocabulary readout filtering, or **its signed-weight training target**: the released implementation fits a smooth token-rank surrogate and selects pass@k, while this comparator fits small-edit `ΔS`. It is an algorithmic adaptation, **not an exact J++ replication**. Before development, document whether an exact J++ LRP/weighting port can hook both Qwen audio sites within the six-hour/30-GiB budget; if not, retain this clearly labeled inspired comparator and do not imply an exact head-to-head benchmark. **The inspired fixed expert map is a required comparator at FIT, development and locked test, including the primary MAE gate.** Use the same training cases, VJPs, target basis, score endpoint and parameter-count disclosure for every map. A conditional map adds inference-time source-feature computation and cannot claim J++'s fixed-map zero-extra-inference-cost property.

For each of the 20 outer FIT folds, exclude **both voices, both directions, and both templates** of one word pair from every map, feature PCA, standardization, bandwidth/penalty choice, and calibration. The rank-32 basis is independent of all 20 map pairs, so it is safe to reuse across outer folds. Refit each comparator on the same remaining 19 pairs, choose hyperparameters by inner pair folds, then score the four held-out first-template contexts without backward passes. The **conditional family is the sole primary candidate**: never switch to the best comparator after seeing results. For every family/site, choose hyperparameters by smallest mean inner pair-held-out absolute `ΔS` error over the three α values, with numeric ties within `1e-12` resolved by **smaller λ, larger kernel bandwidth, fewer expert clusters, then larger L2 penalty**, as applicable, then lexical parameter order. The outer folds estimate performance of this full selection rule. For the final FIT model, rerun the same pair-held-out tuning on all 20 map pairs, then refit once. Aggregate errors and bootstrap uncertainty at the **word-pair** level with 10,000 percentile resamples seeded from the frozen corpus-manifest SHA-256; resample whole pairs with all associated voices/directions/α values. Freeze the resulting map, basis, category-independent calibration, score policy and code before opening development. No model selection or threshold changes based on development or locked test.

## Prospective gates and controls

The FIT advancement gate is pair-held-out and applies **separately at both encoder31 and connector**. Per site it has **80 contexts × three α values = 240 all-planned small-edit measurements**. Derive that site's numerical floor from repeated zero edits on the **16 independent basis pairs**, using `max(1e-4, 10 × maximum absolute zero-repeat score drift)`; freeze it before map-pair CV. Require every planned measurement to have finite unpatched/patched score, connected gradient, nonempty bins and verified effective edit; otherwise the site's primary gate fails, with observed-only diagnostics reported separately. Conditional MAE must be at most **0.85× each of** zero-change, same-rank global, the better same-rank ridge, and the J++-inspired fixed expert comparator. Each ratio also needs a pair-bootstrap 95% upper bound below **1.00**. A zero or undefined comparator MAE makes the ratio undefined and the gate fail. Require at least **192/240** effects above floor and at least **75%** correct signs among them; ties count wrong. Report the exact `matches/determinate/240` counts, all comparator errors, and full-answer validity. **Both sites**, FIT task validity and FIT prefix coverage/purity must pass before development. Rank-16 versus rank-32 captured energy and errors are descriptive, with no best-rank switch.

If FIT passes, run development **once** with no new backward pass. Each site has **96 directions × three α values = 288 all-planned small-edit measurements**; the identical completeness rule applies. The development gate repeats ≤0.85 against **all four** comparators, bootstrap upper bound <1, ≥**231/288** above-floor effects, and ≥75% correct signs among them, and also requires ≥72/96 clean one-word+EOS semantic answers plus the frozen prefix coverage/purity gates. The development-to-locked decision requires **both sites** to pass; a one-site result is reported but does not open test. Failed development remains negative and the fresh locked test stays sealed. After a pass, freeze all artifacts and run the new locked test once under the **identical** 288-per-site and clean/prefix gates, reporting pair-bootstrap confidence intervals and every category/voice/template row without exclusions. Passing predicts a reusable **task-specific reduced-rank local transport** in one model; it does not imply a full Jacobian Lens, general audio understanding, or a security vulnerability.

Freeze these **complete-answer and time controls before development**; run them unchanged on locked test if opened. Use the frozen six-bin row map: the four word bins together define whole-word support, and the other two bins are the preword/postword supports. At each site/direction, require exact `α=0` and passive-hook suffix-token parity, one prompt hook invocation, no decode-step edit, expected source/target shapes, and recorded effective FP32 norm; a failure makes that site's primary gate fail. Generate the complete suffix for clean, self, reversed-donor, whole-word `α=1` uniform edit, and preword/postword/random equal-effective-norm `α=1` controls. Also score **preword/postword/random `α=.05`** edits with effective norms matched to the whole-word `α=.05` edit; do not substitute the large-edit scores. The random direction has a manifest-derived seed. Record all **96 planned directions per site** even when the clean answer is wrong or EOS/surface is malformed. Classify an exact donor answer only if the complete one-word suffix has final EOS and no other special token. Large-edit donor-answer rate is a separate causal-behavior endpoint; label it positive only at **≥72/96** all-planned directions and ≥75% of clean-eligible directions. For an additional **time-preference** label, require whole-word exact-donor count to strictly exceed each control count, each control's exact-donor count ≤9/96, and `|ΔS_word| > |ΔS_control|` at `α=.05` for each equal-norm control in at least **72/96** paired directions, with ≥86/96 finite valid comparisons per control. Ties, invalid edits, malformed answers, or missing outputs cannot be dropped; they fail their planned comparison, and any invalid control withholds the time label. The small-`α` score edits and large-`α` answer edits have different purposes and are never pooled. **The map predicts only the prompt-final state and first-token class score; full generated answers are observed after an actual intervention, never inferred from the map.** A score-gate pass without complete-answer changes is score sensitivity, not behavioral causality. Report the fraction of measured full terminal-change energy captured by rank 32 on FIT, development, and test; a score pass with low capture supports only a task-score predictor. Because post-attention rows can carry information from outside their nominal time, a time label means sensitivity at selected rows, not an isolated phoneme circuit.

## Compute and stop rules

The map fit needs **80 × 32 = 2,560 target VJPs** if both sites share each backward graph, at most 5,120 site-specific VJPs otherwise, plus basis and validation forwards. Benchmark one FIT map context with all 32 cotangents, both hooks, and all score/edit checks before the full fit. Multiply measured per-context wall time by 80 and add measured basis/processor/load overhead. Stop if projected **cumulative wall time across all attempts**, including model loading, failed attempts and resume overhead, exceeds **six hours**, if MPS driver allocation reaches **30 GiB**, or if pinned-source/zero-edit checks fail. Every invocation gets an immutable elapsed-time receipt; completed context checkpoints bind source/input/output hashes and cumulative time. Resume charges the maximum prior cumulative time, and an unreceipted partial attempt or memory log fails closed rather than resetting the budget. Check the deadline between VJPs and cases, and note that an in-flight kernel cannot be interrupted by this guard. Development/test evaluation is forward-only and has a separately frozen bound before either split is opened. Reuse v1 infrastructure only after binding exact source and result hashes; no v2 Qwen model output begins from this design document alone.

## Claim boundary

The lexical task is a positive control for mechanism work. It does not yet test an authorization boundary. If the map transfers, the next separately frozen task should compare audio and typed requests at a consequential model-internal decision (for example, read versus cancel before a stub tool call), trace the first divergent representation, and causally patch that representation. A security claim would require a specified trust boundary, attacker capability, complete action output, and reproducible violation under a realistic harness. A synthetic hidden-state edit is evidence about model causality, not itself an attacker-reachable exploit.

This v2 corpus varies lexical content and two synthetic voices. It **does not** establish robustness to noise, speaking rate, microphone/channel effects, accents, or human voices. A later, separately versioned acoustic-robustness phase should freeze same-transcript paired recordings across fixed-seed 20/10 dB noise, fixed rate changes, and independent human/accent speakers where available. It should test where those changes first alter the audio pathway, whether the learned map remains predictive without refitting, and whether any changed internal feature causally changes a complete answer. Freeze its speakers, transformations, ASR/human intelligibility measures, splits, thresholds, and control edits before inference. Keep that phase out of v2's primary gate and make no acoustic-robustness claim until it runs.
