# Independent review: audio-J-lens test-stimulus freeze

I audited [SPLIT_V1.json](../SPLIT_V1.json), [render.py](render.py), [manifest.json](manifest.json), [BINDING.json](BINDING.json), and the generated WAV files **without running Qwen, ASR, or a Jacobian evaluator**. I did not modify the frozen inputs.

The split separates the red/blue fit control, the cat/dog and Monday/Friday development pairs, and the new north/south and seven/eight test pairs. The test combines two word pairs, two phrasings, and two TTS families: **8 matched pairs and 16 clips**. Its words are disjoint from fit and development. Piper Amy and Kokoro Bella are new relative to the fit control but **reused from development**; this is a lexical and phrasing holdout, not an independent-speaker holdout.

The binding's split and manifest SHA-256 values match the current files. The manifest's split, renderer, held-out TTS plan, and pinned render-helper hashes match. The split's fit-control and development-manifest hashes match, as do the held-out plan's TTS source/dependency hashes. The current frozen files are present in commit `7540662` (2026-11-18 01:04:20 -0500) and match that commit; the split, renderer, manifest, and binding modification times occur in that order. The renderer refuses to overwrite existing outputs. Its direct code and pinned helper load CPU Piper/Kokoro and PCM utilities, with no Qwen/ASR inference or answer-based selection path. These records support a pre-evaluation freeze, although local files and commit history cannot prove that no unrecorded model query occurred outside this workspace.

I checked all **16 raw and 16 processed fragment WAV file hashes**, all **16 final clip file and decoded int16 PCM hashes**, and the manifest's unique pair keys and scripts. Every final WAV is mono, 16 kHz, 16-bit PCM. Independently rebuilding each clip as shared prefix + 1,920-sample silence + right-padded word fragment + 1,920-sample silence + shared suffix gives exactly its saved PCM bytes. Within each matched pair, the clips have equal length and are **sample-for-sample identical outside the recorded word-slot interval**; all eight pairs differ inside the interval. The recorded slots are assembly boundaries, not independently aligned phoneme boundaries.

No discrepancy was found. Before test evaluation, check `BINDING.json` and the clip hashes, and keep all 16 clips in the reported denominator. `SPLIT_V1.json` deliberately leaves `wav_manifest_sha256` null; the separate binding supplies the frozen manifest hash.
