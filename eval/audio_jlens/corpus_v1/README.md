# Prospective corpus for a fitted audio-time transport

**Stimuli only, 2026-11-18. No Qwen or ASR answers were inspected.** Selection was committed as `70ad236` before the CPU TTS render. The [proposal](../NEXT_PHASE_CORPUS_LENS_PROPOSAL.md) is a design, not a frozen model-evaluation protocol.

The [selection](SPEC_V1.json) contains 48 fixed word pairs: 12 each in animal, color, object, and nature. A deterministic seed assigns six pairs per category to the fit pool, three to development, and three to locked test. Within the fit pool, three pairs per category are flagged for the first 48-example map; the other 12 pairs are reserved for fit-only checks. Words are unique across every pair and split. The selection excludes all words used in the earlier red/blue, cat/dog, Monday/Friday, north/south, and seven/eight assays.

Two split-exclusive spoken templates per split, two pinned CPU TTS families (Piper Amy and Kokoro Bella), and both words in every pair produce **192 matched audio pairs / 384 WAVs**. Each word is rendered separately and right-padded to the longer processed word fragment. The exact same prefix, gap, and suffix PCM is then used for both members of a pair. The [rendered manifest](rendered/manifest.json) records every source fragment, clip hash, slot sample interval, TTS version and voice, split, template, and word. The renderer reopens every final 16 kHz PCM16 WAV and verifies all file and PCM hashes, equal length, identical samples outside the word slot, and nonidentical samples inside it.

Selection and CPU rendering are separate. [make_spec.py](make_spec.py) uses the pinned Qwen **tokenizer only** to save exact one-token answer aliases and IDs; it loads no Qwen weights. [render.py](render.py) loads the two pinned TTS engines from the earlier voice plan, refuses to overwrite outputs, and loads no candidate or ASR model. The [binding](rendered/BINDING.json) links selection SHA-256 `4c4cb6beafdd27509a073872f71dea004be686a6d8fafccbae410b8b11d7f265` to manifest SHA-256 `adf8cd02d24a43ef576bcb993dc54bd2527440a62798e12fc788def3c063971f`.

This is synthetic speech with concatenated fragments. The source timing is a constructed word slot, not an independent phoneme alignment. Some aliases have a leading space or different case, and a model may produce a semantically right multi-token spelling; the future scoring protocol must keep the raw surface, frozen first-token alias score, semantic answer, and EOS separate. The two voices were used in earlier experiments, so this corpus tests fresh words and split-exclusive wording, **not held-out speakers**. Independent ASR and human intelligibility checks have not yet been run. The 384 clips have not been used to select or fit a candidate map.
