The selected examples are 12 preselected fit word pairs × two voices × two directions under `fit_chosen`; the runner's corpus preflight opens fit-pool WAVs only. Stage 1 hooks post-encoder31, post-connector, and the post-block35 text residual, saving the **final prompt position** as the terminal target. It forms 24 undirected natural-audio terminal differences and an **uncentered** rank-16 SVD basis, with deterministic signs, a fixed relative rank tolerance, and per-category fit means. No evaluation-split state is used in the basis.

Stage 2 releafs the encoder31 output while preserving its downstream connector and language graph. One grad-enabled forward is retained for 16 target-basis VJPs to both source sites. The connector and encoder gradients are separate derivatives of the same target; they must not be added. Each bin's source vector is the mean of its donor-minus-recipient rows, while each fitted Jacobian row is the **sum** of per-row gradients averaged over 48 contexts. Those quantities match a uniform additive edit of every row in that bin. They do not equal an arbitrary rowwise donor replacement derivative. The six bins use four word-slot quarters plus one preword and one postword quarter, with nominal row-center assignment and exact-boundary ties to the earlier bin. Empty bins abort instead of being silently dropped.

Smoke mode captures all 48 fit states and runs one VJP on the first fit case without saving a fitted map. The full mode writes an atomic stage-1 checkpoint, then atomic accumulator checkpoints after each complete 16-VJP context. On resume, it enforces frozen metadata, basis identifier, exact case-ID prefix, and progress-prefix consistency; the checkpoint is authoritative if the trailing progress line was missed. The 30 GiB MPS process cap is set before loading, and memory is logged after load, each stage-1 case, and each VJP. A grad/no-grad terminal mismatch above `1e-3` aborts.

**Follow-up checks, not smoke blockers:** The six-interval helper does not explicitly check the postword interval against final clip duration; all 24 selected pair/voice groups have at least 1.79 seconds of postword margin in the frozen manifest.
