# Phase 2 proposal: a fitted audio-time transport

**Status: design, 2026-11-18. No new model inference.** This is ready to turn into a versioned protocol and immutable stimulus manifest *before* fitting. It is not a claim that the transport works.

## Question and evidence that motivates it

Can one map fitted on a corpus, with no test-example backward pass, predict how a change in a spoken interval alters a later language-model state and its answer? If so, which part of the signal entered at the audio frontend, encoder, connector, and language stack, and how stable is that account across voices, words, and models?

Our Qwen2.5-Omni-3B FP32 experiment established a narrower prerequisite: a **fresh gradient at each recipient** predicts small edits at the connector and encoder layer 31. The independently audited locked test had **10/16** clean-eligible directions per site (three number pairs yielded digits rather than the frozen word forms), **30/30** correct small-step signs among eligible directions, and **10/10** eligible complete donor-answer flips under full swaps. The all-planned exact-word flip count is **10/16**, not 16/16. The fitted norm coefficient was zero, so the norm comparator duplicated the zero comparator. These are local derivatives, not a reusable lens ([audit](LOCKED_TEST_FP32_RESULT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md)).

Our single-anchor Qwen semantic-bridge readout reached no top-100 bridge hits, and most of its apparent affine gain was explained by a constant anchor. Its source labels were also a poor match to the chat task ([review](../semantic_bridge/FIT_PILOT_REVIEW.md)). The next corpus must ask for and validate the target answer; every affine readout must beat its constant intercept.

Voxtral supplies a separate methodological warning: two clips identical outside a spoken-word PCM interval became different at all 2,689 padded log-mel frames because its frontend uses a clip-wide floor. Two fit-only feature-splice experiments found that complete Red/Blue answers followed the word feature window, while outside/padding swaps moved scores without flipping answers ([Piper](../../models/second_architecture/DECOMPOSITION_FINDINGS_V1.md), [Kokoro](../../models/second_architecture/VOXTRAL_KOKORO_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md)). A hidden-state time heatmap alone cannot attribute spread to the encoder.

## Relation to prior methods

Anthropic's [Jacobian Lens paper](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html) and [released fitter](https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens/blob/581d398613e5602a5af361e1c34d3a92ea82ba8e/jlens/fitting.py) average residual-to-residual Jacobians across a corpus, then use the model's output head. Their estimator uses one forward plus `ceil(d_target / dim_batch)` backwards per prompt. The public [Audio Jacobian Lens repository](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/tree/1784e62e4caaf801bfc8dc1208860703b5f3d074) **already implements** fitted decoder and rectangular encoder-to-decoder lenses for Whisper, and has Chatterbox and LFM pathways. Its [Whisper pilot](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/blob/1784e62e4caaf801bfc8dc1208860703b5f3d074/docs/experiments/WHISPER_TINY_PILOT.md) found the aligned encoder affine readout mostly matched a remote/constant baseline. We should extend and benchmark that code where possible, not claim to invent an audio J-lens.

Audio activation patching and probing are also prior art: [Glazer et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15882) study ASR; [Peng and Nuchged](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19211) separate preserved prosodic information from its use in answers; [Lin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28189) report cross-modal concept neurons and interventions. The potential contribution here is a *reusable, time-binned audio-to-answer transport* with held-out causal calibration, explicit frontend controls, and output-surface accounting in two different audio-language architectures. Whether that combination is new enough for publication requires a fresh literature check after results.

## Estimator and target

Start with the pinned Qwen Thinker, the existing connector and post-block encoder-31 hooks, and the **prompt-last final residual before final norm**. Do not fit on Talker states or claim anything about generated speech. Fix the prompt template and answer task. For each exact-PCM pair, use the frozen **swapped-word sample interval** `[t0,t1)`, never the whole utterance. Divide that interval into four equal-duration word bins. Add one immediately preceding and one immediately following bin, each of duration `(t1−t0)/4`; if either cannot fit within the clip or maps to zero source rows, mark the planned example ineligible rather than moving a boundary after inspection. Map interval centers through the frozen model-specific frontend/time map. Assign each source row to the bin containing its nominal time center, with an exact-boundary tie going to the earlier bin; no row belongs to two bins. Save row counts and any acoustic receptive-field overlap between bins. Post-attention rows may contain information from elsewhere, so a selected row is not a local acoustic feature.

For site `s` and bin `b`, let `H_{s,t}` be its row activations and `z_{s,b}=mean_{t in b} H_{s,t}`. Fit one **corpus-averaged rectangular operator** per site/bin:

`J_{s,b} = mean_i [sum_{t in b_i} ∂h_T(i)/∂H_{s,t}(i)]`.

This is the derivative for a *uniform additive edit to all rows in the bin*. It is not the exact derivative of an arbitrary rowwise donor patch. For two clips with matched prompt and row layout, let `δ_b=z_b(donor)−z_b(recipient)` and apply the **six-bin uniform state edit** `H_{t in b}←H_t+αδ_b` for every bin. Predict its terminal-state change by `Δĥ_T=αΣ_b J_{s,b}δ_b`. The predicted patched state is **anchored to the actual unedited recipient**: `ĥ_T(α)=h_T(recipient)+U Uᵀ Δĥ_T`, where `U Uᵀ` is omitted for a future full-width map. No test gradient or donor terminal state enters this prediction. Apply the model's final norm and head to this predicted state. The **primary measured endpoint** is the same six-bin uniform intervention at the same source hook, same `α`, and same recipient; its unpatched reference is obtained in the same no-grad execution mode. A donor-derived `δ_b` chooses the edit direction but does not turn this into a full donor-row swap. At small `α`, compare the predicted score change with the measured score change; at `α=1`, report curvature without assuming linearity.

For an **absolute** readout, save fit-only source and terminal means `μ_{s,b,c}` and `μ_{T,c}` within each known answer category `c`, and assess `ĥ_T=μ_{T,c}+U UᵀΣ_b J_{s,b}(z_b−μ_{s,b,c})`, decoded through the actual final norm/head. This is a corpus-centered extrapolation, not a prompt-specific identity: acoustics and wording absent from `z_b` remain in its error. Compare it against the *same category's* constant terminal anchor `μ_{T,c}`. The paired, recipient-anchored difference is primary; the absolute readout is a secondary test of how useful the lens is without a recipient terminal state. Keep connector and encoder-31 maps separate; never add them, since connector states already descend from encoder states.

For a **rowwise full donor patch**, the local first-order expression is `Σ_t (∂h_T/∂H_t)·(H_t(donor)−H_t(recipient))`; in general this is **not** `J_bδ_b`. Save the within-bin residual `H_t(donor)−H_t(recipient)−δ_b` and its norm. The full donor patch and the natural waveform-pair difference are distinct, secondary outcomes; compare them with the uniform-edit prediction as an explicitly marked approximation, never as a calibration success by definition.

For a lower-cost first pass, choose a fixed rank-16 target basis `U` from the fit-only SVD of the 24 undirected donor-minus-recipient final-residual differences (12 pairs × two voices, including clean-answer failures), and fit `UᵀJ` by 16 VJPs per fit prompt. If those 24 vectors have numerical rank below 16 under a fit-only tolerance frozen in the protocol, stop this version rather than changing rank after development. Save the basis and singular values. This is a *reduced-rank transport*, not a full-vocabulary Jacobian Lens; decode with the lifted `U(UᵀΔĥ_T)` and report how much held-out terminal-state difference lies outside `U`. Do not imply that a missed label is absent from the model. A full-width map is a second stage only after a measured cost and positive held-out reduced-rank signal. The existing one-prompt full-width Qwen fit cost 256 backward batches and 147.8 seconds at the **LM** source; an encoder-source fit may be slower, so benchmark one fit-only prompt first and cap both time and 30 GiB MPS driver allocation.

The first-token residual readout does **not** by itself predict a complete reply or EOS. For each frozen answer pair, precompute **unique first-token ID sets** `A` and `B` from all predeclared surfaces (leading-space/case/digit variants where valid), require `A∩B=∅`, and store the exact tokenizer revision and IDs before fitting. The one scalar for prediction and MAE is donor-oriented **first-token log odds** `S(h)=logsumexp(logits(h)[donor aliases])−logsumexp(logits(h)[recipient aliases])`. The vocabulary softmax denominator cancels algebraically; normalized full-vocabulary alias masses may be reported descriptively. Predict `ΔS_pred(α)=S(ĥ_T(α))−S(h_T(recipient))`; measure `ΔS_actual(α)=S(h_T^{uniform-edit}(α))−S(h_T(recipient))`. Both use the same final norm/head, no-grad reference, alias IDs, and `α`. Absolute-readout scoring orients log odds toward the frozen expected answer versus its paired foil. Full-vocabulary ranks are descriptive only. Measure full generated answer plus EOS as an independent causal endpoint. A later two-position transport can address complete-answer likelihood if the first stage works.

## Corpus and freeze order

Create a new corpus; the opened red/blue, cat/dog, Monday/Friday, north/south, and seven/eight material is only a plumbing and historical baseline, never the new locked test. Preselect 48 balanced lexical pairs (12 in each of four answer categories), two independent TTS families, and six question templates (two assigned exclusively to each split). Assign whole **word pairs** to a 24-pair fit pool, 12-pair development, and 12-pair locked test, balanced 6/3/3 within each category, with a published seed and hashes. Within the fit pool, preselect **12 map-fitting pairs**, three per category; reserve its other 12 pairs for fit-only task validity, rank coverage, and convergence diagnostics, without adding them to the saved map. The map fits exactly **48 recipient examples**: 12 pairs × two voices × two directions × the first fit template. Rank 16 therefore requires **768 target-basis VJPs** if both source sites are recorded in one backward graph, or at most 1,536 if sites must be run separately, plus 48 fitting forwards and fit-only basis-collection forwards. The second fit template and the other 12 fit-pool pairs cannot be added to this version's map after development is viewed. Development and test each have **96 planned directional examples**: 12 pairs × two voices × two directions × two split-specific templates. Hold out one additional voice or human recording set as a separate speaker-transfer challenge if acquisition is possible; do not relabel recurring Piper/Kokoro voices as speaker holdout. Keep both patch directions and every clean failure in the all-planned denominator. A small separately balanced prosody or speaker-attribute corpus, with identical transcripts across each pair and validated audio cues, follows the lexical positive control; it is a distinct task, not pooled into lexical accuracy.

The lexical pairs are a **positive control**, not a full test of the user's audio hypothesis. Before fitting, also freeze a same-transcript acoustic challenge on the new development/test words: clean speech, a second independent voice, fixed-seed additive noise at 20 dB SNR, and fixed-seed additive noise at 10 dB SNR, all with identical text and prompt. Record the independent ASR transcript and human intelligibility for each; failures remain visible. Evaluate whether the learned map predicts the *natural waveform-induced* terminal-state and answer-score differences from these conditions, separately from the uniform state-edit endpoint. Inspect how each perturbation changes raw waveform, raw features, normalized features, encoder rows, and connector rows, including outside the nominal spoken interval. These are measured propagation profiles, not proof that the model uses every changed coordinate. A second architecture needs its own frozen frontend controls.

Before model fitting, freeze: exact WAV and processor-feature hashes; sample intervals and row maps; processor version; Qwen/Voxtral revisions; prompts; split; candidate answer surfaces; scripts; alpha values; source hooks; fit rank; metrics; baselines; exclusions; and resource caps. Validate PCM equality outside the intended word interval, independent ASR transcripts, and a small blinded human listening check. For Voxtral, additionally compute raw and normalized feature differences and a shared-max frontend control before interpreting any off-word source bin. Do **not** use ASR alignment from the target model as both the fitting and evaluation location.

The existing number failure requires a prespecified output policy. For each category, list semantically equivalent short answer surfaces (for example, `Seven`, `seven`, `7`) **before** any model result. Report the raw generated bytes/tokens and EOS, strict exact-surface accuracy, and separately normalized semantic accuracy. Candidate first-token alias IDs are deduplicated; never choose the best alias after seeing a response. If the output is an explanation, extra words, or lacks EOS, record it rather than silently treating it as a one-word answer. The primary denominator remains all planned directions; a clean-eligible subset is supplementary.

## Baselines, causal tests, and decision rules

Compare the fitted operator with: zero change; affine target mean alone; a global/non-time-binned operator of equal rank; shuffled time-bin labels; a fit-only linear ridge map on the same pooled states; a transcript-only route using the *same model and prompt* on the exact script and on an independent ASR transcript; and the per-recipient VJP as an **oracle diagnostic**, never as the reusable baseline. Fit all learned comparators on the same 48 examples and select ridge regularization using fit-pool data only. Normalize input bin vectors using fit-only statistics; report the six-bin map's greater parameter capacity relative to the global map. Compare each method under the same **six-bin uniform edit** and donor-oriented `ΔS` where it is applicable; typed/transcript routes are behavioral controls, not comparable state-edit operators. Hold model output, prompt, and scoring constant across routes. Report full-vocabulary rank descriptively and predicted-versus-measured small-alpha `ΔS` as the primary numerical comparison. Weight the 48 fitting examples so each of 12 lexical pairs has equal total weight; average and bootstrap development/test errors at the pair level, with per-category rows.

At development and locked test, evaluate each fixed map without any new backward pass. Compute prediction for donor-minus-recipient *mean-bin uniform edits* and measure the same bin-uniform intervention at `α={0,.01,.025,.05,1}`. Separately run full rowwise donor replacement at `α=1` to compare with the earlier assay; do not equate that nonlinear full swap to a first-order prediction. Measure the **natural audio-pair change** with unpatched model forwards and compare its terminal-state difference and candidate score difference with the map's `Σ_b J_bΔz_b` only as an approximation; record its error apart from the exact-intervention error. Do this for both word changes and same-transcript voice/noise changes. For interventions use clean, self, reverse, equal-norm wrong-time, random, and bin-shuffled controls; report hook count, source/donor shapes, complete answer, EOS, and score. A norm-matched offword control alone is insufficient because a post-attention row may carry word information. At Voxtral also run the processor-level word/outside/padding feature crosses on new fit items and keep them separate from hidden-state causal claims.

**Proposed preregistered gate, to commit before the first new model run:** the primary unit is **all 96 planned directions per split**, even when the unpatched answer is wrong. `ΔS` MAE includes every direction and fixed small-alpha condition; sign agreement excludes only effects below the fit-derived repeated-zero numerical floor, and reports that exclusion count. Empty source bins, disconnected gradients, and missing patched scores remain in the 96-planned denominator and fail the transport gate rather than disappearing from the metric. Require (i) at least 75% correct sign among determinate **all-planned** effects; (ii) all-planned `ΔS` MAE at most 0.75 times both zero and global-operator baselines; and (iii) the absolute affine **expected-answer log odds against its paired foil** exceeds the same-category constant anchor's log odds on at least 70% of **all-planned** directions; an exact tie is not an improvement. Also require at least 72/96 unpatched semantically correct, one-short-answer-plus-EOS outputs as a *task-validity gate*, using the frozen alias policy. Report every metric again on clean-eligible directions and report full-answer flips with both 96-planned and eligible denominators, but a conditional pass cannot override an all-planned or validity failure. Separately label *time-selective* only if the four word bins' combined, norm-matched intervention has a larger absolute `ΔS` than **each** preword, postword, and bin-shuffled norm-matched control in at least 75% of clean-eligible directions and fewer than 10% of those controls produce an exact donor answer. The natural-waveform approximation and voice/noise invariance have separate descriptive metrics and cannot be counted toward the uniform-edit gate. These thresholds are **proposal values**, not yet frozen or achieved. Report uncertainty by bootstrapping at the word-pair level, not by counting voice/direction repeats as independent examples. A failed gate remains a useful result about where averaging or time pooling breaks.

## Bounded security continuation

Only after the reusable transport and time controls transfer, freeze a separate authorization task with inert `list` and `cancel` stubs or a spoken `approve`/`reject` decision. The independent variable should be attacker-controlled **audio** (voice, noise, prosody, or an acoustic perturbation) with the request transcript held fixed or independently checked. Ask where the decision diverges, whether a fitted bin/direction predicts the divergence on fresh cases, and whether a causal intervention changes the **complete emitted action**. Compare typed, transcript, and audio routes with identical policy and tool schema. A security finding needs a stated trust boundary, attacker capability, violated property, repeat rate, and full action consequence; a logit shift or readable concept is not sufficient.

## Stop/scale decision

1. Freeze the manifest and reduced-rank protocol; benchmark one of the 48 map-fitting examples with both sites, 16 VJPs, and one source/target capture. Project the measured time to 48 examples (or to 96 site passes if simultaneous gradients fail). Stop if memory exceeds 30 GiB or that projected fit exceeds a six-hour model-work cap. Save the estimate and actual device-memory samples. Use no locked-test model output to adjust it.
2. Fit the 16-direction reduced-rank maps on fit only; inspect development once. If they fail against constant/global/ridge baselines, diagnose alignment, output task, processor spread, and rank coverage on fit/development only, and publish the negative result. Do not spend on a full-width map or open locked test for that version.
3. If development passes, freeze code/artifacts and run the new locked test once. A passing result supports a **reusable task-specific audio transport** in Qwen. Then port the measurement grammar, not the weights or layer numbers, to Voxtral and a distinct acoustic task. The earlier Whisper negative encoder lens is a required comparison, not an inconvenient exception.
