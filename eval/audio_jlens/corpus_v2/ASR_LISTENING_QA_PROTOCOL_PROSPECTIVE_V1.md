# Prospective Qwen v2 stimulus intelligibility gate

**Designed before any v2 ASR or human listening result was opened.** The rendered corpus has a [technical binding](rendered_v2/TECHNICAL_BINDING.json), but that receipt verifies bytes and assembly, not what anyone hears. This plan covers all **480** primary WAVs: 60 pairs × two words × two voices × two split-specific phrasings. It permits no replacement from the 12 inactive reserve pairs, no selection on Qwen answers, and no Qwen model inference until a separate quality receipt is reviewed and frozen. The existing Qwen v1 development failure and locked-test firewall are unchanged.

## Fixed input and blind queue

`make_qa_queue_v1.py` reads **only** the v2 `manifest.json`, `TECHNICAL_BINDING.json`, and candidate metadata. It checks their pinned hashes, 240 unique matched records, 480 unique clip paths/hashes, the 36/12/12 whole-pair split, and no reserve use. It writes an internal case map containing the intended script, middle word, paired alternative, source hash, split, voice, and template, plus a separate listener queue containing only opaque IDs in deterministic shuffled order. The queue has no words, filenames, categories, pairs, or voice labels. An audio-serving interface must resolve IDs on the server and expose only the audio bytes and an ID to listeners; it must not send the internal case map or reveal the filename in an HTML URL. The queue cannot be edited in response to ASR or listening outcomes. It includes locked-test audio for input-quality review, but never candidate-model answers or activations.

The original middle-word slot is fixed by the TTS assembly metadata. The listener is asked to transcribe the **one word between the sentence prefix and final question/request** and to mark whether it is clear. The interface records an initial free-text answer before showing any reference. It records reviewer ID, clip ID, play count, clear/unclear flag, confidence 1–5, timestamp, and optional note. Require exactly one initial response for every ID; later corrections and adjudications are append-only rows that preserve the first response. A reviewer must not have seen the internal case map or model output. Log the queue, interface source, browser/server version, response file, and WAV hashes. A manual listening claim requires a real human response for every clip; an ASR output or this agent's metadata inspection cannot substitute for it.

## Two independent ASR screens

Run each recognizer on all **480** frozen WAVs, audio only, with no transcript prompt, target word list, candidate words, or Qwen output. Prespecify and hash executable source, installed packages, exact model revision and weight files, processor/tokenizer files, CPU-only execution, four threads, one clip per inference, decoding settings, and a fail-closed 480-row output. Save raw transcripts before comparison with references. The first recognizer is the locally pinned `openai/whisper-tiny.en` revision `87c7102498dcde7456f24cfd30239ca606ed9063`, using the existing [v1 audit method](../corpus_v1/asr_audit.PROTOCOL.md). The second proposed recognizer is `facebook/wav2vec2-base-960h`, a CTC ASR model trained for 16 kHz English; acquire and pin an exact revision and local weight SHA **before** its first v2 inference. The two have different decoding architectures and training paths, though neither is a human ear and their errors may still correlate. If the second checkpoint or a reproducible CPU environment cannot be pinned, stop before Qwen inference and version this protocol; do not quietly fall back to a second Whisper checkpoint. [Whisper model card](https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-tiny.en), [Wav2Vec2 model card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/wav2vec2-base-960h), [fairseq Wav2Vec2 usage](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairseq/blob/main/examples/wav2vec/README.md).

For each model and clip, retain the full raw transcript and a normalized token sequence. Normalize **after** decoding by Unicode NFKC, casefold, and extraction of Unicode letter/number words with internal apostrophes; ignore punctuation and whitespace differences. Do not map synonyms, phonetic guesses, homophones, or numeric forms to the intended word. Report literal exact script match, normalized full-script exact match, word error count/rate, intended middle word occurring exactly once as a whole token, paired alternative occurrence, empty/truncated output, and model disagreement. Aggregate over all 480, each split, category, voice, template, and pair without dropping difficult clips. Both recognizers must have 480 raw outcomes and verified WAV/PCM hashes for their output to be a valid screen. ASR disagreement is a **listening flag**, not proof of unintelligibility; ASR agreement is not proof of human perception. The v1 audit found both plausible phonetic substitutions and omissions, which motivates keeping this distinction [v1 independent review](../corpus_v1/asr_audit.REVIEW.md).

## Human listening and advancement rule

One blinded human reviews **every one of the 480 clips** in queue order. A second blinded human independently reviews every clip for which either ASR fails whole-word identity, the two ASRs disagree, the first listener marks unclear or low confidence (1–2), or the first listener's normalized middle word differs from the target. If the first two listeners disagree on the middle word or clarity, a third blinded human reviews that clip. Do not show any listener the reference before their answer is sealed. The human review records all misses and disagreements, including locked-test input-quality misses, without selecting clips out of the corpus.

Define a clip as **quality-confirmed** only if the first listener's normalized middle-word answer equals the intended word and it is marked clear, or, after a flagged review, at least two independent listeners give that exact word and mark it clear. A paired-alternative answer, a remaining disagreement, missing response, or an unclear majority is unresolved. A reviewer can correct a typographical mistake only in an append-only correction row with a reason; the original blinded answer and all aggregate counts stay visible. ASR errors can remain flagged if the human rule confirms the word. This rule is strict because the later experiment interprets word-level model effects. It does not claim that every person would hear the same thing.

The pre-inference **go** gate requires: the unchanged technical binding; both complete ASR passes with pinned sources and 480 saved transcripts each; all 480 initial blinded human responses; completed second/third reviews for all flags; and **480/480 quality-confirmed clips**. Any unresolved clip stops Qwen v2 inference on this version. Report the full failed case list and keep the 60-pair population intact. An alternative corpus requires a new prospective selection/render/QA version and its own frozen split and audit; the current reserves never activate automatically. If the gate passes, a separately frozen Qwen v2 fitting/evaluation protocol may use the corpus; this quality receipt by itself does not authorize opening locked-test Qwen answers.

## Receipt and limits

An independent CPU auditor must recompute all 480 case identities, source hashes, raw transcript counts, normalization and WER, listener coverage, blind-order provenance, flag assignment, adjudication, and the go/stop rule from immutable ASR/listening files. Commit the ASR runners, listening interface, and auditor before outcomes. Publish a SHA-bound quality receipt with individual and aggregate failures, including all original responses; do not report a pass from a subset. Human listening and ASR are input-quality checks. They do not establish a Qwen mechanism, transfer, or security property.
