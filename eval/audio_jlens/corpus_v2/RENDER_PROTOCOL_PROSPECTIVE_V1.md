# Prospective Qwen audio corpus v2 CPU render and audit

**Not frozen; no v2 WAV or model output is authorized by this document.** Before rendering, an independent reviewer must verify the renderer and auditor, then create `RENDER_FREEZE_V1.json` with the exact SHA-256 values of this protocol, `CANDIDATES_UNFROZEN.json`, `CANDIDATE_VALIDATION_UNFROZEN.json`, `render_v2.py`, and `audit_wavs_v2.py`. The renderer refuses `--render` unless that receipt exists and matches. A review may require a revised protocol/code first. No reserve pair can substitute for a failed primary pair in this version.

## Fixed workload

Use the 60 primary pairs and deterministic 36/12/12 split in the candidate validator report; all 12 reserves remain inactive. For each pair, render both words with Piper Amy and Kokoro Bella under both templates belonging to its split. This makes **240 matched pair/voice/template records and 480 WAV files**. No ASR, Qwen, Voxtral, or answer model is loaded by the renderer or technical auditor. The renderer uses the exact pinned v1 TTS models, packages, preprocessing, fragment rendering, and assembly helper; it does not download assets.

Render prefix and suffix once per voice/template and each word once per voice/pair. Assemble with the pinned 1,920-sample gaps and right-padded word slot in mono 16 kHz PCM16. For each matched pair, both WAVs must have equal length, identical int16 PCM outside the half-open word slot, and distinct PCM within it. Include the exact primary population, split, FIT role, template, voice, word slot, individual fragment hashes, WAV and PCM hashes, frame count, sample rate, subtype, and SHA-256 provenance in the manifest. Never change a pair or template after inspecting audio or model output. Any render failure leaves no completed binding and stops the corpus.

`render_v2.py --validate-only` checks the candidate/report and local pinned TTS inputs without loading TTS models or writing audio. `--render` requires the frozen receipt and creates a fresh output directory exclusively. It writes `manifest.json` only after all 240 matched records exist. No partial directory is a valid corpus.

Run `audit_wavs_v2.py --validate-only --output-dir ...` after rendering. This independent, CPU-only readback checks the exact 240-record/480-WAV population, all manifest/source/fragment/output hashes, WAV structure, nonempty and finite nonzero amplitude, equal pair length, and exact paired outside-slot identity. It fails on missing or extra clips. A successful audit creates `TECHNICAL_BINDING.json` exclusively. It does not prove intelligibility. Save an independent ASR transcript and listening review for every WAV, with failures retained and reported, before any model inference; a later frozen evaluation protocol must define how those QA results affect proceeding. The technical receipt alone must never be treated as model-inference authorization.

The locked-test **WAVs** are rendered and audited with all primary words; their answer/activation/score data must remain sealed until a separately frozen locked-test evaluation. This corpus procedure may read prior locked-test **word metadata** for vocabulary exclusion, never prior locked-test WAVs or model output.
