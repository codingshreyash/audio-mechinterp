## Scoring and intervention checks

- The pinned `Qwen2_5OmniThinkerForConditionalGeneration.forward` returns `lm_head` logits for the full sequence. For a two-token `[word, EOS]` continuation, the code appends only the word to `input_ids` and `attention_mask`. It reads logits at `prompt_len−1` for the word and at `prompt_len` for EOS, summing their float32 log probabilities. This is the causal teacher-forced indexing. The pinned processor batch has `input_ids`, `attention_mask`, `input_features`, and `feature_attention_mask`; extending the first two while retaining the audio tensors is consistent with the pinned multimodal forward, which recomputes audio placement and position IDs from the extended inputs.
- The source module hook replaces its two-dimensional output with `detach().requires_grad_(True)`. This cuts upstream gradients while preserving downstream derivatives from the selected audio state. Separate donor and recipient complete-answer scores yield `grad_donor−grad_recipient`, the gradient of the donor-oriented score contrast. The candidate-specific source states are required to match exactly. The local prediction uses that gradient dotted with the **effective BF16-rounded edit** at selected rows, retaining the measured baseline intercept. The patched score reruns both candidate continuations at the same site and rows. The CPU toy tests verify orientation, both scoring positions, EOS contribution, small-edit agreement, zero edit, and hook cleanup.
- The pinned audio encoder layer-15 and connector `proj` outputs are flat `[rows, width]` tensors; the former is before pooling, the latter after pooling/projection. The source-matched time map gives different row sets: on the fit inputs, Piper has 27 encoder and 13 connector nominal word rows; Kokoro has 78 and 40. Both word windows remain within one encoder attention chunk, as required by the current selectors. The target is a scalar **complete-answer score contrast**, not a target token row or a residual-to-residual transport. Per-row terms measure sensitivity at a mixed internal state, not acoustic-frame attribution.
- The script records alpha fractions `0, .05, .10, .25, 1`, effective edit norm, predicted/measured score change, per-row contributions, and full greedy answer. Alpha zero checks exact zero edit, near-zero measured score change, and unchanged token IDs. The 30 GiB guard logs measured MPS allocation after model load, each VJP, cached prefill, and each alpha, and aborts after recording an over-limit value. Its CPU mock test verifies record-before-abort. A guard checked between operations cannot prevent a single operation from exhausting memory.

## Limits before interpreting a model run

This is the plan's **first fit-only plumbing gate**, using the original red/blue pair. The previously saved fit pilot reported complete donor flips at encoder layer 15 and the connector for both voices/directions, but this script must still reproduce its own full-swap outcome. It implements the alpha ladder and zero/self check; it does **not yet** implement the plan's wrong-time, random, row-shuffle, scaled wrong-time controls, encoder layer 31, fit-only norm-baseline calibration, numerical-noise floor, or development/test thresholds. Do not treat its output alone as a passed locality or locked-test gate.

BF16 gradients are accumulated through a BF16 source leaf, then converted to float for the dot product. Recording the effective BF16 edit addresses edit quantization but does not guarantee accurate derivatives; the planned small-alpha measured-versus-predicted comparison is essential. Donor source states come from a separate uncached forward. The script compares cached and uncached source states with a 0.03125 maximum-absolute tolerance and checks unchanged clean answer at alpha zero; it does not directly prove equality between standalone cached forward and `generate` prefill at every layer. Audio row centers are nominal splice-time mappings, and attention can mix information across the whole chunk. Any observed score change without a complete greedy answer change remains score-level evidence.
