# Conceptual review before development transport evaluation

This reviews [the phase-2 proposal](NEXT_PHASE_CORPUS_LENS_PROPOSAL.md) against the saved fit-only corpus and scoring results. No new model inference or development/test answer inspection was done.

## Resolve before opening development

1. **Freeze one prompt-last score policy.** Promote the disclosed, fit-informed V2 expanded-prefix expected-versus-foil log odds to the new versioned primary `ΔS` if the goal is a score aligned with observed first-token segmentations. Keep V1 single-token log odds as an exact replication measure and V2 canonical complete-sequence-plus-EOS as a separate later-decoder behavior measure. V1's natural first-token aliases covered only 21/44 correct map-fit generations; V2 covered 44/44, but a prefix such as `L` can start a different word. State that a successful V2 transport predicts **prefix score**, not a complete lexical decision. Freeze and report held-out prefix purity, the top token's expected/foil/other class, complete answer plus EOS, and exact donor-answer flips. High absolute prefix mass alone cannot resolve prefix ambiguity. The four wrong map-fit generations already had positive V2 expected-versus-paired-foil scores.
2. **Make the time map executable and site-specific.** Commit the exact waveform/processor-frame/encoder-row/connector-row center formula, boundary tie, row-count convention, and zero-row rule before development. A connector row and post-attention encoder row can carry information from outside its nominal time bin. Treat source-bin labels as nominal locations, and report receptive-field overlap and processor feature changes outside the waveform word slot.
3. **Define fair comparator inputs and targets.** The six-bin derivative predicts the six-bin **uniform additive edit**. A global baseline needs a frozen row-count-weighted pooling rule for six donor-minus-recipient bin deltas; a ridge baseline must train to the same uniform-edit terminal change or score change at the same `α` and on the same 48 fit examples. A ridge map trained on natural donor terminal differences and evaluated against uniform edits would answer a different question. Keep typed/transcript controls behavioral rather than treating them as state-edit baselines. State explicitly that the constant target anchor is an absolute-readout comparator; it collapses to the zero-change comparator for a recipient-anchored delta.
4. **Close gate edge cases.** Specify a minimum number or fraction of determinate small-alpha signs in each 96-case split, how effects below the fit-derived numerical floor enter the all-planned pass decision, and what happens if the zero/global MAE denominator is zero. Missing rows or patched scores must count as failures, not disappear. The current “75% of determinate” wording could otherwise pass on very few determinate cases. If complete donor-answer flips have no minimum gate, label a passing result as score transport only when behavior does not change.

## Fit-only diagnostics and claim limits

- A rank-16 basis from **24 undirected, fit-only** donor-minus-recipient terminal differences does not leak development/test information. Freeze raw versus centered SVD, pair/voice weights, sign convention, and numerical-rank tolerance. Report the basis' projection capture for the **uniform-edit** terminal deltas on fit, since a basis chosen from natural donor differences may miss the intervention being predicted. Do not change rank after development.
- The proposed `J_b = mean_i Σ_{t∈b_i} ∂h_T/∂H_t` is the correct local derivative for adding one shared vector to every row in bin `b`. With `δ_b` equal to donor-minus-recipient **bin means**, the predicted `Σ_b J_b δ_b` and measured six-bin uniform additive intervention are matched. A rowwise donor swap has a different derivative and should remain a secondary approximation. Use the same recipient anchor, FP32/no-grad reference mode, source hook, and `α` in prediction and measurement.
- The 48 fitting directions are repeated observations from 12 lexical pairs; pair-level weighting and pair-level uncertainty are appropriate. Report all 96 planned development/test directions, as well as clean-eligible subsets, with every absence explicit. A learned prefix-score effect, even with a clean causal state intervention, is not by itself a security vulnerability or a complete action change.
