# Design review: proposed corpus-fitted audio transport

Reviewed 2026-11-18 while `NEXT_PHASE_CORPUS_LENS_PROPOSAL.md` was **unfrozen** (reviewed SHA-256 `893e1881cdeb71904facada48384734992866f39b9b63f26e63a4ef6375252f0`). No model inference was run. These are protocol fixes to make before the next corpus or model evaluation.

## Fix before freezing

1. **Make the source bins and tested edit identical.** The proposal divides the whole spoken-content interval into four bins, then refers to a singular “word bin.” Define the six intervals from the exact swapped-word slot, state whether the word spans one or several bins, and freeze the row-overlap/tie policy at encoder and connector. Record row counts and zero-row cases. With `z_b=mean_t H_t`, the proposed `J_b=mean_i sum_t ∂h_T/∂H_t` predicts a **uniform additive edit** `H_t←H_t+α(z_b^donor−z_b^recipient)` at every selected recipient row. Its target should be the terminal-state and score change measured under precisely that patch. Natural donor-minus-recipient rowwise replacement and an unpatched donor-versus-recipient terminal difference are separate endpoints; the same `J_b Δz_b` does not generally predict either. Variable row counts across examples also change the summed derivative's scale, so predefine weighting/strata and log effective edit norms.
2. **Specify the scalar readout and the anchor.** Freeze one primary first-token score, for example `S(h)=log Σ_{v∈donor aliases} P(v|h) − log Σ_{v∈recipient aliases} P(v|h)` after the *actual* final norm and head, with duplicate first-token IDs removed. State whether the paired prediction is scored by adding the lifted rank-16 `Δĥ_T` to the **measured recipient** terminal state (an oracle anchor) or by subtracting two absolute affine predictions; report both separately if both are useful. Define the actual patched-model `ΔS` on the same candidate alias sets. “Rank/mass better” and “score effect” currently leave multiple possible gates. Full generated answer plus EOS remains a separate endpoint: a first-token score cannot establish complete-answer likelihood.
3. **Freeze what the gate counts.** Say explicitly whether sign and MAE thresholds must pass on the all-planned set, the clean-eligible set, or both, and how non-EOS, digit aliases, empty bins, and missing gradient cases enter each denominator. Define the numerical floor from fit-only repeats and a single primary affine metric (rank *or* mass), tie rule, and pairwise comparison to the constant anchor. With aliases such as `Seven`/`7`, semantic normalization and strict surface scoring must be separate and frozen before any new outputs.

## Strengthen the comparisons and cost plan

- A six-bin family of maps has more parameters than one global map of the same rank. The global and ridge baselines need comparable fit examples, explicit input normalization, regularization chosen only on fit, and a stated parameter/capacity difference. Score each with the **same intervention and terminal readout**. Time selectivity needs effective edit-norm matching for pre/post/bin-shuffled controls, not equal duration alone; post-attention rows can carry word information outside their nominal time bin.
- Freeze how fit examples are weighted. Both directions, two voices, and two templates from one lexical pair are correlated; average and bootstrap at the **pair** level. With 12 development and 12 test pairs (three per category), report per-category results and broad uncertainty. Split-exclusive templates test joint word/template generalization but confound their contributions; add a separate crossed diagnostic if you want to say which transferred. A 70% clean-pair threshold is especially coarse at this size.
- Budget for actual prompts, not “24 pairs”: 24 fit pairs × two voices × two fit templates × two word clips is up to **192 forward contexts**, each needing 16 target-basis VJPs per source-site measurement if reuse works. Benchmark one representative **encoder** and connector prompt with all six bins and both source sites, including retained-graph memory and gradient extraction; then extrapolate and enforce the six-hour cap. Fit-only SVD, target means, regularization, and any model-selection step must stay on fit (or be explicitly allocated to development), never the locked test.

## Position the claim

The proposal correctly distinguishes a task-specific, rank-16 audio transport from Anthropic's corpus-averaged, full-width text J-lens, which averages over prompts and token positions and uses the model's unembedding for vocabulary readout ([Anthropic method](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html)). Prior [Audio Jacobian Lens work](https://github.com/kennethli319/audio-jacobian-lens/tree/1784e62e4caaf801bfc8dc1208860703b5f3d074) already implements fitted audio-to-decoder maps; the contribution must be framed as a validated task/time/causal extension if results support it. Keep the security continuation as a separate, predeclared action-level experiment. A better rank or a score shift alone is neither an interpreted circuit nor a security violation.
