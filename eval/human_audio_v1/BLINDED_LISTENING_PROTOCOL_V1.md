# Blinded human listening, version 1

19 November 2026. The four-speaker source render has an independent technical PASS, but no human intelligibility judgment. This bundle asks two independent English-fluent listeners to transcribe **all 16** final WAVs without seeing the source transcript, perturbation condition, ASR proxy output, target-model output, or each other's answers.

Builder V1 copies the exact SHA-bound WAV bytes into separate reviewer A and B directories under opaque names and different fixed permutations. Each reviewer opens only their own `REVIEW.html`, listens, writes the words heard, marks `clear` or `unclear`, reports whether any added sound is audible after the words (`yes`, `no`, `unsure`), and exports one complete locked JSON. The local form stores each first submission and does not display later clips' metadata. The technical case map stays outside reviewer directories. Do not invent responses from manifest, ASR, or browser automation.

The intake must reject a partial queue, altered WAV hash, duplicate reviewer identity, invalid schema, missing transcript/clarity answer, or out-of-order record. The first two independent transcripts are compared only after both exports exist. A disagreement in normalized words, clarity, or added-sound judgment sends that clip to a third English-fluent listener in a new opaque queue; third reviewer sees neither prior answer nor reference. If both first reviewers agree on the wrong words, the clip fails the reference gate; a third reviewer is not a way to turn it into a pass. A `clear` report and normalized reference match from both final human judgments are required for a perception-invariant zero/noise pair. Audible-tail reports are preserved rather than silently excluded.

The listening form and mapping are source-only assets. No human review is complete until actual exported JSONs have been independently validated. ASR agreement alone is insufficient. Sealed-test speaker **target-model outputs** remain unopened until the development method and all listening/ASR gates are frozen.
